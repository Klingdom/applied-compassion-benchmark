## Key Findings

- Why it matters: this is the first time Belden has been individually assessed. The published 35.9 is a seed value shared by 115 Fortune 500 companies. The measured 36.9 is one point above it, in the same Developing band.
- Why it matters: Belden does what most mid-cap companies do not. It set 12 specific sustainability goals in 2021 and reported progress against all of them in its 2025 report, including a 25% cut in Scope 1 and 2 emissions against a fiscal 2019 baseline.
- Why it matters: employees rate it well, and someone outside checked. Belden earned Great Place to Work certification in 20 countries in 2025.
- Why it matters: the weak spot is who gets prioritised. Equity measures 2.2 out of 5 because coverage is not measured by population and no access barrier mapping was located.
