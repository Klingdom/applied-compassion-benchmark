## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Apple is trying to settle rather than fight, and that is not the same as fixing anything yet. Bloomberg reported on 17 July 2026 that Apple and the US Department of Justice are in early talks to settle the 2024 antitrust lawsuit over the iPhone.
- Apple has made multiple settlement offers to the Department this year. No trial date has been scheduled, and both sides remain cautious; settlement negotiations can end without a deal.
- The underlying allegations describe compassion-relevant conduct. The Department alleged Apple harmed competitors, software developers and consumers by blocking super apps, discouraging competing messaging platforms, limiting cloud game-streaming and restricting rival digital wallets and smartwatches.
- Under the methodology, correcting course under external pressure is anchor-2 to anchor-3 behaviour on AB2. It is already what the published Accountability score of raw 3.0 reflects, so it does not raise the score.
- Apple confirms at an assessed 58.1 against a published 59.4, a difference of -1.3. Its strongest dimensions are Action and Systemic Thinking at raw 3.6 and 3.4; its weakest are Equity and Accountability, both at raw 3.0.
