## ATTRIBUTION — THE SCANNER'S LEAD ITEM IS NOT FREETOWN'S CONDUCT

**The protest ban was issued by the Sierra Leone Police, a national force. It is not attributable to Freetown City Council and is not scored against this entity.**

The scan's lead item is the Sierra Leone Police prohibiting a Citizens Advocacy Network demonstration planned for 26 August 2026, by letter dated 20 August 2026, under Section 17(2) of the Public Order Act. The Public Order Act vests that power in the **Commissioner of Police**, not in any municipal authority. The Inspector General of Police invited the organisers to an interview on 19 August; the organisers declined to attend.

**This is exactly the Lagos precedent.** The 2026-08-18 cycle rejected a Lagos flag because the tear-gassing was carried out by the federal Nigeria Police Force, not by the city. The same reasoning disposes of this item. It is carried as national context only, and appears once in the scoring — at S4 Structural Critique — as evidence about the civic environment Freetown City Council operates in, not as its own act.

**The flooding is partly attributable and is scored.** Severe flash flooding in August 2026 killed a five-year-old girl, her mother and her grandmother when a wall collapsed at Metchem. Over 300 homes were completely flooded in Juba; two children were reported missing; hundreds were displaced and community boreholes were contaminated. **The disaster response was led by the National Disaster Management Agency — again a national body.** What is scored against Freetown City Council is its own standing flood-mitigation work and the fact that people continue to die in the same way each rainy season.

## Key Findings

- **Freetown's published score is almost certainly a placeholder.** It uses the same eight numbers as Abuja, a city 3,000 miles away that has also never been assessed.
- **The protest ban that triggered this review was not Freetown's doing.** The Sierra Leone Police, a national force, banned it under the Public Order Act. The city council has no such power.
- **Flooding in August 2026 killed a five-year-old girl, her mother and her grandmother** when a wall collapsed. Over 300 homes flooded in Juba, two children went missing, and boreholes were contaminated.
- **Freetown runs a serious climate-adaptation programme.** The United Nations Environment Programme, the World Bank and a UK research institute all document it, including a campaign to plant one million trees to stop the erosion that causes the floods.
- **A first real measurement puts Freetown at 38.1 out of 100, not 18.8 — but no change is proposed.** The good evidence is about long-running programmes, not about anything Freetown did in the past six days, and the rules require new conduct for an upgrade.
