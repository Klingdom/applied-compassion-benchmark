## Key findings

- Cohere scores highest of all 15 labs in this study, at 43.8, and the reason is Aya — not its enterprise business. Aya is a multilingual open-science programme covering more than 70 languages, deliberately including low-resource ones that no commercial buyer is asking for.
- Aya is sustained, not a gesture. It runs across four model generations from Aya 101 in early 2024 to Tiny Aya in 2026, and Expedition Aya gives researchers from those language communities a mentored route into the work with published results.
- Tiny Aya runs without cloud dependency, which removes a real barrier for people in low-bandwidth settings. That is a documented access barrier removed, not a stated intention.
- Fourteen news publishers sued Cohere in February 2025, and in November 2025 a federal judge in New York refused to dismiss any of it, finding the publishers had adequately alleged direct infringement, secondary infringement and false endorsement. Nothing has been decided on the merits and nothing here is scored as proven harm.
- The score still falls 17.1 points from the published 60.9, the smallest fall in this study. The published number was never measured; Cohere's real practice sits in the Functional band, not Established.
