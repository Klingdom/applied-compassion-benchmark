- Uruguay chose a rights-based answer to rising homicides. Its 10-year National Security Plan, reported on 11 September 2026, rejects the mass-detention model used in El Salvador.

- Uruguay's prisons are the weak point. They held more than 16,000 people by December 2025 in worsening conditions, and four inmates died in a fire that June.
- The Exemplary score of 83 is shared with four other countries and has never been fully audited.
