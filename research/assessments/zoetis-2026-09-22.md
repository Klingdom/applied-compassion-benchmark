## Key Findings

- Zoetis finished a five-year sustainability cycle with numbers you can check: Scope 1 and 2 emissions down 29% since 2021, 85% of global electricity from renewable sources, and 35 million dollars in Zoetis Foundation grants disbursed from 2021 to 2025.
- Its animal-health work genuinely addresses causes rather than symptoms. A study of its Dairy Wellness Profit Index reported measurable gains for U.S. dairy producers — better herd health, longer animal lives and lower methane intensity per unit of output.
- Its own employees describe the opposite story inside the company: toxic culture, retaliation for giving negative feedback, layoffs arriving without warning, and a reorganisation that they say destroyed a good culture. This is testimony, not an audit, but it is consistent across reviews.
- One practical access improvement is worth naming: a single-size, dual-temperature shipper now lets ambient and cold-chain products travel in one box, which lowers the cost of stocking medicines for smaller veterinary practices.
- The published score of 77.9 is a seed value, byte-identical across 11 unrelated Fortune 500 companies. This assessment records a first real measurement and routes the gap to calibration review rather than filing a one-off reset.
