## Why no score was assigned

Hocoma was selected for Batch 1 of the robotics-labs expansion on the basis that it holds roughly 16% of the rehabilitation robotics market and is the largest single firm in that segment. **The mandatory corporate-status check performed before assessment found facts that make a published score indefensible, so no assessment was carried out.**

Under §2.4 of `research/INDEX_EXPANSION_SCOPE_2026-08-20.md`, every baseline assessment must open with a dated corporate-status check, and **no status check means no assessment**. This entity fails that check.

## What could not be resolved

- **Whether Hocoma AG, the Swiss operating entity at Volketswil, is still trading normally.** Its website remains live with 2026 copyright and continues to list Lokomat products, training and technical documentation. That is consistent with an operating business and also with an unmaintained site.
- **Whether any buyer has acquired Hocoma AG or its assets.** No acquisition, administration, insolvency filing or asset sale was located in either direction.
- **Whether the parent's suspension of operations extends to the Swiss subsidiary.** The 6 November 2025 announcement concerns DIH Holding US, Inc. No dated source was found stating what happened to Hocoma AG specifically.

## Why this matters to the index

The robotics-labs roster already carries multiple entity-currency defects, including a company that stayed ranked "Established" for eleven months after it ceased operating. **Publishing a composite for a company whose parent has announced it is suspending operations would repeat exactly that error.** A dated tombstone or a hold is more honest than a number.

Note also that the scope study listed Hocoma as "Operating, 2025-11" on the strength of a November 2025 platform announcement — **the same month the parent told its regulator it was suspending operations.** A product press release is not a corporate-status source. That is a lesson for batches 2 to 4.

## Recommended disposition

**Hold. Do not add to the index.** Before any assessment is attempted:

1. Obtain a dated filing from the Swiss commercial register (Handelsregister, canton of Zurich) for Hocoma AG confirming whether the company is active, in liquidation, or transferred.
2. Search for any 2026 asset sale, administration or restructuring involving DIH Holding US, Inc. or its subsidiaries.
3. If Hocoma AG is confirmed operating under new ownership, re-list under the correct current name and parent, and assess from scratch.
4. If it is confirmed defunct or in liquidation, record a dated tombstone and do not list.

Under the four-state rule in §2.4 this entity is currently **unclassifiable** between Operating, Acquired and Defunct. That is precisely the case in which no number should be published.
