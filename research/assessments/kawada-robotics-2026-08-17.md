## Entity identification

KAWADA Robotics Corporation is a Japanese company within the KTI KAWADA GROUP, whose parent is Kawada Technologies, Inc. It builds the NEXTAGE dual-arm humanoid industrial robot, launched in 2009 and developed with Japan's National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST). It showed the NEXTAGE Fillie at CES 2026. It is privately held and publishes no sustainability or responsibility report.
