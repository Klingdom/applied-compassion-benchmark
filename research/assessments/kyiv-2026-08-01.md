### Score Difference Analysis

The -3.1 difference is carried forward unchanged from 22 July 2026 and is not driven by this window's event. Two figures in the scan summary do not survive checking and are corrected here so they do not propagate. The scan reports 32 killed and almost 180 injured in Kyiv on 31 July 2026, and describes it as the deadliest single strike on the capital this year with the highest number of children injured in a single attack. Contemporaneous reporting gives materially lower figures for the capital: the Kyiv Independent records one person killed and three injured in Kyiv city in the 30 July wave, with at least eight killed and more than 50 injured across all of Ukraine, and wire coverage of the 31 July wave reports three killed and more than a dozen wounded in the capital. The figure of 32 killed with about 180 injured closely matches the Kyiv strike of 31 July 2025, one year earlier. This appears to be a year-confusion in the scan summary rather than a finding. The correction does not change Kyiv's score in either direction, because the casualties are attributable to the attacker, not to the city government.

### Recommendation

Confirm the published score of 50.0, band Functional, with a watch flag. No proposal. The watch is on sustained civil-protection capacity under repeated long-range attack, not on the attacks themselves. A Kyiv-attributable finding would be evidence that shelter provision, emergency medical response or support to displaced residents is degrading — for example an independent audit of metro shelter capacity or of overcrowding, which contemporaneous reporting notes in densely populated districts.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: the city is the target, not the actor.** Russia struck Kyiv with ballistic missiles and drones overnight into 31 July 2026. Kyiv is scored on what its own government does, so being attacked does not lower its score.
- **Why it matters: the city did protect people.** Thousands of Kyiv residents sheltered in metro stations ahead of the attack, and Mayor Vitali Klitschko coordinated first responders at strike sites while the State Emergency Service ran rescues.
- **Why it matters: the scan casualty figures are wrong by a year.** The scan reported 32 killed and about 180 injured in Kyiv on 31 July 2026. Contemporaneous reporting gives one to three killed in the capital. The higher figure matches the strike of 31 July 2025.
- **Why it matters: shelter crowding is the real city-level signal.** Reporting notes overcrowding in metro stations in densely populated neighbourhoods. That is a capacity question the city can act on, and it is what the watch flag is set for.
- **Why it matters: no score moves.** Kyiv is confirmed at 50.0, rank 53 of 250 global cities, band Functional.
