## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**DIRECTIONALITY, BOTH WAYS.** Screening rule 3 requires that an entity surfaced on negative evidence must not yield an upgrade. It cuts the other way too: an entity surfaced on positive evidence must not yield a downgrade on that evidence. Neither direction is taken here, because the evidence does not reach the conduct threshold.

**THE POSITIVE EVIDENCE IS AN INTENTION, NOT A MEASURE.** The joint statement's operative verb is decisive. The twelve foreign ministers 'confirm their intention to introduce national and/or support European restrictions'. Haaretz reports (9 September) that Swedish, Danish and Finnish diplomats clarified their countries do not currently plan unilateral trade restrictions and would restrict settlement trade only as part of an EU decision. Denmark has therefore signed a statement of intent conditional on EU action. Under the plans-are-not-conduct screen, that is a stated commitment, tier 2 in substance even though the statement itself is a tier 5 government document. It does not lift any anchor. No upward move.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE (read first).** Published 81.3 (Exemplary, rank 12) was applied from `change-proposals/denmark-2026-04-25.json`, a downgrade from 100 on a multi-year evidence stack: a December 2025 European Court of Justice ruling that the 'ghetto law' potentially violates the EU ethnic-discrimination directive, a March 2026 visa ban on nationals of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar and Sudan during humanitarian crises, and sustained Amnesty and UN Committee Against Torture criticism on non-refoulement. The Equity dimension (raw 3.8, the lowest) carries that stack. The baseline is evidence-based and was set by Denmark's own conduct.

**WHY THIS IS WITHHELD RATHER THAN FILED (the material finding).** Denmark is in the registered `ASSESSOR_OVERRIDE_NAMES` set. The canonical formula applied to Denmark's own published dimension vector returns 90.2, not the published 81.3 -- a gap of 8.9 points. `validate-indexes.mjs` reports this as a warning, not an error, precisely because the override registration exempts it. The integer-grid expansion used by this cycle's convention returns 88.6. So a mechanical assessment that located no new conduct at all still measures +7.3 against the published value. That +7.3 is entirely the registered override and contains no evidence whatsoever. Filing it would be a textbook false positive: an upgrade, on an entity surfaced on conditional positive evidence, whose whole magnitude comes from a scoring convention. Screening rule 5 applies -- this is a calibration question about the override, and it is recorded as `flag-for-review` for coordinator-level review of the override cohort, not as a one-off upgrade.

**NO DOWNWARD EVIDENCE LOCATED.** No new adverse Danish conduct was located in the 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-18 window. The ghetto-law and non-refoulement stack behind Equity 3.8 is already priced and is not re-scored on continuation alone.
