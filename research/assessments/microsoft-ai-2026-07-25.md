## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the layoff figure that flagged Microsoft was a year old. The roughly 9,000 job cuts equal to about 4 per cent of the workforce happened in July 2025, not July 2026, and that claim was dropped.
- The real July 2026 figure is smaller. Microsoft cut about 4,800 roles, or 2.1 per cent of its workforce, announced on 6 July 2026, after offering voluntary buyouts to roughly 7 per cent of its US staff.
- Two law firms opened WARN Act compliance investigations into the Redmond mass layoff notice filed on 6 July 2026, and a separate Maryland WARN disclosure showed 213 Elder Scrolls Online staff cut.
- Unions have taken legal action over how the Xbox layoffs were carried out. Microsoft's responsible AI structures remain strong, so the score falls only on internal consistency and workforce stability.
