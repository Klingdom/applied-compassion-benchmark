That is RISK-006.

**WHY THE BAND-CROSSING TRIGGER IS NOT USED HERE.** Section 3f makes a band crossing filable at any delta. Screening rule 3 requires directionality to match evidence and there is no evidence; screening rule 5 requires calibration concerns to be recorded as `flag-for-review` rather than filed as one-off moves. Filing would put a Functional label on a Fortune 500 row on the strength of arithmetic. Withheld, with both rules named.
