### Recommendation

The published score is **overstated** and should be revised down to 46.2, band Functional. This is a seed correction, not a conduct downgrade.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Wandercraft has the strongest evidence base of any of the ten labs, and it still does not reach the "Exemplary" band the index publishes for it. That gap is the clearest proof the 83.0 value was never measured.
- Wandercraft is the only lab in the study that published a peer-reviewed safety evaluation of its device in patients outside its cleared indication — people with tetraplegia and high paraplegia. It then used that evidence to extend the label to them.
- The FDA extended Atalante X indications a second time on 3 November 2025, covering spinal cord injury from C4 to L5 and multiple sclerosis. Each extension reaches a group that was previously excluded.
- Its Walk as a Service programme took the exoskeleton out of hospitals and into a long-term care residence. The published case study reports a 58% increase in walking distance and a Functional Independence Measure score rising from 69 to 104 over a year.
- Accountability scores 2.2 of 5 and Integrity 2.2 of 5. Wandercraft publishes clinical evidence but no institutional data on harm, correction, repair, staff wellbeing or values governance.
