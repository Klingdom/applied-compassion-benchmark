## Assessment Context

- **Anti-false-positive screen — this is the decisive point.** The scan flagged Arizona on U.S. District Judge Roslyn O. Silver's order of 17 July 2026 appointing Annette Chambers-Smith as receiver over health care for more than 25,000 people in ADCRR prisons. That order was already scored. Arizona's published score of 36.2 was set by the 2026-07-19 assessment, which states in its own key findings that 'The judge finalised it on 17 July 2026 and installed a receiver paid $500,000 a year with power to hire, fire, and set the health budget.' The published dimension set matches that assessment exactly. There is therefore no new conduct in this window and no basis for movement.
- **Tier-provenance correction recorded.** The scan originally tagged Arizona Tier 1. A post-hoc audit found it had a base_priority of 3, far below the Tier-1 selection threshold, and had in fact surfaced through a Tier-3 prison-healthcare sector sweep. It was retagged Tier 2. The evidence, sourcing and dates are unaffected and the flag remains legitimate; only the provenance label changed.
- **Order verified independently.** The 17 July 2026 date and scope are corroborated by Arizona Mirror, Arizona Capitol Times and the ACLU press release, all carrying the same date. The receivership itself was ordered earlier — the court placed medical care under receivership in February 2026 and Arizona appealed in March 2026; the 17 July order finalised it and named the receiver.

## Key Findings
