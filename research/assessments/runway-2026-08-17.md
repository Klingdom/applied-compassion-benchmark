### Score Difference Analysis

**Action (-7.5 points scaled) and Boundaries (-7.5 points scaled) moved least, because Runway has real programmes.** The Hundred Film Fund carries $5 million with a stated potential $10 million plus $2 million in credits, is open to documentary and experimental makers, and was reported independently by Variety, IndieWire and Realscreen. A $10 million Builders fund followed on 31 March 2026. Those support Resource Mobilization and Autonomy Preservation at 3.

**Systemic Thinking (-2.5 points scaled) is the highest dimension at 2.4.** Runway embeds C2PA provenance in every output and states publicly that provenance is not a complete solution to misuse. Participating in a cross-industry standard and naming its limits is anchor 3 on both Root Cause Orientation and Coalitional Compassion.

**Accountability (-18.75 points scaled) fell furthest.** 404 Media published an internal Runway spreadsheet in July 2024 showing training data scraped from named YouTube creators using open-source tooling routed through purchased proxies to avoid being blocked. No acknowledgment by Runway was located, before or after publication. All five Accountability subdimensions sit at 2.

**Litigation was not scored as harm.** Businessing LLC v. Runway AI was filed in the Southern District of New York on 27 February 2026 alleging circumvention under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, and Runway is a co-defendant in Andersen v. Stability AI. Neither is decided.

### Recommendation

**The published 35.9 is overstated and should be revised down to 27.5. The band does not change.** Twenty-nine of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure rather than on evidence of harm, so the magnitude should be read as an upper bound on the correction.

## Key Findings

- **Journalists obtained a primary internal document.** 404 Media published a Runway spreadsheet showing the company trained on thousands of YouTube creators' videos and on pirated films, downloaded through purchased proxies to evade blocking.
- **Runway funds the people its technology affects.** The Hundred Film Fund offers grants from $5,000 to more than $1 million, and a separate $10 million Builders fund for early-stage startups launched on 31 March 2026.
- **Runway implements a cross-industry provenance standard and says it is not enough.** All outputs carry C2PA watermarks, and the company states publicly that provenance gives platforms and law enforcement a signal rather than a solution.
- **Accountability is the emptiest area.** All five Accountability subdimensions score 2. No transparency report, no published failure, no acknowledgment of the documented scraping and no repair to creators was located.
- **Twenty-nine of 40 subdimensions rest on nothing being found in either direction.** That is absence of disclosure, not evidence of harm, and it is stated here explicitly.

## Strongest Dimensions

**Systemic Thinking (2.4)** is strongest, on C2PA provenance implementation and the $10 million Builders fund. **Boundaries (2.2)** and **Action (2.2)** follow, both lifted by the film and startup funds.

## Weakest Dimensions

**Awareness (2.0), Empathy (2.0), Equity (2.0), Accountability (2.0) and Integrity (2.0)** all sit at the absence baseline. Accountability is the most consequential, because Runway has a documented harm and no located acknowledgment of it.

## Evidence Gaps

Employee evidence is tier 1 only: Glassdoor ratings are high in aggregate but one review describes constant pressure and surveillance. No institutional workforce data was located. No moderation outcome data was located, so Efficacy rests on activity description rather than results. No independent verification of the film fund's disbursement or grantee outcomes was located.

## Scoring Convention Applied

**Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is reserved for positive documented evidence of a specific failure. This is the majority convention across the three most recent de-seeding studies and was applied identically to all seven labs in this study. **Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** Where this company does not publish, the low anchor was scored and said so explicitly. A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review; a score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.
