## Key Findings

- IDEXX Laboratories has never been assessed by this benchmark. Its published 77.9 is a placeholder shared with ten other companies. This is the first real measurement, landing at 45.6.
- No adverse evidence about IDEXX was found at all — no regulatory finding, no lawsuit, no labour dispute, no complaint. The score moves because IDEXX does not publish in eight of the forty areas the benchmark measures, and absence of disclosure is scored at the low anchor.
- That distinction matters and should not be lost: absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm. IDEXX is not being marked down for anything it did.
- IDEXX does publish substantial, goal-tracked work: 121,400 hours of learning and development in a year, more than 24,000 volunteer hours against a 15,000-hour target, external recognition as a top disability-inclusive employer, and an on-track 2030 emissions target.
- The evidence test for a band change failed, because no independent adverse determination exists to pass it with. That failure is recorded rather than hidden.
