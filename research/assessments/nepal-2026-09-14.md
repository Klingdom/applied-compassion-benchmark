## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Nepal (priority 67, news score 40, the joint highest) on the Nepal-Tibet glacier-collapse flood that began on 26 August 2026. The NDRRMA's 12 September update put the death toll at 1,386. Nepal was last assessed on 2026-07-30 (confirmation at 45.6, with a watch flag).

## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**DEATH TOLL AND DATES VERIFIED.** Akashvani (13 September 2026, citing the NDRRMA) confirms 1,386 dead and about 5,130 missing. Wikipedia's compilation dates the figure to 12 September. The Al Jazeera URL the scanner cited is dated 1 September, before the window, and carries the earlier 1,050 figure; it is context only. The CNN 5 September live page returned HTTP 451 and was not used. A search synthesis also produced a stale '900-939 dead, 4,500 missing' figure, which was rejected.

**THE DISASTER IS NOT STATE CONDUCT; THE WARNING AND RESPONSE ARE.** The glacier collapse is a natural event and is not scored. The state's early warning, evacuation authority, relief and systemic learning are scored.

**SCAN OVERSTATEMENT CORRECTED.** The scan said 'no glacier-hazard early-warning system existed'. Mongabay and Wikipedia both say a flash-flood warning system did exist. It was configured for monsoon and perennial floods, and upstream stations were swept away before alerts could be sent. The scan's point about an 'unaddressed recommendation from a similar 2021 disaster' is confirmed in substance: a 2022 study of the October 2021 disaster called for binding forecast-to-action procedures that remain unimplemented.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE.** The 48.4 is shared by 9 countries but Nepal was individually assessed on 2026-07-30 (45.6, watch flag). That sidecar is the starting point here. There is no applied change.

**COUNTERVAILING EVIDENCE CREDITED.** A large relief fund, monthly housing compensation, free medical and mental-health care, 21,465 security personnel and 1,348 rescue flights hold Action and Empathy at their July values.

## Key Findings

- Nepal's flood warning failed the people it was meant to protect. Official text alerts about the 26 August 2026 glacier flood arrived as the water did, and only in Nepali.
- The same lesson went unlearned twice. A study after a 2021 disaster urged legally binding rules linking forecasts to evacuations, and they were never put in place.
- The death toll reached 1,386, with about 5,130 still missing, according to Nepal's disaster agency on 12 September 2026.
- The relief effort was large. It included a Rs 13.23 billion relief fund, monthly payments for damaged homes, free medical care and more than 1,300 rescue flights.
