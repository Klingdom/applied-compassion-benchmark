## Key Findings

- Why it matters: a court in Baku just handed nine journalists and activists sentences of 12 to 15 years, the harshest of Azerbaijan's crackdown so far. The Baku Grave Crimes Court convicted them on 27 July 2026 in the Toplum TV case, on charges of illegally receiving Western donor funds.
- The sentences exceed the seven-and-a-half to nine years given to AbzasMedia journalists in June 2025, so the trend is worsening.
- Attribution: the court is part of the Azerbaijani state, not the Baku municipal administration. The finding is scored as a civic-space condition in the city, not as a municipal act, which limits how far the score can move.
- Baku is already in the Critical band at 18.8 and ranks 188th of 250 global cities, so there is little room below.
- Assessed composite 16.9 against a published 18.8. Delta -1.9, below the 5-point proposal threshold.
