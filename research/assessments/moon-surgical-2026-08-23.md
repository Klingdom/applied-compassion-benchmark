## Baseline-establishment note

**Corporate status check — operating.** Moon Surgical is operating. Operating status was confirmed from the US Food and Drug Administration regulatory record as reported by dated third-party trade press: FDA clearance of the commercial Maestro system in June 2024, clearance of the AI-enhanced ScoPilot capability in March 2025, and two further clearances in July 2025 covering Maestro connectivity and a Predetermined Change Control Plan for ScoPilot. **Disclosure-capacity note.** Moon Surgical is a private French company that has raised roughly US$86.7 million. France's corporate duty-of-vigilance law applies only to much larger employers, so it has no obligation to publish a social report and does not publish one. Most low scores here measure that absence.

## Key Findings

- **Moon Surgical scores 26.3 of 100. Its single distinguishing practice is regulatory rather than voluntary: in July 2025 the US Food and Drug Administration cleared a Predetermined Change Control Plan for its AI-powered ScoPilot feature.**
- **A change control plan means the company had to say in advance how its AI would be allowed to change, and how each change would be checked, before any of it reached a patient.** That is anticipatory harm assessment with a regulator watching, and it is rare in this batch. Anticipatory Awareness scores 3 of 5.
- **More than 1,100 patients have been treated with Maestro, and no outcome data about them was located.** Patient counts measure activity, not whether the help worked.
- **Thirty-seven of 40 subdimensions rest on absence.** Moon Surgical is a private French company that has raised about US$86.7 million and has no legal duty to publish a social report. This is a disclosure finding, not a conduct finding.
- **No harm was located.** No lawsuit, recall, warning letter or safety incident against Moon Surgical was found.
