## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Iran is already near the bottom of the scale, and its own July 2026 conduct confirms that placement rather than changing it.
- Between 16 and 22 July 2026, Iran executed five political prisoners arrested during the January 2026 protests — close to one political execution per day.
- A further 12 young protesters were transferred to the Isfahan Revolutionary Court’s Enforcement Unit on 5 July 2026 and face imminent execution. Amnesty International says at least 78 people are under sentence of death, including minors.
- US strikes killed 50 people in Iran in July 2026, but harm inflicted on Iran by another state does not change Iran’s own score. The assessed score of 2.5 exactly matches the published 2.5.
