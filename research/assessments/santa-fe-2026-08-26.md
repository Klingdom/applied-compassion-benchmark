## ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION — THE ACTOR IS FEDERAL, NOT THE CITY. THE LAGOS PRECEDENT APPLIES DIRECTLY.

**The arrests were made by US Immigration and Customs Enforcement, a federal agency. They are not the City of Santa Fe's conduct and are not scored against it.**

The scanner flagged Santa Fe on more than a dozen ICE arrests, including a father followed to La Familia Medical Center with his 11-year-old daughter, and a mother stopped while driving a two-month-old on oxygen to a cardiologist. These are federal enforcement actions. The city has no authority over them and did not carry them out. **This is the same reasoning the 2026-08-18 cycle used to reject a Lagos flag**, where tear-gassing was carried out by the federal Nigeria Police Force rather than by the city.

**What is scored is the City of Santa Fe's own response**, which is documented and is on the compassionate side of the ledger:

- Mayor Michael Garcia publicly called the enforcement escalation "frightening and deeply unsettling" and said "Santa Fe is a community that cares for its neighbors, and we will continue to stand together during this difficult time."
- Santa Fe is a sanctuary city.
- The city has ordinances **in the pipeline** to forbid federal immigration actions being staged on city streets and property, and the mayor has been working toward an executive order or resolution restricting ICE use of city-owned parks, garages and parking lots.

**The last point is why this is not an upgrade.** Those ordinances are **not enacted**. Announced intent is not conduct. Anchor levels reward what an institution has done, not what it has said it will do.
