## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the 144 people who died did so at sea on a boat that left another country, and Mauritania is the state that brought 387 others ashore alive. UNHCR reported on 21 July 2026 that 144 people were dead or missing off Nouadhibou between 14 and 18 July 2026, in incidents that also produced three rescue and disembarkation operations.
- The deadliest vessel set off from Bafuloto in The Gambia and was rescued on 18 July after 25 days adrift, with 38 survivors and bodies aboard.
- Two children who lost their entire families at sea are recovering in hospital in Mauritania.
- Attribution: the deaths are not Mauritanian conduct. The in-window Mauritanian conduct on the record is rescue, disembarkation and medical care, which is positive but is also a baseline legal duty.
- A scan claim that Mauritania has intensified a migration crackdown with raids, mass pushbacks and coastal surveillance could not be tied to any in-window dated source and was NOT scored.
- Assessed composite 20.6 against a published 20.3. Delta +0.3, inside normal assessment variance. No upgrade is proposed.
