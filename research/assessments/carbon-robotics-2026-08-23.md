## Key Findings

- **Carbon Robotics scores 26.9 of 100, and the strongest evidence in its file was produced by two universities rather than by the company.** Cornell University and Rutgers University ran independent 2024 field trials on beet, spinach and pea using a commercial LaserWeeder. Published in peer-reviewed journals, they found laser weeding as effective as or better than three named herbicides, cutting weed biomass by 97 per cent or more.
- **Removing herbicides from the field is a genuine root-cause intervention, and it is independently evidenced.** A separate peer-reviewed study validates the system in organic farming, where no chemical option exists. That earns 3 of 5 on Root Cause Orientation — one of the higher Systemic Thinking scores in this batch.
- **The company's central labour claim has nothing behind it.** Carbon Robotics markets the machine on eliminating hand labour, and its own case study reports weeding labour down by more than 50 per cent at one farm. The chief executive says the technology is "not really taking away jobs" because growers cannot find workers. **No employment data, no farmworker survey and no transition provision were located.** Under this methodology that claim is scored as unevidenced, not credited.
- **Farmworkers are the people most affected and the people least visible in this file.** Hand-weeding is among the most precarious agricultural work there is. No channel, survey, consultation record or reporting line carrying farmworkers' views to Carbon Robotics was located.
- **Five of eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor of 25.0.** No workforce data, no complaint route, no self-published failure report and no repair process were located. **No adverse finding was located either.**
