## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Neura Robotics raised up to $1.4 billion in June 2026, one of the largest rounds in humanoid robotics, and publishes almost nothing about how it treats anyone.
- Neura Robotics is a German company founded in 2019 in Metzingen, with over 600 employees from 45 countries and a stated mission of "We Serve Humanity".
- The Series C round of up to $1.4 billion is backed by Tether, Nvidia, Amazon, Qualcomm and Bosch. A funding round is not a compassion signal and is scored only where it evidences resource mobilisation.
- The one third-party verification found is DIN EN ISO 9001:2015 certification covering development, manufacture and sale. That is a quality standard, not a safety or human-rights one, and it is scored narrowly.
- No adverse conduct of any kind was located — no lawsuits, no safety incidents, no labour findings. The gap to the published score is missing disclosure, not misconduct.

## Strongest Dimensions

## Evidence Gaps

The only third-party verification located is an ISO 9001 quality certification, which speaks to manufacturing consistency rather than to any compassion dimension, and it is used to support only two subdimensions.
