## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**ATTRIBUTION.** The protest is third-party conduct and is NOT scored. The state conduct is the government's policy reversals and the governance measures EDRi documents.

**DATES.** The Al Jazeera article is dated 10 September 2026 (in window). The 26 August EU veto is one week before the window and is context only. EDRi's update is dated 1 July 2026, pre-window but recent. The policy reversals Al Jazeera reports are undated within the Janša government's term.

**SOURCE DATE CONFLICT, NOT SCORING-RELEVANT.** Al Jazeera says Janša 'took office in July 2026'. Other reporting says parliament appointed him on 22 May and the government formed on 4 June.

**STALE SOURCE REJECTED.** A Peace Institute article, 'Slovenia: New Government Targeting NGOs and the Media', appeared in search results as current. On fetch it is dated 23 April 2020 and concerns Janša's previous government. It was excluded.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE.** The 62.5 has never been individually assessed. It is the 14-country placeholder vector, and Slovenia was explicitly named in the 2026-08-18 digest as part of that cluster, recommended for de-seeding.

**SCREENING - WITHHELD.** The canonical composite gives 58.7, a -3.8 move that crosses Established into Functional. The band-crossing clause is mechanically met. The proposal is withheld for three reasons:
1. **Evidence tier.** The band-crossing test requires at least two sources with at least one at tier 4 or above. The movement rests on Al Jazeera (tier 2) and EDRi (tier 1); the only tier-5 source is a neutral migration-policy headline. The test fails.
2. **Grid rounding.** About -1.9 of the -3.8 is the published 3.5 rounding down to 3.4. That alone takes 62.5 to 60.6, still Established.
3. **Plans are not findings.** EDRi's strongest items (the NGO funding cut and the surveillance amendments) are announced or expected, not confirmed as enacted, and are not scored.

This is the same discipline applied to Portugal on 2026-07-30, where a harsher reading crossing the band was considered and rejected on sourcing tier.
