### Score Difference Analysis

The published vector `4 / 4.5 / 4 / 3.5 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4` is byte-identical across six robotics labs occupying consecutive alphabetical ranks. It is a never-assessed uniform seed. Kinova Robotics has never been individually assessed against the 40 behavioural anchors before this report.

The published value asserts practice at the fourth anchor across all eight dimensions: published disaggregated data, formal pathways, documented reparative action, independent verification and community confirmation. Kinova Robotics publishes none of those things. 26 of the 40 subdimensions here score at the low anchor of 2, which under this study's fixed rule means **no evidence was found in either direction** — not that harm was found.

### Recommendation

The published score is **overstated** and should be revised down to 35.6, band Developing. This is a seed correction, not a conduct downgrade.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Kinova serves one of the highest-need, lowest-power groups the benchmark tracks — power wheelchair users who cannot use their arms. That earns real Equity credit. It does not earn an "Exemplary" band.
- The strongest evidence is a payer decision. Assistive robotic arms became a covered Medi-Cal benefit on 1 September 2024, and Disability Rights California publishes a guide telling disabled Californians how to claim it.
- A named commercial payer policy exists: AmeriHealth Caritas VIP Care coverage policy CCP.1520 covers the wheelchair-mounted Kinova Jaco2. Public and managed-Medicaid coverage removes the largest barrier for low-income users — cost.
- The JACO arm can be driven by joystick, head array or sip-and-puff control, so people with very different residual movement can use the same device. That is genuine contextual sensitivity.
- Accountability and Integrity both score 2.0 of 5, the absence-of-disclosure baseline. Kinova is private and publishes no institutional data on harm, correction, repair or internal culture. That is an evidence finding, not an adverse one.
