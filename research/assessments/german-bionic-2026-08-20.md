## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. German Bionic Systems GmbH does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index.

**Corporate status check — flagged.** German Bionic is operating under its own name from Augsburg, Germany, and presented the Exia exoskeleton at CES 2026. **Ownership changed in March 2026, when Archimedes Partners acquired the company**, describing the deal as strengthening German Bionic's financial foundation and supporting international scaling ([German Bionic, acquisition notice](https://www.germanbionic.com/news/archimedes-partners-acquires-german-bionic-to-scale-ai-powered-exoskeleton-platform); [German Bionic, Exia at CES 2026](https://www.germanbionic.com/news/ai-to-wear-german-bionic-presents-the-exia-robotic-exoskeleton-at-ces-2026)). The company retains its name, brand and independent public communications, so it is scored as operating rather than frozen under §2.4. **Re-verify at the next publication cycle**; an acquired company that stops publishing independently must be re-scoped.

**Evidence base and its limits.** Almost everything located is either company-authored or trade-press coverage of company announcements. **No study with data on injury reduction was located**, despite the company's central claim that its exoskeletons "measurably reduce injury risks". That gap is stated explicitly below rather than credited.

**Absence versus conduct.** No adverse finding of any kind about German Bionic was located. **Where a score rests on absence of disclosure, the row says so.**

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **32 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 8 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **German Bionic says its exoskeletons "measurably reduce injury risks". No measurement was found.** No study, dataset or independent evaluation supporting the claim was located. Efficacy therefore scores 2 of 5 — the absence baseline — for a company whose entire proposition is injury prevention.
- **The product design itself is genuinely person-adapted.** Independent trade press describes Exia adjusting support in real time to the individual wearer and load, up to 38kg of dynamic lift support. That earns Contextual Sensitivity and Proportionality above the floor.
- **The wearer is not the customer, and nobody discloses how that is handled.** Employers buy the devices; workers wear them and generate detailed movement data. No consent architecture was located. This is the sharpest unaddressed question in the record.
- **Ownership changed in March 2026.** Archimedes Partners acquired the company. It still publishes under its own name, so it is scored as operating — but this record needs re-verification next cycle.
- **Thirty-two of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure**, and no adverse finding of any kind was located.

## Strongest Dimensions
