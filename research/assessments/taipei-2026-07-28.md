## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Taiwan's own watchdog found that Taipei's labour department logged a child worker's sexual-assault report and then did nothing for her, and the department blamed the central government. The Control Yuan passed its censure of the Taipei Department of Labor on 22 July 2026, after the 16-year-old McDonald's employee died by suicide.
- The Control Yuan named three failures: not applying the best-interests-of-the-child principle, not providing or referring support to the victim in time, and insufficient communication between agencies.
- This is Taipei's own conduct. The Department of Labor is a Taipei City Government agency, so the attribution rule does not shield the city here.
- The department's public response on 27 July 2026 was that the problem lies in central-government systems and law. That deflection is why Accountability falls below 4.0.
- Assessed composite 73.3 against a published 81.4. Delta -8.1, and the assessed value crosses from Exemplary into Established.

## Strongest Dimensions

- Awareness, Empathy, Boundaries and Integrity (all raw 4.0). Taipei's service systems are documented, funded and consistent, and the complaint did reach a national oversight body.

## Weakest Dimensions
