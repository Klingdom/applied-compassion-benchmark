## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the harm that flagged Kansas City was inflicted by federal agents, not by the city. Under the benchmark’s attribution rule, another actor’s conduct does not lower a city’s score.
- The documented harm is serious. KCUR reported on 13 July 2026 that at least 30 Kansas City residents were detained during the World Cup, including six workers at a food warehouse on 9 July.
- The city’s own conduct ran the other way. Mayor Quinton Lucas joined seven mayors and 38 local governments in a federal amicus brief, and the City Council passed a five-year moratorium on non-municipal detention facilities.
- The assessed score of 36.9 is 1.0 point above the published 35.9, inside the 5-point threshold, so the developing-band score is confirmed.
