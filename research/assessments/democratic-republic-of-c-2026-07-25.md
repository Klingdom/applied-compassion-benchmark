## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the killings that flagged the DRC were carried out by a non-state armed group, so they do not lower the state's score. At least 16 people were killed on 22 July 2026 in an attack blamed on the Allied Democratic Forces at Mandeite and Ndiapanda in Mambasa territory.

- Opposition leader Martin Fayulu was injured during that policed rally. The DRC sits near the absolute floor and this evidence confirms that placement.
