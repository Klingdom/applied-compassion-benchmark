### Recommendation

**CONFIRM.** The composite delta of -3.4 is inside the 5-point threshold. The violence itself is non-state conduct and is not scored against the city; the failure to keep public clinics open to everyone is municipal conduct and is scored. A watch flag is set.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the attacks were not carried out by the city, but keeping the clinic doors open was the city's job. Under the attribution rule, violence by the vigilante group Operation Dudula is non-state conduct and does not lower Johannesburg's score. The failure to guarantee access to public health facilities does.
- Reporting on 14 July 2026 found the anti-migrant group blocking foreigners from public hospitals and clinics, including the Roodepoort Clinic west of Johannesburg, with vulnerable women reportedly forced to give birth on bare floors.
- Nigeria completed the evacuation of its own citizens on 15 July 2026. A fifth and final government-arranged flight carrying 305 people left O.R. Tambo International Airport, bringing the total repatriated since 10 June to 1,490 — the largest such evacuation since the 2019 xenophobic riots.
- A returnee described the atmosphere in an interview published 20 July 2026, saying there is hate in the atmosphere.
- The 2019 pattern recurred in 2026, which is why Accountability sits at raw 1.8: systemic learning did not hold, and no repair has been documented for those attacked, displaced or refused care.

## Strongest Dimensions

- Action (ACT) at raw 2.6 and Systemic Thinking (SYS) at raw 2.4 — routine municipal delivery and long-horizon planning are functional.

## Weakest Dimensions

- Accountability (ACC) at raw 1.8. The same failure recurred seven years later and no repair has been made.
