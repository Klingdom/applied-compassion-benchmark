## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: children were among those taken in.** The Philippine National Police arrested about 51 protesters at a demonstration on Commonwealth Avenue in Quezon City on 27 July 2026, and organisers say 13 of them were minors.
- **Why it matters: the charges are about litter and face coverings, not violence.** Police cited Republic Act No. 9003 (the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act), Presidential Decree No. 880 (the Public Assembly Act) and local ordinances banning face coverings.
- **Why it matters: rights groups say the scale was the point.** Advocacy groups called the response "overkill" and demanded the release of those detained, against a backdrop of roughly 22,000 police deployed across Metro Manila for the State of the Nation Address.
- **Why it matters: the move is small on purpose.** One protest, documented at tier 2, moves three of 40 subdimensions. It does not rewrite a national score.
