## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the Haitian state has lost effective control of most of its capital to gangs; the critical-band score reflects near-total institutional collapse, confirmed by the latest UN data.
- By July 19, 2026, the UN reported 1.5 million displaced, at least 1,600 killed in three months, and gang control of 70-75% of Port-au-Prince.
- Over half the population now needs relief aid, underscoring the state's inability to protect or serve citizens.
- No in-window evidence of institutional recovery; the published 4.7 is confirmed.

## Evidence Gaps

- State collapse and insecurity make institutional data scarce; assessment relies on UN humanitarian reporting rather than government sources.
