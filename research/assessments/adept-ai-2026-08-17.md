## Key findings

- Adept AI is an entity-record currency problem, not a scoring problem. Amazon hired its leadership and licensed its technology in July 2024, and reporting puts the remaining company at roughly 20 employees, down from about 100.
- It is still operating under its own name, led by former engineering director Zach Brock, so it is not the Rethink Robotics case of a dissolved company still ranked. But it is not the company the index entry describes.
- Thirty-nine of 40 subdimensions rest on complete absence of evidence. No adverse conduct of any kind was found — no lawsuits, no labour findings, no regulatory action, nothing.
- The 22.8-point fall measures missing disclosure by a 20-person company, and nothing else. It should not be read as a finding about conduct.
- The open question is whether a 20-person residual entity belongs in a published ranking at all. That is a scope question for the index, not a scoring question.
