## Entity identification

Harmonic Bionics, Inc. of Austin, Texas built the Harmony SHR, a bilateral upper-extremity robotic exoskeleton that works with a patient's scapulohumeral rhythm. The device was registered with the US Food and Drug Administration as a Class II 510(k)-exempt device and commercially launched in April 2023. The business was acquired by Bioness Medical in June 2025.

### Score Difference Analysis

The published Empathy score of 4.0 asserted that patients confirm they feel heard and shape decisions. Nothing was located on patient or clinician consultation. The published Equity score of 3.0 asserted measured coverage; no payer coverage, pricing or access-support evidence was located for the Harmony SHR, unlike the exoskeleton companies assessed in the concurrent Exemplary-cluster study, where Medicare, Medi-Cal and Veterans Affairs decisions were findable. Where this company genuinely earns credit is Action and Accountability, on two specific items the seed could not see: an independent completed clinical trial with posted results, and a voluntary customer-wide safety warning after a single malfunction.

### Recommendation

The published score is substantially overstated and should be revised down to 30.0. The record should also be corrected to show Bioness Medical as the acquirer of the business. Harmonic Bionics is the strongest of the three Cluster A labs, and the reason is specific: it is the only one whose product has been examined by an outside hospital that published the result.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: this company held the benchmark's second-highest band on a placeholder, and it no longer exists on its own.** Bioness Medical acquired the assets and business of Harmonic Bionics on 18 June 2025.
- **It is the strongest of the three labs at this seed, and for a concrete reason.** Shirley Ryan AbilityLab ran an independent trial of the Harmony SHR in 21 acute stroke patients, completed it on 30 May 2023, and posted the results.
- **The company warned all its customers after one machine malfunctioned.** During a demonstration, a customer hit the emergency stop and saw smoke in the rear enclosure. No patient was in the device. The company sent a precautionary notice to other customers in 2024.
- **No access or affordability evidence was found.** Unlike exoskeleton makers with published Medicare or Medi-Cal decisions, nothing was located on how patients pay for Harmony SHR therapy.
- **Roughly 30 of the 40 scores rest on the absence of any published evidence.** That is not a finding of harm.

## Strongest Dimensions

Action, Boundaries and Accountability, all at 2.4 of 5. Each is carried by a specific verifiable item: an independent hospital trial with posted results, an FDA-constrained indication, and a voluntary customer-wide safety warning.

## Weakest Dimensions

Empathy, Equity and Integrity, all at 2.0. Nothing was located on patient consultation, complaint validation, cultural adaptation, access design or how the company behaves when compassion costs it something.

The report's existence, device, manufacturer and page title are verified; the narrative text was not read line by line. No pricing, payer or patient-experience information was located.

## Scoring Conventions Applied

- **Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is used only where positive documented evidence of a specific failure exists. This is the "2-convention", the more conservative of the two conventions live in this repository.
- **A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review. A score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.**
- 32 of 40 subdimensions sit at 2. Of those, approximately 30 rest on absence of disclosure rather than on located evidence of a practice.
