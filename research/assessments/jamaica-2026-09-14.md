## Key Findings

- Jamaica is rebuilding homes after Hurricane Melissa, but slowly compared with the damage. On 3 September 2026 it signed contracts for 268 temporary homes worth J$1.05 billion, about US$6.6 million.
- The figure is in Jamaican dollars. Reading it as US dollars would overstate the spending about 160 times.
- Families were checked before selection. The Ministry of Labour and Social Security verified each household's circumstances.
- The need is far larger. About 24,000 homes were destroyed, and many families were still in shelters six months after the storm.
