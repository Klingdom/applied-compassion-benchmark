## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Brain Corp does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index, and no prior score, seed or placeholder existed for it. All eight index files were searched for the entity name before assessment and returned no match. There is no published-comparison section because there is nothing to compare against. This report establishes the first number.

**Corporate status check — operating.** Brain Corp is operating. Walmart's own corporate newsroom records the completion of Sam's Club's national inventory-scan deployment and states that Brain Corp thereby became the world's largest supplier of robotic inventory scanners. Dated independent reporting records more than 50,000 BrainOS-powered robots deployed, use in at least 1,800 Walmart stores since 2019, roughly US$193 million raised from SoftBank Vision Fund and Tennant, and a headcount of about 304 as of 30 June 2026. Corporate status rests on that customer and third-party record rather than on a company press release.

**Disclosure-capacity note.** Brain Corp is a private US company of roughly 300 people that has raised about US$193 million and whose software runs more than 50,000 deployed robots. Its reach is very large relative to its size. It has no obligation to publish a social report and does not publish one.

**⚠️ Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** Where a score rests on nothing being found, the row says so in plain words. No lawsuit, recall, regulator action or safety incident against Brain Corp was located. **Employee-review discussion of repeated layoffs was seen on Glassdoor and is not relied on for any score**, because no dated independent source corroborates it — the same convention applied to Skydio in Batch 2.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **37 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** (93%) and 3 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **Brain Corp scores 25.0 of 100 — the structural floor — despite its software running more than 50,000 deployed robots. Thirty-seven of its 40 subdimensions rest on absence.**
- **This is the largest reach-to-disclosure gap in the batch.** BrainOS robots run in at least 1,800 Walmart stores since 2019 and in nearly 600 Sam's Club locations. No measurement of what that has done to store associates — injury, workload, hours, staffing — was located anywhere.
- **Brain Corp says automating monotonous tasks makes jobs more enjoyable, and publishes nothing to show it.** No employment data, workload study or retail-worker survey was located.
- **Robots move through public retail aisles carrying sensors, and no data-handling policy for that was located.** The retailer buys the robot; shoppers and associates are the ones recorded.
- **No harm was located.** No lawsuit, recall, regulator action or safety incident against Brain Corp was found. Employee-review discussion of repeated layoffs was seen and deliberately not used, because no dated independent source corroborates it.
