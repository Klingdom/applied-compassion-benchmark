### Analysis

The assessed composite of 46.9 sits 14.0 points below the published 60.9 and crosses from Established into Functional. This is the smallest fall of the four seed-cluster corrections in this batch, and deliberately so: ManpowerGroup scores at anchor 3 or above on 33 of 40 subdimensions, carries 17 external ethics assessments, and has the batch's only 4/5 subdimension (S1 Root Cause Orientation). What separates 46.9 from 60.9 is not conduct; it is the absence of independent verification and the absence of any published measurement of outcomes for the workers ManpowerGroup actually places.

## Key Findings

- ManpowerGroup has never been assessed by this benchmark. Its published 60.9 is the known 22-company Fortune 500 placeholder. This is the first real measurement, landing at 46.9.
- No adverse evidence about ManpowerGroup was found at all. The score moves because 60.9 was never measured, and because ManpowerGroup publishes nothing in seven of the forty areas the benchmark looks at.
