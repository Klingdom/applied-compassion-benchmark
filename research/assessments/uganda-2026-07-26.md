## Key Findings

- Why it matters: in Uganda the army is now being used against the people who challenge it in court. Human Rights Watch reported on 16 July 2026 that since mid-June, security forces have unlawfully seized at least five critics of President Yoweri Museveni and his son, army chief General Muhoozi Kainerugaba.
- Lawyer Erias Lukwago was seized from his Kampala home on 15 June, the day he was preparing to serve legal summons on Kainerugaba. Kainerugaba appeared to confirm he was holding him, boasting that he had him in a basement and sharing a photograph of what appeared to be Lukwago blindfolded.
- Since 28 June the military has surrounded and laid siege to the premises of the Nation Media Group, Uganda's largest independent media company, after Kainerugaba ordered the shutdown of its television and newspaper operations.
- The United Nations Secretary-General issued a note to correspondents on 2 July 2026 noting concern about the detention of political and civic actors, including cases where whereabouts have not been clearly established.
- Uganda's previous assessment on 12 July 2026 was a screening confirmation about disease containment. It recorded governance concerns as already priced and unchanged. The Human Rights Watch report published four days later documents an escalation that no prior assessment evaluated.
