## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Haiti on the UN Designated Human Rights Expert's finding that at least 613 people were killed and 375 injured in two Port-au-Prince neighbourhoods between 6 March and 31 July 2026, and on Haitian Times reporting of intensified gang attacks in early August. Haiti was last assessed two days ago, on 2026-08-18, and sits near the floor at 4.7 of 100.

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ATTRIBUTION — the documented harm is overwhelmingly gang conduct, not state conduct.** The UN Designated Human Rights Expert describes the "wanton, intentional infliction of incredible pain" **by gangs**. Gangs are non-state armed actors. Their killings are not scored against the Haitian state. What is scored is the state's own conduct: its near-total failure to protect, investigate or provide for the displaced, and the conduct of the Haitian National Police and the transitional authorities. This distinction is the reason Haiti's Awareness, Empathy and Action dimensions sit at 1.2 rather than lower — the state's failure is a failure of capacity and provision, which the anchors score at the low end, not an affirmative campaign of state violence.

**FUTURE-DATED MENTION VERIFIED (gate warning 4).** The scan referenced "December 13" elections. This is a **genuinely scheduled future event, not a misdated past one**. Haiti's Provisional Electoral Council issued a decree setting 13 December 2026 for the first round of presidential, legislative and local elections plus a constitutional referendum, with a runoff on 21 February 2027 and candidate registration running from 20 August to 2 October 2026. France 24 dates the decision to 2026-07-27 and the Haitian Times to 2026-07-28. **A scheduled future election is not scoreable conduct and is not scored here.** It is recorded only as context for why gangs intensified attacks.

**Date checks.** UN News story path /2026/08/; Haitian Times /2026/08/12/; France 24 /20260727-. All explicit 2026. No year-confusion found.

## Floor limitation

**Haiti is near the arithmetic floor.** Five of its eight published dimensions — Equity, Boundaries, Accountability, Systemic Thinking and Integrity — are already at 1.0. The maximum possible remaining downward movement from 4.7 is 4.7 points, below the 5-point filing trigger. **A downgrade proposal for Haiti is arithmetically unavailable** unless the band-crossing clause applies, and Haiti is already in the lowest band. Haiti can only be confirmed or moved upward.

### Analysis

The assessed composite of 3.1 sits 1.6 points below the published 4.7, in the same Critical band. No filing trigger is met and, as noted above, none can be met downward: five of eight dimensions are already immovable and the total remaining downward room (4.7 points) is below the 5-point trigger. The published score is confirmed. The difference between 3.1 and 4.7 is not a finding that Haiti deteriorated in 48 hours; it reflects this assessment scoring the Awareness, Empathy and Action dimensions at 1.2 rather than the published 1.5 because integer subdimension anchors cannot produce 1.5, and because two of the three credits in those dimensions (UN cooperation, gang-suppression force deployment) are weak.

## Key Findings

- Haiti holds at about 5 of 100. Five of its eight dimensions are already at the minimum, so no downgrade proposal is arithmetically possible — the whole remaining distance to zero is smaller than the threshold needed to file one.
- The UN's human rights expert for Haiti found at least 613 people killed and 375 injured in just two Port-au-Prince neighbourhoods between 6 March and 31 July 2026. More than a third were people with no gang connection, including 111 women and 77 children.
- That killing is done by gangs, not by the government. The benchmark scores the state on its failure to protect, investigate and provide — not on the gangs' acts.
- About 1.5 million people are displaced across Haiti. There is no state registry of them.
- Haiti has scheduled its first national election in a decade for 13 December 2026. That is a real future date, verified against the electoral council's decree. A scheduled election is not conduct, and it is not scored.

## Strongest Dimensions

Systemic Thinking (SYS, raw 1.40) is marginally the strongest, on the strength of a published multi-stage electoral calendar and active participation in international security and humanitarian coalitions. It remains deep in the Critical band.

## Weakest Dimensions

Equity (EQU), Boundaries (BND), Accountability (ACC) and Integrity (INT) all sit at exactly 1.00, every subdimension at the minimum. Equity is the most firmly evidenced: 111 women and 77 children among 613 dead in two neighbourhoods, with no state provision for the 1.5 million displaced.

## Evidence Gaps

- The UN expert's full report was not published at the time of assessment; the figures come from UN News reporting ahead of its release.
- No breakdown is available of how many of the 613 deaths involved Haitian National Police operations rather than gang action, which limits precision in attributing state conduct.
- Haiti was assessed two days earlier, on 2026-08-18. This cycle adds the UN expert's figures and the verified election date but is otherwise a short-interval re-test.
