### Recommendation

**DOWNGRADE** (confidence: medium). Composite delta of -7.2 points crosses the 5-point threshold; a change proposal has been filed.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: a military coup has replaced elected government with unaccountable rule. On November 23-26, 2025, Guinea-Bissau's Military High Command seized power before election results were announced and jailed the opposition leader.
- The country was suspended from ECOWAS, the African Union, and the CPLP, and Freedom House cut its score 8 points to 33/100, citing the coup and institutional collapse.
- The former prime minister and PAIGC leader Domingos Simoes Pereira was ordered back to prison on July 10, 2026; ECOWAS demanded release of all detained political figures on July 21, 2026.
- The published score of 20.3 predates any individual assessment of the coup. The downgrade reflects the military government's own conduct: political exclusion (EQU), loss of accountability (ACC), and reversal of democratic order (INT).

## Evidence Gaps

- Compassion-dimension mapping of a governance rupture is desk-based; service-delivery and humanitarian-response evidence under the transitional government is limited. ACT, EMP, and SYS held at baseline for lack of specific new evidence.
