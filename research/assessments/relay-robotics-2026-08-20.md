## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Relay Robotics, Inc. does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index.

**Corporate status check (required before assessment).** Relay Robotics, Inc. is operating under that name. On 3 June 2026 it announced with BayCare Health System the deployment of two additional Relay robots at Winter Haven Hospital and Winter Haven Women's Hospital, building on a 2025 deployment at St. Anthony's Hospital ([PR Newswire, BayCare expansion](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/baycare-expands-autonomous-hospital-delivery-with-new-relay-robots-at-winter-haven-hospitals-302788892.html)). No acquisition, rename or insolvency event was found. No funding disclosure was located.

**Evidence base and its limits.** Relay's best evidence is a **jointly issued announcement with its hospital customer**, which quantifies an outcome — an estimated 150-plus clinical hours recovered per month across the Winter Haven deployment — and a delivery reliability figure above 99%. A customer co-signing a number is stronger than a company asserting one alone, but it is still promotional material, not an independent evaluation. It is treated as tier 3.

**Absence versus conduct.** No adverse finding of any kind about Relay Robotics was located. **Where a score rests on absence of disclosure, the row says so.**

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **32 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 8 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **Relay Robotics markets itself on reducing nurse burnout, and it does publish a number: 150-plus clinical hours recovered per month at two Winter Haven hospitals.** That figure is issued jointly with BayCare Health System, so the customer stands behind it. No independent evaluation exists.
- **The claim has never been tested against nursing outcomes by anyone other than the seller and the buyer.** No peer-reviewed study, no staff survey and no attrition data were located. Efficacy scores 3 of 5, not higher.
- **The relationship with BayCare grew over time**, from St. Anthony's Hospital in 2025 to two more hospitals in 2026. Sustained engagement is real evidence and lifts Follow-Through.
- **Thirty-two of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.** No adverse finding of any kind about the company was located.
- **Nobody asks what happens to the porters.** Relay's robots absorb delivery-runner work that hospital couriers and porters also do. No analysis of that effect was located, so Interconnection Awareness scores 2 of 5.

## Strongest Dimensions
- **Action (40.0)** — a published reliability standard, a quantified outcome co-signed by the customer, and multi-year sustained deployment.

## Weakest Dimensions

## Evidence Gaps
- No independent evaluation of the nurse-burnout claim.
- No published data on Relay's own workforce or funding.
- No consent or data-handling disclosure for sensors operating in patient corridors.
- No analysis of the effect on adjacent hospital roles.
