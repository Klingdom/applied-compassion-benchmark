## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Marathon Petroleum on final Texas Commission on Environmental Quality reports released 18 August 2026 covering an uncontrolled emissions event at its El Paso refinery on 4-5 August 2026. **Marathon Petroleum has never been individually assessed by this pipeline** (`last_assessed: null`), so this is a first measurement against a published value of 15.6.

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ACTOR VERIFIED — this is COMPANY conduct, not purely a regulatory or third-party action.** The check the cycle instruction required was run. The **release itself is Marathon Petroleum's own conduct**: an electrical issue at Marathon's refinery caused a shutdown and restart of its Fluid Catalytic Cracker, producing intermittent flaring from 4 August at 7:47pm to 7 August at 3:55pm. The TCEQ reports are a regulator's documentation *of* company conduct, not the conduct itself. **Marathon's response is also company conduct** and is scored: its own air-monitoring team monitored through 10 August and reported air quality below community exposure guidelines.

**Emissions figures — the corrected number is used.** Preliminary TCEQ reporting listed three contaminants. Final reports list **20 air contaminant compounds**, including more than 3,800 pounds of carbon monoxide and about 1,095 pounds of sulfur dioxide. A subsequent update puts the total at **about 15.4 tons of pollutants — more than 10 tons higher than previously reported**. The upward revision of more than ten tons is itself scored, under Transparency and Harm Acknowledgment.

**Attribution of the regulatory actions.** The TCEQ's 3-0 vote to grant a contested case hearing on the El Paso air permit, and US Representative Veronica Escobar's call for an EPA investigation, are **third-party actions** and are not scored as Marathon conduct. They are used only as evidence that the company's own account is contested.

**Date checks.** KVIA carries /2026/08/18/ and /2026/08/10/. Inside Climate News carries /18082026/. Incident dates 4-7 August 2026 are given in the TCEQ filings as reported. All explicit 2026. No year-confusion found.

**SCORING CONVENTION APPLIED.** Several subdimensions below are scored at the low anchor because Marathon publishes no relevant data, not because harm is established. **Absence of disclosure is scored as 2, and absence is not evidence of harm.** This is stated explicitly wherever it applies.

### Analysis

The assessed composite of 13.7 sits 1.9 points below the published 15.6, in the same Critical band. No filing trigger is met. The published score is confirmed. This is Marathon Petroleum's first individual assessment and it lands close to its published value, which is a useful independent check on that value. ## Key Findings

- Marathon Petroleum holds at about 15 of 100 in the Critical band. This is the company's first real assessment and it lands 1.9 points below the published value — close enough to confirm it.
- An electrical fault at Marathon's El Paso refinery on 4 August 2026 caused three days of intermittent flaring. Final regulator reports list 20 air contaminant compounds released, including more than 3,800 pounds of carbon monoxide.
- The total was later revised to about 15.4 tons of pollutants — more than 10 tons higher than first reported. The correction came from the regulator, not the company.
- The refinery sits in a predominantly Hispanic, low-income El Paso neighbourhood. Residents had to demand answers, the city council had to formally request a public meeting, and a state report contradicted the company's claims about odours.
- Several scores here reflect that Marathon publishes nothing on the subject, not that harm is proven. Absence of disclosure is scored at the low anchor and is not evidence of harm.

## Strongest Dimensions

Boundaries (BND, raw 2.00) is the strongest dimension, and only because every subdimension in it is scored at the absence-of-disclosure anchor rather than on established conduct. Action (ACT, raw 1.80) follows, on the strength of regulatory filing and on-site monitoring that did occur.

## Weakest Dimensions
