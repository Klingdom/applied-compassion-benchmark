## Attribution

The New Jersey Attorney General's investigation is the **state's** act, not GEO's, and is not scored as GEO conduct. What is scored is the underlying conduct the state describes: the conditions and care at a facility GEO owns and operates. The New Jersey release is explicit that GEO is the responsible operator: "For detained individuals, including those held by a private corporation like GEO Group, those rights include the right to humane treatment and adequate medical care." The record-high ICE custody-death trend is **ICE system-wide** and is scored as context only, not as GEO conduct — the same limit applied on 2026-08-11.

**PRE-ADJUDICATION.** An investigative subpoena is not a finding. Nothing here treats New Jersey's investigation as establishing that GEO violated anyone's civil rights.
