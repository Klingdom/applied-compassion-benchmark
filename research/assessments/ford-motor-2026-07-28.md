## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Ford is catching its own defects, and it is producing far more of them than its rivals. Ford recalled 387,911 Explorer and Aviator SUVs on 20 July 2026 for a seat-latch fault and 565,691 Broncos on 24 July 2026 for fire risk, taking it to 60 recalls in 2026 against General Motors' 19.
- Both recalls carry free remedies and plainly stated injury risk, so detection and response are working. That keeps Action at raw 3.0.
- No injuries or deaths were reported in any in-window source. Nothing was scored beyond what the sources state.
- The count itself is the finding. Sixty recalls in a year indicates upstream design and supplier practice is not changing, so Systemic Thinking falls to raw 2.6.
- Assessed composite 41.9 against a published 42.5. Delta -0.6, below the 5-point proposal threshold.

## Strongest Dimensions

- Action (raw 3.0). Ford mobilised free remedies across nearly one million vehicles in a single week and stated the risk plainly.

## Weakest Dimensions
