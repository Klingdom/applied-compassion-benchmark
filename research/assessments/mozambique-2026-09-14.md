## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Mozambique (priority 57) on the 3 September 2026 Islamic State Mozambique attack on a hunting camp in the Niassa Special Reserve, the group's first incursion into Niassa since May 2025. Mozambique has never been individually assessed.

## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**ATTRIBUTION - NON-STATE ATTACK NOT SCORED.** The killing, abductions and destruction were committed by Islamic State Mozambique, a non-state armed group. They are NOT scored against the state. The state's protective failure (the incursion, the destroyed police post) and its response (security patrols, return of residents) are scored.

**FIGURES RECONCILED.** AP (7 September) reports 'up to 20 workers abducted' while local police report 'at least 11 people, including children'. Both are in the sources; the scan's 'up to 20' is the upper figure. About 2,700 displaced (up from an initial 2,000). One killed.

**DATE CHECK.** Club of Mozambique (8 September) dates the attack to Thursday, which is 3 September 2026, consistent with the scan.

**PRE-WINDOW EVIDENCE USED, AND WHY.** HRW World Report 2026 (events of 2025) documents police involvement in killing about 400 people during the post-election unrest, incommunicado detention of child protesters, and no credible investigation. This is within three years and has never been scored, because the entity has never been assessed. It moves three Accountability and Boundaries subdimensions. Direction is consistent with the negative surfacing evidence.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE.** 23.4 is shared by 7 countries, has never been individually assessed and has no applied change.

**NEAR THE BAND EDGE.** Measured 21.3 is 1.3 points above the Critical threshold.

## Key Findings

- Mozambique's insurgency is spreading. On 3 September 2026 Islamic State fighters attacked a hunting camp in Niassa province, abducting 11 to 20 people and forcing about 2,700 to flee.
- The attack is not counted against the government, because the insurgents carried it out. The government's weak protection and its response are counted.
- The deeper problem is impunity. Human Rights Watch says police were involved in killing about 400 post-election protesters, and no credible investigation followed.
- Mozambique scores 21.3 on current evidence, just 1.3 points above the lowest band.
