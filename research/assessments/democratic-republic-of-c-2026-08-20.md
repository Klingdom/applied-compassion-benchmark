## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged the DRC on continuing clashes between M23 and pro-government Wazalendo militia near Bukavu and Kavumu airport in South Kivu through August 2026, keeping populations displaced by M23's earlier capture of Kavumu at renewed risk. The DRC was last assessed on 2026-08-11 and sits near the floor at 2.3 of 100.

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ENTITY-RECORD DEFECT — recorded, not silently fixed.** The published countries index carries this entity as **"Democratic Republic of C"**, a name truncated at 24 characters. The real entity is the **Democratic Republic of the Congo**. This assessment covers the real country. The truncated slug `democratic-republic-of-c` is retained so that linkage to prior assessments, proposals and rotation state is not broken. **The truncation is logged here as an entity-record defect for coordinator repair.** A related mapping flag was resolved on 2026-05-29 (DRC maps to 2.3, not Republic of Congo at 20.3); the display-name truncation is a separate, still-open defect.

**ATTRIBUTION — most documented harm in this window is non-state.** M23 is a Rwandan-backed armed group. Wazalendo are pro-government militia, not regular Congolese armed forces. Neither is scored wholesale against the Congolese state. What is scored against the state is its own conduct: its inability to protect civilians in territory it claims, its reliance on irregular militia as proxies, and its documented rights record as assessed by Human Rights Watch. Rwandan troop presence is foreign-actor conduct and is attributed to Rwanda.

**MIXED DATED/UNDATED SOURCING RESOLVED (gate warning 2).** The scanner listed friendsofthecongo.org as `date_verified: false`. It is **not** relied upon. The August clashes are independently carried by **Critical Threats' Congo War Security Review** editions dated 7 August 2026 and 14 August 2026, which place specific clashes on 1, 2 and 7 August 2026 in named locations (Kabushwa, Buliriko, and villages in the Ziralo group). Two dated sources; the undated Friends of the Congo item is used only as supporting colour.

**Year check.** Critical Threats URL path carries /august-7-2026. Explicit 2026. No year-confusion found.
