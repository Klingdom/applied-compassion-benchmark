## Key Findings

- **LimX Dynamics scores 26.2 of 100. Two of its 40 subdimensions rest on located evidence; the other 38 rest on absence.** No lawsuit, safety incident or regulatory action was located either.
- **Open code is the only thing lifting this score off the floor.** LimX Dynamics publishes working repositories on GitHub and ships TRON 1 with an open software development kit and hardware interface that runs in Python. Research groups can build their own capability instead of depending on the company. That earns 3 of 5 on both Autonomy Preservation and Coalitional Compassion.
- **This is silence by choice, not by constraint.** LimX Dynamics raised about US$400 million in six months and has confidentially filed for a Hong Kong listing. It can afford to publish safety and human-effect data. It does not.
- **A funding round is not a compassion signal.** A US$2.2 billion valuation carries no weight in this framework and none was given.
- **Six of eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor of 25.0.** Only Boundaries and Systemic Thinking rise above it.
