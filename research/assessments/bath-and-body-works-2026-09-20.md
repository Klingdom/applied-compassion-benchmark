## Key Findings

- Why it matters: this is the first time Bath & Body Works has been individually assessed. The published 48.4 is a seed value shared by 83 Fortune 500 companies. The measured 49.4 is one point above it, in the same Functional band.
- Why it matters: the supply-chain standard is real and specific. The Supplier Code of Conduct is built on core ILO conventions, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and UN Global Compact principles 1 to 6, and bars forced, prison, indentured, bonded and trafficked labour.
- Why it matters: the company reports on itself every year. Bath & Body Works has published ESG reports annually since its inaugural edition in April 2023, plus a separate Culture and Inclusion report.
- Why it matters: history is the weak spot. No specific, community-involved acknowledgment of past harm was located, which holds Equity subdimension EQ5 at 2 out of 5.
