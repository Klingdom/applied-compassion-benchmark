## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Microsoft (priority 55, T2) on filings unsealed 17 September 2026 in The New York Times and Daily News litigation against Microsoft and OpenAI. The filings quote a January 2023 internal memo by Microsoft's Director of Applied Science calling AI training on unlicensed content 'the largest theft of labor in human history', and an analysis showing Copilot cut New York Times click-throughs by up to 93 percent. Microsoft was last assessed on 2026-06-25.

## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**THE NETFLIX PRECEDENT, APPLIED (the decisive screen).** Two distinct things surfaced and both fail to reach conduct. First, the allegations: that Microsoft and OpenAI bypassed paywalls, mass-scraped content and stripped copyright notices from training data. These are The New York Times' pleaded contentions in active, undecided litigation. Under the Netflix precedent an allegation is labelled an allegation and is not scored as a finding. No court has ruled. Second, the quoted remark: Dr. Brent Hecht's memo is one employee's characterisation of a practice, surfaced through discovery. A quoted internal remark is not a finding of institutional conduct. Nothing moves.

**WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS SCORABLE.** A judgment or a settlement with admitted facts would make it scorable at tier 5. So would a Microsoft disclosure acknowledging the practice. Neither exists. The 93-percent click-through figure is drawn from a filing by the opposing party and was not independently verified here.

**THE ONE TEMPTING MOVE, AND WHY IT IS DECLINED.** It is tempting to score Anticipatory Awareness (A5) or Correction Willingness (AB2) downward on the reasoning that Microsoft's own senior scientist flagged the harm in January 2023 and the company proceeded. That reasoning requires accepting the plaintiff's framing of what Microsoft then did, which is the contested core of the case. Scoring it would convert an allegation into a finding by inference. Declined.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE (read first).** Published 65.3 (Established, rank 26) sits on a differentiated vector with six of eight dimensions below 4.0, and Microsoft has an assessment history running through 2026-06-25 and a long proposal trail from April and May 2026. No stale-baseline argument is available or used.

**DIRECTIONALITY.** Microsoft surfaced on negative evidence. No upward move was considered. No downward move is supported, for the allegation reasons above.
