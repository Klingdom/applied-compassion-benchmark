## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**Date CONFIRMED, with one refinement.** The amended complaint was filed in a Minnesota federal court on **7 August 2026** and first reported on **13 August 2026** (Bloomberg, Claims Journal, Modern Healthcare). Healthcare Dive's fuller account is dated **17 August 2026**. The scanner's `evidence_date` of 2026-08-13 is correct as the reporting date; the underlying filing date is 7 August 2026. Both are in window. All sources explicit 2026; no year confusion.

**AN INVESTOR SUIT IS AN ALLEGATION, NOT AN ADJUDICATED FINDING — AND IT IS SCORED AS ONE.** This is the central discipline for this entity tonight. Every claim in the amended complaint is unproven:

- that Stephen Hemsley "supported the decision to eliminate an audit program entirely";
- that the programme had found at least $200 million in unsupportable Medicare diagnosis codes;
- that the Change Healthcare integration was rushed such that firewalls, access controls and multifactor authentication were deficient;
- the characterisation "corporate governance failures on a historic scale";
- the assertion that UnitedHealth "defrauded the federal Medicare program, denied medically necessary care to its most vulnerable members".

Healthcare Dive states plainly that these are allegations, not legal findings, and that UnitedHealth declined to comment. **No subdimension is moved on the strength of the complaint alone.** What the filing does establish, and what is scored, is narrower: a serious, particularised governance allegation is now on a federal docket, and UnitedHealth has not answered it publicly.

**Already-priced facts are not re-counted.** The Department of Justice criminal and civil Medicare investigations, the Change Healthcare breach affecting about 190 million people, the AI claim-denial class action and the chief executive transition were all priced in earlier assessments, most recently 2026-07-27. They appear here as standing context.

**NEAR-FLOOR NOTICE (stop-and-report trigger 7).** UnitedHealth sits at 9.4 with **22 of 40 subdimensions at the floor score of 1**. Its capacity to absorb further adverse evidence is limited. Reported as a methodology observation, not a proposal.
