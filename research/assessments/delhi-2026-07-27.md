## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the crackdown that drew two international human rights organisations happened on Delhi’s streets, carried out by Delhi Police. Human Rights Watch reported on 23 July 2026 that on 20 July, security forces used tear gas and batons and shut down mobile internet in central Delhi.
- Delhi police forcibly removed hunger-striking activist Sonam Wangchuk from Jantar Mantar to hospital on 18 July 2026.
- Amnesty International reported on 20 July 2026 that authorities suspended metro services and mobile internet, and that police installed barricades to prevent marchers reaching Parliament.
- Human Rights Watch based its findings on nine witness interviews and 10 verified social media videos. Delhi produced no equivalent public review of the operation.
- The assessed composite of 28.1 is 4.7 points below the published 32.8. That is below the 5-point proposal threshold, so this is a confirmation with a watch flag rather than a downgrade proposal.
