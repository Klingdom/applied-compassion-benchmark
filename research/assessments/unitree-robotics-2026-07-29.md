## Assessment Context

- **Date correction carried forward, not re-imported.** The 2026-07-26 scan mis-dated the Pentagon 1260H designation of Unitree to 20 July 2026. The true date is 8 June 2026. That error is not repeated here; the 1260H designation is treated as pre-window structural context only.
- **In-window event.** The FCC announced on 28-29 July 2026 that imports of new Chinese-made humanoid robots, robot dogs and power inverters are banned on national-security grounds, following a determination by a White House-convened interagency body that the products pose unacceptable risks. The rules took effect immediately and apply to models not yet authorised for sale in the United States. Unitree is named as an affected company.
- **Attribution rule applied.** The import ban is a decision by a foreign regulator about Unitree, not conduct by Unitree. Under the standing attribution rule it does not by itself lower Unitree's score. What it does is corroborate, from an independent state source, the underlying security finding — the previously disclosed undocumented remote-access tunnel on Unitree devices — that was already scored in the 2026-07-26 assessment.
- **Anti-false-positive screen.** No genuinely new Unitree conduct occurred in this window.
