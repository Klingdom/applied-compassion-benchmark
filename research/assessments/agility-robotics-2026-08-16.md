## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Agility Robotics builds a humanoid that does warehouse work, and it publishes nothing about what happens to the workers whose tasks it takes.
- Digit version 5 is being built to sense humans and work without a safety fence alongside warehouse staff. That is a genuine worker-safety design commitment and it is the company’s strongest compassion evidence.
- Digit works commercial shifts at Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada in Ontario and at a GXO warehouse in Flowery Branch, Georgia, and has moved 100,000 totes at one GXO site.
- The IEEE Humanoids 2026 conference has made labour displacement its defining theme. No Agility position on the displacement its own product causes was located.
- This assessment supersedes the 2026-07-22 Agility file, which returned 61.9 and recommended CONFIRM. That file cited a single TechCrunch article for all 40 subdimensions and is not a valid 40-subdimension assessment.

## Strongest Dimensions

Awareness (2.6) and Action (2.6). Both rest on the same two facts: Digit version 5 is designed to sense humans and drop the safety fence, and Digit is in live revenue deployment at Toyota Canada and GXO with 100,000 totes moved at one site. Neither is independently verified.

## Weakest Dimensions

Equity (2.0), Boundaries (2.0) and Accountability (2.0), each with all five subdimensions at the low anchor. Agility publishes no coverage data, no disaggregation, no scope limitation, no failure disclosure and no reparative-action record, and it has taken no public position on the worker displacement its product produces.

## Evidence Gaps

Agility Robotics is a private company with no published responsibility report, no workforce data, no independent audit and no community testimony. Twenty-six of the forty subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure. The strongest positive evidence, the fence-free version 5 design, comes from a single technology outlet reporting a forward-looking product claim about a robot not yet unveiled at the time of writing — it is a stated intention, not a verified shipped capability, and it is scored accordingly. The April 2024 staff reduction is the only internal-culture evidence available and it does not carry a number.
