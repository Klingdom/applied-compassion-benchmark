## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Sudan on drone strikes between 10 and 13 August 2026 that hit Kadugli, El Obeid, Khartoum and parts of Darfur, forcing NGO evacuations and logistics-hub relocations. Sudan was last assessed on 2026-08-11 and sits at the absolute floor of the countries index (0.0 of 100, all eight dimensions at the minimum 1.0).

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ATTRIBUTION CORRECTION — the scanner's framing was incomplete.** The scan summary attributes the August evidence to "RSF-attributed paramilitary drone strikes". That is correct for the 10-13 August wave, but the UN OCHA statement of **3 August 2026** that the scanner also cites rests on a strike carried out by the **Sudanese Armed Forces** — the state's own military. UN News quotes OCHA directly: "At least 35 people were killed and dozens more injured in a drone strike by the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) on a traditional court on Sunday afternoon." Al Jazeera (2026-08-02), Middle East Eye and Sudan Tribune independently report the same SAF-attributed strike on the traditional court at Garra al-Zawaya, North Darfur, with tolls of 35 to 37. This assessment scores the SAF strike as **Sudanese state own-conduct**. The RSF strikes are third-party armed-group conduct and are scored only through the state's response to them.

**Date checks.** The UN News/OCHA item is dated 2026-08-03 (URL path /2026/08/). The Al Jazeera report is dated 2026-08-02. The National's multi-city report is dated 2026-08-12. All are inside the 2026-08-06 to 2026-08-20 window or immediately adjacent to it, and all carry an explicit 2026 year. The scanner listed sudantribune.com/article/317008 as `date_verified: false`; it is not relied upon here. No year-confusion was found.

## Floor limitation

**Sudan is at the arithmetic floor of the scale.** All eight published dimensions are already at 1.0, the minimum anchor value, producing a composite of exactly 0.0 of 100. Every one of the 40 subdimensions is at the low anchor. **Further downgrade is arithmetically unavailable.** No evidence found in this cycle — including a state-military strike on a civilian court and an access collapse across four operational hubs — can be expressed as a score movement.

This is the second consecutive cycle in which this limitation has been recorded for Sudan. The 2026-08-11 cycle noted that the scale cannot distinguish Sudan today from any future deterioration. That finding is **reaffirmed here as a methodology finding, not a score change**. The benchmark's 1-5 anchor scale has no headroom below 1.0, so an entity that reaches the floor becomes insensitive to further evidence. Any remedy belongs at the methodology level (for example, a sub-floor severity annotation carried alongside the score), not in a per-cycle score proposal.

## Key Findings

- Sudan holds at 0 of 100 — the lowest score the benchmark gives. All eight dimensions are already at the minimum, so the scale cannot record any further decline.
- A drone strike carried out by Sudan's own army killed at least 35 people at a traditional court in North Darfur on 2 August 2026. The scanner had framed the month's evidence as paramilitary conduct; part of it is state conduct.
- Aid workers were fully evacuated from Kadugli, the besieged and famine-affected capital of South Kordofan, after strikes between 10 and 13 August 2026.
- Sudan's humanitarian appeal has received about $1.2 billion of the $2.9 billion needed this year — roughly 41 percent.
- This is the second cycle in a row in which the benchmark records that it cannot distinguish Sudan today from any worse Sudan tomorrow. That is a limitation of the scale, not a judgement that things have stopped getting worse.
