## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged India on fresh violence and protests in Manipur on 31 August 2026: two villagers killed in an armed attack, and police firing tear gas at protesters marching on Kakching district headquarters. India was last assessed on 2026-08-11 (assessed 16.3 against a published 15.6, confirmation).

## Evidence-date, lifecycle and attribution checks

**ATTRIBUTION — the scan conflates two separate events with different actors. They are separated here.**

The scan summary presents "two villagers were killed in an armed attack, and police fired tear gas at protesters" as one crisis. They are two distinct events with two distinct actors:

1. **State action — scored.** Manipur police fired tear gas to disperse a torchlight protest march near the Keirak Dam area in Kakching district on 31 August 2026, after some protesters threw stones. **At least seven protesters, including four women, were detained.** This is state conduct and it is scored.
2. **Non-state violence — NOT scored as state action.** Two villagers were killed and one wounded when **suspected militants** opened fire in Kangpokpi district. The perpetrators are unidentified armed actors, not state forces. These deaths are **not** scored against the Indian state. What is scorable is the state's protective failure, which is already reflected in India's existing Awareness and Action scores for Manipur.

**SUBJECT-MATTER CORRECTION.** The scan describes the protest as concerning "the contested delimitation/census process", organised by the "Campaign for Just and Fair Delimitation". Indian outlets consistently report the protest slogan as **"No NRC, No Census"** — the National Register of Citizens, not delimitation alone. The distinction matters because NRC is a citizenship-register question with different rights implications. No scoring turns on it, but the record is corrected.

**SOURCE-QUALITY FINDING.** The scanner's sole source for India was `kmsnews.org` (Kashmir Media Service), a partisan outlet with a standing editorial position on India. **A single partisan source is not an adequate basis for scoring a state.** The events were independently corroborated for this assessment against Business Standard, Deccan Chronicle, India Today NE and Northeast News, all dated 31 August 2026, and the corroborated accounts are what is used.

**DATE CHECK — PASS.** All sources carry 31 August 2026, matching `evidence_date: 2026-08-31`. Explicit 2026 throughout. No year-confusion.

## Key Findings

- Manipur police tear-gassed a protest march in Kakching on 31 August 2026 and detained at least seven people, four of them women. That is state action and the benchmark scores it.
- Two villagers were also killed in Kangpokpi the same period — by suspected militants, not by the state. The benchmark does not score those deaths against India. The scan presented both events as one crisis; they are separated here.
- The protest was against the National Register of Citizens and the Census — the slogan was "No NRC, No Census" — not primarily about delimitation as the scan described.
- The scan's only source for India was Kashmir Media Service, an outlet with a standing editorial position against India. One partisan source is not enough to score a country. The events were re-verified against four independent Indian outlets before anything was scored.
- India confirms at 16.3 of 100, unchanged from the assessment three weeks earlier. Nothing this cycle moves an anchor.

Nothing here is a new finding of that severity, and this assessment does not present it as one.
