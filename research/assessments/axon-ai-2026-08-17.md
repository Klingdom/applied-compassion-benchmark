### Score Difference Analysis

**Action (+15 points scaled) and Systemic Thinking (+10 points scaled) both rose.** The published seed implies generic activity. Axon has a specific ten-year goal to cut gun deaths between police and the public by half by 2033, a national database built to track that number, and a Q2 2026 investor report stating combined fatalities fell nearly 10 percent in 2025. That is upstream work with published measurement, which is anchor 3 on Efficacy, Resource Mobilization, Follow-Through, Root Cause Orientation and Long-Term Impact.

**Boundaries (-12.5 points scaled) and Integrity (-12.5 points scaled) both fell.** The Electronic Frontier Foundation, using public records from dozens of police agencies, found Draft One erases the initial AI draft on export. If a report contains bias, an error or a lie, there is no record of whether the officer or the AI produced it. Asked how agencies could audit this, an Axon representative said it 'wasn't a scenario we anticipated needing to make easy'. That is a documented design decision, not an absence of disclosure.

**Awareness (-2.5 points scaled).** Axon had an AI Ethics Board. Nine of its twelve members resigned on 6 June 2022 after the company announced Taser-equipped drones for schools, against the board's advice. A blind-spot mechanism that is overridden scores worse than one that never existed, because the company knew.

**Empathy and Equity (-12.5 and -6.25 points scaled).** Reuters documented more than 1,000 deaths after police Taser use through 2018, with over half in identified vulnerable groups and at least 32 percent of the dead being Black. Reuters also documented a company operation to place vendor-paid experts first into those investigations. That evidence closes in 2018, outside the currency window, and was scored at the absence anchor of 2 rather than the floor.

### Recommendation

**The published 35.9 is overstated and should be revised down to 30.6. The band does not change.** This is not a pure calibration correction: specific, independently documented design and governance decisions drive it. The entity name should also be corrected — see the corporate-status note below.

## Key Findings

- **ENTITY-NAMING DEFECT: the index publishes 'Axon AI', which is not an operating entity.** The company is Axon Enterprise, Inc., listed on Nasdaq as AXON. 'Axon AI' is a product family. All scoring was performed against Axon Enterprise, Inc.
- **Axon publishes a number that embarrasses it.** Its Moonshot goal commits to halving gun deaths between police and the public by 2033 and it built a national database to count them. Independent analysts at IPVM call the plan vague, but the count is public.
- **An independent investigation found the AI police-report tool destroys its own audit trail.** The Electronic Frontier Foundation reported in July 2025 that Draft One erases the initial AI draft on export, leaving no record of what the AI wrote versus what the officer wrote.
- **Axon overrode its own ethics board.** Nine of twelve AI Ethics Board members resigned on 6 June 2022 after the company announced Taser-equipped drones for schools, a proposal the board had advised against.
- **Reuters documented more than 1,000 deaths after police Taser use through 2018.** More than half of the dead fell into an identified vulnerable group. That evidence is more than five years old and was scored conservatively at the absence anchor, not as a current finding.

## Strongest Dimensions

**Action (2.6) and Systemic Thinking (2.6)** are the strongest, and both rest on the Moonshot programme: a specific ten-year goal, a public dataset, published interim results and convened roundtables with police leaders, academics and community representatives.

## Weakest Dimensions

**Empathy (2.0), Equity (2.0), Boundaries (2.0) and Integrity (2.0)** all sit at the absence baseline. Boundaries and Integrity are the more serious of the four, because they are held down by documented conduct rather than by silence: the Draft One audit design and the ethics board resignation.

## Evidence Gaps

No workforce wellbeing, turnover or internal culture data was located in Axon's filings or its 2025 Responsibility Report. No disaggregated review by Axon of the racial disparity Reuters documented was located. The Reuters database closes at the end of 2018; no equivalent current dataset was located, so the most serious harm evidence about this company is more than five years old.

## Scoring Convention Applied

**Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is reserved for positive documented evidence of a specific failure. This is the majority convention across the three most recent de-seeding studies and was applied identically to all seven labs in this study. **Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** Where this company does not publish, the low anchor was scored and said so explicitly. A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review; a score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.
