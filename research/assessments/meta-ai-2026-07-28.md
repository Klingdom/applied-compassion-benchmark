## Key Findings

- Why it matters: an artificial-intelligence system built for internal productivity was used to help decide who lost their job, and no one has shown it was tested for fairness first. Twenty-six Meta workers sued on 14 July 2026 over the Metamate system; a judge declined to halt the layoffs on 17 July 2026 while flagging serious questions.
- This is the first US lawsuit to target a major company's internal artificial-intelligence system for discriminatory employment decisions.
- Meta AI is scored here on system design and deployment, not on the employment decision. Meta Platforms is scored separately on the employment decision.
- No pre-deployment fairness assessment, no published evaluation, and no bias audit could be found for the scoring system.
- Assessed composite 24.4 against a published 26.3. Delta -1.9, below the 5-point proposal threshold.
