## Key Findings

- Why it matters: a court had already said this data could not be used for immigration enforcement, and it was used for immigration enforcement anyway. Court filings made public on 17 July 2026 show Immigration and Customs Enforcement forwarded improperly obtained Medicaid data to Palantir.
- Palantir operates an application called ELITE that ICE agents use to display the addresses of noncitizens who may be subject to deportation. The Medicaid enrolment data on millions of Americans was used in it.
- The data reached ICE from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services in January 2026. The disclosure came in a motion filed by more than 20 Democratic state attorneys general suing over the data-sharing agreement.
- Asked what was done to ensure contractors had purged the data, defendants said it had been shared over a Microsoft Teams chat and that the shared data was deleted from the chat.
- Palantir AI sits at the published floor of 0 out of 100 — the lowest score on the AI labs index. A floor entity cannot move lower, so this is a confirmation at 0 with a sustained watch flag.
