## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Paris's published 62.5 has never been measured. This first measurement puts the City of Paris at 40.6 of 100 (Functional), 21.9 points lower.
- The City counts its homeless population every year. On 22 January 2026 it found 3,858 people with no shelter, the highest figure since 2018 and 10% more than in 2025 (Ville de Paris).
- The City gets credit for acting on that count. It added 1,000 shelter places since 2023, sheltered 4,500 people in March 2026, and housed 253 people in its own gyms after a State eviction in August 2026.
- The October 2026 curfew was ordered by the national police authority, not the City, so it is scored under France.
- 15 of 40 subdimensions have no usable evidence and are scored at 2. Scored at 3 instead, Paris would be 50.0, still Functional and still below the published band.
