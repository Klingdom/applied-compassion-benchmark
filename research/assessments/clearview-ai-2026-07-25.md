## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the one repair Clearview AI had on the table has been taken off it. On 13 July 2026 the Seventh Circuit vacated a lower court's approval of the company's biometric-privacy class settlement, reopening the case.
- The court objected to unequal treatment inside the class. Illinois plaintiffs would have received 10 shares, plaintiffs in New York, California and Virginia 5 shares, and everyone else just 1 share.
- The court told the district court to weigh whether class representatives had protected the class, as opposed to the attorneys' interest in settling the litigation.
- The practical result is that no repair is in place for the people whose faces were scraped. Reparative action stays at the floor and the critical-band score is confirmed.
