## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the conduct that flagged Guatemala City was not the city’s. Inter Press Service reported on 24 July 2026 on Guatemala’s captured national justice system, describing a state long in the hands of what Guatemalans call the pact of the corrupt.
- The report centres on the prosecution of journalist and elPeriódico founder José Rubén Zamora, who spent 1,295 days in detention before house arrest on 12 February 2026. Six United Nations experts warned his treatment could amount to torture.
- Over 100 people, including around 50 judges and prosecutors, have fled Guatemala into exile rather than face fabricated charges.
- The attribution rule applies. Judicial persecution is national conduct by the Public Ministry and the courts, not conduct by the municipal government of Guatemala City.
- The assessed composite of 18.1 is 0.7 points below the published 18.8. This is a confirmation with a watch flag on the national environment.
