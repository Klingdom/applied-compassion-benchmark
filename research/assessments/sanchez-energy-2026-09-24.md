## The finding

Sanchez Energy Corporation **no longer exists under that name or as a public company.**

- Sanchez Energy filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2019.
- It exited Chapter 11 in 2020 as a **privately held company renamed Mesquite Energy**, having shed roughly $2.3 billion of debt.
- Founding chief executive Tony Sanchez left the company; the chief financial officer was named interim chief executive.
- Subsequent litigation, including a $2 million settlement with the Sanchez family and Fifth Circuit appeals, concerns the bankruptcy estate rather than an operating public company.
