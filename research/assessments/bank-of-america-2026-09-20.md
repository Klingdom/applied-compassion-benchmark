## Key Findings

- Why it matters: this is the first time Bank of America has been individually assessed. The published 67.2 was a seed value. The measured 64.4 is 2.8 points below it and stays in the Established band.
- Why it matters: the bank moves real money into underserved places. It has committed more than $1 billion to community development finance institutions across the United States.
- Why it matters: it also has a repeat consumer-harm record. The CFPB fined it $225 million in 2022 over unemployment-benefit disbursement and ordered about $250 million in 2023 over junk fees, withheld credit-card rewards and accounts customers never asked for.
- Why it matters: a regulator said the fix worked. The CFPB terminated the Bank of America consent order three years early.
- Why it matters: staff are under pressure. The bank filed a mass-layoff notice in Elma, New York in March 2026, a WARN Act investigation followed, and the chief executive expects headcount to fall as the bank adopts artificial intelligence.
