## Delta will pay $78.7 million for the 2020 fuel dump over Los Angeles schools, and still denies it did anything wrong

On 28 September 2026 a federal judge tentatively approved a $78.7 million class settlement over the January 2020 Delta Air Lines fuel dump. The jet fuel fell on homes and schools in Los Angeles and Orange counties. The class is described as nearly 13,000 property owners and residents; after fees, about $51.2 million would reach them (MyNewsLA, 2026-09-28). The scanner's figure of 38,000 was not found in the fetched article and is not used.

The payment is repair. It came through a court case more than six years after the event, and Delta still denies wrongdoing, blaming a mechanical issue. Harm Acknowledgment (AB1) moves from 3 to 2: harm here was addressed only after outside legal pressure. Reparative Action (AB5) stays at 3. Delta measures 61.3 (Established), 1.2 below the published 62.5. No proposal.
