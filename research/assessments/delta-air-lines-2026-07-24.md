## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the event that flagged Delta did not happen in 2026. The $8.1 million False Claims Act settlement over Payroll Support Program compensation caps was announced in July 2025.
- No material harm evidence was found inside the 10-24 July 2026 window. The most recent labour disputes on record — a settled retaliation claim and the AFA-CWA organising drive — date from 2025.
- The unresolved tension is labour voice. AFA-CWA is organising 28,000 Delta flight attendants and says Delta runs an anti-union website, while Delta pays sector-leading profit sharing to the same workforce.
- The assessed score of 61.9 is 0.6 points below the published 62.5, so the established-band score is confirmed.
