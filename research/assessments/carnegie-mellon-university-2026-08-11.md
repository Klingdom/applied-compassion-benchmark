## Key Findings

- Carnegie Mellon is investigating ten students for painting a fence built for painting. The Fence is a century-old designated free-expression structure. Nine of the ten investigated students were barred from campus while the investigation runs, and one faces possible expulsion.
- Eight organisations, including Jewish groups and the main faculty association, told CMU to stop. Signatories to the joint condemnation include Jewish Voice for Peace, the American Association of University Professors, the Sunrise Movement and Palestine Legal.
- The university expanded the investigation after the criticism, not before it. It went from eight students investigated and seven restricted on 13 July 2026 to ten investigated and nine restricted by 29 July 2026.
- The benchmark already scored this two weeks ago, and is not scoring it twice. An open proposal from 29 July 2026 covers this exact event. Tonight's independent re-scoring lands on the identical composite of 43.7, which confirms that proposal rather than adding a second one.
- Punishment came before any finding of responsibility. Nine students lost campus access while still under investigation.
