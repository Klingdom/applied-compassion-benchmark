## Key Findings

- Why it matters: a federal jury decided this, so it is a finding rather than an allegation. On 22 July 2026 a jury in Portland, Oregon found that Nike discriminated against former engineer Heather Hender on the basis of gender.
- The jury found Nike failed to pay Hender at a level comparable to her male colleagues, and promoted her more slowly in part because of her gender.
- The award was about 19,730 dollars in compensatory damages plus at least 7.5 million dollars in punitive damages. The punitive figure exceeded the 2 million dollars her lawyers had asked for.
- The case began in 2018 when Hender and three other employees sued over systemic sex discrimination in pay and promotion. The other three settled before trial, and Nike contested this claim through a six-day trial rather than settling.
