### Score Difference Analysis

The interesting thing about Sarcos is that its abandoned product was the most obviously compassion-relevant thing in this entire study. The Guardian XO exoskeleton existed to stop workers destroying their backs. That is upstream injury prevention, exactly what the Systemic Thinking anchors reward. The company stopped selling it in November 2023 for commercial reasons and became a software business. Under the Integrity anchors that is a commitment abandoned under financial pressure, scored 1. Against that, the company said so publicly rather than quietly, which earns 3 on Scope Clarity. The rest of the record is a listed company that discloses what securities law requires and nothing else. The fall of 10.9 points is modest because the seed was already low.

### Recommendation

The published score is overstated and should be revised down to 25.0. More importantly the record should be renamed "Palladyne AI Corp. (formerly Sarcos)" and its sector corrected from robotics hardware to autonomy software, regardless of any score decision.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: the index scores a robot maker that stopped making robots.** Sarcos suspended commercialization of hardware robotics products in November 2023 and renamed itself Palladyne AI Corp. in March 2024.
- **The abandoned product was the compassion case.** The Guardian XO powered exoskeleton existed to stop workers being injured lifting heavy loads. That line was dropped under financial pressure.
- **The company said so publicly.** It announced the suspension rather than quietly discontinuing the line. Publicly narrowing a promise is honest, and it is the only score above the baseline in this assessment.
- **Its Nasdaq ticker changed from STRC to PDYN on 8 April 2024** — over two years before this assessment.
- **Roughly 30 of the 40 scores rest on absence of disclosure.**

## Scoring Conventions Applied

- **Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is used only where positive documented evidence of a specific failure exists. This is the "2-convention", the more conservative of the two conventions live in this repository.
- **A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review. A score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.**
- 38 of 40 subdimensions sit at 2. Of those, approximately 30 rest on absence of disclosure rather than on located evidence of a practice.
