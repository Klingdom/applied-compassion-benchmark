## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Keenon Robotics does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index.

**Corporate status check (required before assessment).** Keenon Robotics Co., Ltd. is operating under that name, headquartered in Shanghai and founded in 2010. It reports more than 60,000 service robots shipped across 600-plus cities, about $139 million in annual recurring revenue and a valuation around $1 billion. IDC — an independent market research firm — ranked it first in the global commercial service robot market in July 2025, and it launched a C55 cleaning robot at the ISSA North America Expo ([Robotics & Automation News, IDC ranking](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2025/07/21/keenon-robotics-declared-leader-in-commercial-service-robot-market-by-idc/93232/); [Getlatka, Keenon revenue](https://getlatka.com/companies/keenon.com)). No acquisition, rename or insolvency event was found.

**⚠️ This score is overwhelmingly a measure of disclosure, not of conduct.** Repeated searches found **no sustainability report, no ESG report, no ISO management-system certification disclosure and no independent corporate social responsibility audit** for Keenon Robotics. No adverse conduct finding was located either — no lawsuit, safety incident, labour ruling or regulatory action. Thirty-eight of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.

**Disclosure-regime caveat.** Keenon is headquartered in a jurisdiction that does not require the social reporting this benchmark reads. Part of the drag on transparency-dependent subdimensions is structural rather than behavioural.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **38 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 2 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **Keenon Robotics has shipped more than 60,000 robots into hospitals, care homes and restaurants across 600-plus cities, and publishes essentially nothing a compassion benchmark can read.** Thirty-eight of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure. **That is a finding about disclosure, not about conduct** — no lawsuit, safety incident, labour ruling or regulatory action was located either.
- **The one genuine positive is product differentiation by setting.** Keenon builds a hospital robot with clean-side and dirty-side workflow separation, a separate food-service line, and a dedicated senior-living partnership. Building differently for a hospital than for a restaurant is real contextual sensitivity.
- **No sustainability report, no ESG report, no independent audit was found.** Repeated searches returned product launches, design awards and market-share rankings only.
- **Robots operate around patients and care-home residents who did not choose them, and no consent or data-handling disclosure exists.** Consent Orientation scores 2 of 5.
- **Seven of eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor.** This is close to the structural minimum for an operating company with no located adverse conduct.

## Strongest Dimensions
- **Awareness (35.0)** — the only dimension above the floor, on setting-specific product design and EU conformity.

## Weakest Dimensions
- **Seven dimensions tied at 25.0** — Empathy, Action, Equity, Boundaries, Accountability, Systemic Thinking and Integrity, all at the absence floor.

## Evidence Gaps
- No social, ESG or sustainability reporting of any kind.
- No outcome data on effects on patients, residents or staff.
- No consent or data-governance disclosure for cameras and sensors in care settings.
- No employment-effects analysis for the service and care workers whose tasks the robots absorb.
