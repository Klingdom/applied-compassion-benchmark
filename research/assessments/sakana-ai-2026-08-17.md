- Sakana AI's flagship claim did not survive independent review. A peer-reviewed evaluation in ACM SIGIR Forum found its 'AI Scientist' has a 42% experimental failure rate and produces manuscripts with structural errors and misleading claims. IEEE Spectrum reported that critics worry about unfounded hype.
- The company's best compassion evidence is its deal with the nonprofit Current AI to build a shared open-source AI stack for the Japanese language and for Global South communities that dominant AI systems ignore. That is resource sharing with no commercial return.
- In 2026 Sakana also partnered with DEEP DIVE on AI-powered information analysis, a push into defence and intelligence. Open-science positioning and defence work have not been publicly reconciled.
- Nothing was located showing Sakana acknowledged or corrected the failure rate that outside researchers documented. Accountability is the lowest dimension at 2.0.
- The score falls 27.8 points from the published 60.9. Twenty-six of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.
