## South Africa's police told refugees they could not open cases as an anti-migrant 'deadline' approached

An anti-migrant movement, March and March, set 30 September 2026 as a 'deadline' for undocumented foreigners to leave South Africa. Its earlier 30 June deadline led more than 160,000 people to flee, according to CNN. On 28 September 2026 Al Jazeera reported refugees in Durban saying "Police said we can't open cases because we are foreigners." Police said they had no plans to change their normal policing approach for the deadline. Between 14 June and 20 August authorities processed 86,596 foreign nationals for deportation.

The state did act. In June it deployed soldiers to support police in Johannesburg's Hillbrow, and more than 900 people were arrested during nationwide protests. President Cyril Ramaphosa publicly challenged the claim that migrants cause the country's problems. South Africa also declared gender-based violence and femicide a national disaster in November 2025 and extended the Social Relief of Distress grant (R370 a month, about 8 million people) to March 2027.

This is the first scoring of South Africa at the level of all 40 subdimensions. It measures 41.3 (Functional), 8.7 below the published 50.0. That is not filed as a downgrade. The May 2026 baseline confirmed 50.0 on most of the same evidence; only about 2.5 points of the gap come from new conduct. The difference is sent to the coordinator as a calibration question.
