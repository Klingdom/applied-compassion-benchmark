## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Honduras evicted a community from land that a regional court had already ruled was theirs. On 6 July 2026 more than 200 police entered the Garifuna community of San Juan in Tela Bay using tear gas, threats and live ammunition.
- Five community leaders were arrested. They were released within hours after pressure from Garifuna organisations, but they remain criminally charged.
- At the 15 July 2026 initial hearing a judge sent the case to the National Jurisdiction Court in San Pedro Sula, the court used for organised crime. Community advocates call the reclassification racist.
- The eviction breached the 2023 Inter-American Court of Human Rights judgment recognising the San Juan Garifuna people's collective ownership and ancestral rights.
