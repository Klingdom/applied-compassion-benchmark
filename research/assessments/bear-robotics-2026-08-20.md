## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Bear Robotics does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index. It is listed as a named business unit under §R-SUB-2 because its parent, LG Electronics, holds no published composite anywhere in the benchmark.

**Corporate status check — flagged.** Bear Robotics is operating under its own name, with its own product line and public communications. **LG Electronics exercised a call option on 22 January 2025 to take a controlling 51% stake**, and stated an intention to integrate Bear into its CLOi commercial robot line ([LG Global, majority stake announcement](https://www.lg.com/global/newsroom/news/corporate/lg-acquires-majority-stake-in-bear-robotics-to-bolster-robotics-capabilities/); [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/lg-acquires-majority-stake-in-bear-robotics/)). The company continued to launch products independently in 2026, including Servi Q in May 2026. **Re-verify next cycle:** if the brand is absorbed into CLOi and independent disclosure ceases, this record must be re-scoped under §2.4.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **39 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 1 rests on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **Bear Robotics has Servi robots working in more than 25,000 restaurants and publishes nothing about what that does to the people working alongside them.** Thirty-nine of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure. **No adverse finding of any kind was located either.**
- **The company says Servi "assists staff, not replaces them" and offers no evidence for it.** No staffing data, no worker survey, no independent study was located. That claim sits in marketing and nowhere else, which is why Non-Performance and Interconnection Awareness both score 2 of 5.
- **The one real positive is access design.** Monthly subscription pricing from about $999 lets small independent restaurants adopt the robot without capital, and the robot is built to move predictably around wheelchairs and across ADA ramps. Two barriers, evidenced — though the sources are company and reseller material.
- **LG Electronics owns 51% and plans to fold Bear into its CLOi line.** If independent disclosure stops, this record must be re-scoped. Flag it for the next cycle.
- **Seven of eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor**, giving a composite 0.6 points above the structural minimum of 25.0.
