Bloomberg reported on 12 December 2025 that it had selected banks for a 2026 Hong Kong initial public offering. Reported pre-listing restructuring by a named financial newswire is a dated financial source of the required class. No acquisition, insolvency or wind-down was located. **A funding round is not a compassion signal, and none of the above is scored as one.**

It publishes none that could be located.

## Key Findings

- **Galbot scores 25.0 of 100 — the lowest possible score under this methodology when no adverse conduct is found.** All eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor. That means the company publishes nothing this benchmark can read about its effects on people, and that no lawsuit, safety incident or regulatory action was located either.
- **Galbot's robots hand medication to members of the public, and no safety data about that was found.** The G1 works in more than ten unstaffed pharmacies in Beijing, sorting and dispensing drugs 24 hours a day. No error rate, no incident reporting process and no safety certification were located.
- **This is silence by choice, not by constraint.** Galbot raised about US$362 million in March 2026 and is preparing a Hong Kong listing. It can afford to publish. The small accessibility companies elsewhere in this batch cannot, and that difference should be read into the identical scores.
- **A funding round is not a compassion signal.** Galbot is the highest-valued unlisted humanoid company in the world. Valuation carries no weight in this framework and none was given.
- **A Hong Kong listing would change this score's evidence base.** Listed companies must report under the HKEX Environmental, Social and Governance Code. Re-assess after any listing.
