## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. CMR Surgical Ltd does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index.

**Corporate status check (required before assessment).** CMR Surgical Ltd is operating under that name, headquartered in Cambridge, United Kingdom. It announced in March 2026 that more than 45,000 patients worldwide had been treated using Versius, received US Food and Drug Administration 510(k) clearance for the Versius Plus system in December 2025, and submitted a further 510(k) application for gynaecology indications on 29 April 2026 ([CMR Surgical, Versius Plus 510(k) clearance](https://us.cmrsurgical.com/news/cmr-surgical-secures-510k-clearance-of-its-versius-plus-robotic-surgical-system); [CMR Surgical, gynaecology 510(k) submission](https://us.cmrsurgical.com/news/cmr-surgical-submits-510k-application-to-us-fda-to-expand-versius-plus-into-gynecology)). **Flag:** trade reporting indicates CMR is weighing strategic options including a full or partial sale at a reported valuation around $4 billion. No transaction has been announced. Re-verify before the next publication cycle.

**Evidence base.** This is the second-best-evidenced entity in this batch. It rests on FDA regulatory decisions (tier 5), a registered prospective post-market clinical study on ClinicalTrials.gov (tier 5), **peer-reviewed evaluations published under the IDEAL framework** including a registry-based stage 2b urology evaluation and a stage 1/2a first-in-human series (tier 4–5), and an independent NHS real-world comparative study of Versius against da Vinci Xi (tier 4).

## Key Findings

- **CMR Surgical publishes numbers that could hurt it, and that is why it scores where it does.** A registry-based evaluation of Versius urology procedures reports that 11.4% of patients had a complication within 30 days. Most robotics companies in this batch publish no outcome data at all.
- **CMR runs its device through a recognised staged evaluation framework called IDEAL** — first-in-human study, then registry evaluation, then a registered post-market study. That is a published plan for finding out whether the device harms people, not just whether it sells.
- **An independent NHS study compared Versius against the market leader.** Third-party comparison is rare in this field and is the strongest evidence class this benchmark recognises short of an audit.
- **The access case is real but partly commercial.** Versius is built modular and portable so hospitals without a dedicated robotic theatre can adopt it, and it is deployed in India and across Europe. That removes two genuine barriers. It is also how a challenger competes with an incumbent.
- **CMR may be sold.** Trade reporting says the company is weighing a full or partial sale at around $4 billion. Nothing has been announced. This record should be re-verified before the next publication cycle.
