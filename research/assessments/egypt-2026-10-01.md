## Six investigative journalists arrested in two days; the score barely moves because most of the relevant measures were already at the bottom

Egyptian security forces raided homes and arrested six journalists from the investigative outlet Matsda2sh on 28-29 September 2026, according to the Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ, published 2026-09-30). No reasons were disclosed, and phones and laptops were seized. This is new, in-window conduct by the state itself.

The arrests bear on four subdimensions: Signal Amplification (A4), Transparency (AB3), Consistency Under Pressure (I1) and Values Alignment (I4). Three of the four were already at the lowest anchor (1) in the July 2026 assessment. Only Transparency had room to fall. It moves from 2 to 1, because the state is now silencing the independent reporting that exposes its failures. Egypt moves from 21.9 to 21.3, still in the Developing band. No proposal is filed.

Two items surfaced by search were excluded as misdated: the Alaa Abdel Fattah pardon (true date 2025-09-22, ledger `egypt-alaa-abdel-fattah-pardon`) and the Ismail al-Iskandrani checkpoint arrest (true date 2025, ledger `egypt-ismail-al-iskandrani-arrest`). A search summary also attached "5 September 2026" to the Sarah Khalifa death sentence; the date could not be verified from a primary source and was not used.
