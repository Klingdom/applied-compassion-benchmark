**Corporate status check — operating.** Distalmotion SA is operating. Operating status was confirmed from the US Food and Drug Administration's regulatory record as reported by dated third-party trade press rather than by the company: FDA De Novo marketing authorisation for the Dexter system, a 510(k) clearance for hysterectomy in October 2025, an expanded gynaecology clearance in June 2026, and a further 510(k) for ventral hernia repair in August 2026. ## Key Findings

- **Distalmotion SA scores 26.9 of 100 — third in this batch — on the strength of one thing: it built its robot around the cost and space barriers that keep smaller surgical centres out of robotic surgery.** Independent trade reporting confirms the design intent and the target market.
- **The affordability claim is real in design and unevidenced in outcome.** Distalmotion publishes no price for Dexter and no data on how many patients reached robotic surgery who otherwise would not have. Access Design scores 3 of 5, not 4, for exactly that reason.
- **The regulatory record is the only independent evidence available.** FDA De Novo authorisation, a hysterectomy clearance in October 2025, a gynaecology expansion in June 2026 and a ventral hernia clearance in August 2026, plus almost 3,000 patients treated. No adverse regulatory finding was located.
- **Thirty-seven of 40 subdimensions rest on absence.** Distalmotion is a private Swiss company with no duty to publish a social report, and it publishes none. This is a disclosure finding, not a conduct finding.
- **No harm was located.** No lawsuit, recall, warning letter or safety incident against Distalmotion was found.
