### Recommendation

**CONFIRM**. The composite delta of -1.5 points is inside the 5-point confirmation threshold, so the published score of 32.8 is confirmed.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the border-war evidence that flagged Thailand this cycle was mis-dated. The fighting that killed at least 38 people and displaced about 300,000 happened in July 2025, not July 2026, and Human Rights Watch dates it to 2025 in its World Report 2026.
- The real July 2026 evidence is different and narrower. On 13 July 2026 Human Rights Watch reported that Thailand was holding four UNHCR-recognised Chinese refugees at Suan Phlu Immigration Detention Center under pressure from Beijing.
- That conduct fits the score Thailand already has. Thailand runs strong universal health coverage but has weak protection for refugees and political speech, which is what a 32.8 developing-band score describes.
- The assessed score of 31.3 is 1.5 points below the published 32.8, well inside the 5-point confirmation threshold.
