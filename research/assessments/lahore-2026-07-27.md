## Key Findings

- Why it matters: nearly all of the deaths that put Pakistan in the news were not in Lahore. The Washington Times reported on 27 July 2026 that 109 people had died nationwide since 26 June 2026, with nearly half in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, several hundred kilometres from Lahore.
- What is attributable to Lahore is the drainage failure. Heavy monsoon rain inundated more than 130 locations in the city from around 20 July 2026, and sinkholes opened on Canal Road and Johar Town Main Boulevard.
- Punjab’s Home Department imposed Section 144 restrictions province-wide for 60 days, banning swimming in dams, rivers and canals, unauthorised boating, and bathing in accumulated rainwater.
- Residents publicly questioned government flood preparedness. Officials discussed plans to issue timely warnings to communities living along rivers so residents can move to safer areas.
- The assessed composite of 17.5 is 1.3 points below the published 18.8. That is a confirmation, not a downgrade.
