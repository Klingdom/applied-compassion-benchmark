## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Gabon's placeholder score turns out to be roughly right, and that is itself a finding. Gabon assesses at 25.0 against a published 20.3, a gap of 4.7 points, which stays inside the Developing band and falls below the threshold for filing a change proposal.
- Why it matters: Gabon actually insures most of its people. The national health insurance fund covers 76 per cent of the population and two thirds of its members are identified as living in poverty, which is real targeted coverage rather than a stated intention.
- Why it matters: that system nearly broke and the state moved to fix it. Pharmacy managers suspended services in 2024 after months of unpaid bills. The government set up a restructuring Task-Force in July 2026, announced a digital audit in August 2026, and announced a new fund for self-employed workers.
- Why it matters: the transition was real and it is now eroding. Gabon ran an inclusive national dialogue, a constitutional referendum and an election with over 70 per cent turnout. Then it arrested the main opposition leader on 16 April 2026 and kept him in detention when his appeal failed on 3 June 2026.
- Why it matters: accountability went upward for once. A court sentenced former first lady Sylvia Bongo and her son to 20 years for embezzlement on 12 November 2025, though the trial lasted two days and ran in absentia.

## Strongest Dimensions

Systemic Thinking (2.4), Action (2.2) and Equity (2.2). Gabon has a funded national health insurance scheme with a dedicated component for the poor, and it has moved publicly to repair that scheme rather than let it fail quietly.

## Weakest Dimensions

Empathy (1.6) and Boundaries (1.8). Discrimination against immigrants and ethnic minorities is documented, the main opposition leader is in pre-trial detention, and patients were turned away with no alternative when pharmacies stopped serving insurance holders.

## Evidence Gaps

- Two of the strongest positive items, the Fund 4 announcement and the July 2026 restructuring Task-Force, rest on tier-2 regional outlets. Confidence on Accountability and Action is medium as a result.
- The Amnesty International Gabon country page returned HTTP 504 and could not be read.
- No independent evaluation of Gabonese health insurance outcomes was located, so Efficacy is scored on the existence of an announced audit rather than on results.
