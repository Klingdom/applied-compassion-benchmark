## Key Findings

- Why it matters: this is the first US lawsuit saying a big company's own artificial-intelligence system picked who to fire, and it picked people who were out sick. Twenty-six current and former Meta employees sued on 14 July 2026; a federal judge declined to stop the layoffs on 17 July 2026 but said the case raises serious questions.
- The layoff round itself, roughly 8,000 people, is already priced into the published score. It was applied on 26 May 2026. It is not counted twice here.
- What is new is the allegation about how people were selected: productivity and activity metrics applied to workers on medical, parental and family leave.
- The claims are allegations, not findings. A pre-adjudication screen was applied, so movement is deliberately small.
- Assessed composite 6.3 against a published 7.8. Delta -1.5, below the 5-point proposal threshold.
