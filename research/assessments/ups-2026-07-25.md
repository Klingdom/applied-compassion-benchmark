## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the case that flagged UPS had already closed. Reporting on 13 July 2026 was a retrospective on how a former driver's 238 million dollar jury award was cut down, and the parties settled in May 2026.
- The trial judge vacated the 198 million dollars in punitive damages and ordered a new trial, saying the jury's punitive award was not reasonable on the evidence. The Ninth Circuit upheld that, which also eliminated the remaining 39.4 million dollars.
- UPS settled on undisclosed terms. There is no published admission, no disclosed amount and no systemic remedy, so reparative action scores low.
- UPS is separately cutting about 30,000 operational roles and closing 22 facilities in 2026, but is doing so through voluntary buyouts worth 150,000 dollars before tax, which supports the boundaries score.
