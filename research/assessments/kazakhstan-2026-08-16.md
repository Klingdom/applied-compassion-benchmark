## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Kazakhstan's placeholder is close to right, and slightly low. It assesses at 24.4 against a published 20.3, a gain of 4.1 points that stays inside the Developing band and falls below the threshold for filing a change proposal.
- Why it matters: over a million people are about to get health cover they could not pay for. From 2026, local and regional budgets in Kazakhstan will pay compulsory medical insurance contributions for more than one million unemployed and vulnerable citizens.
- Why it matters: 238 people died in 2022 and six officers got three years each. A court convicted six police officers of torturing detainees on 17 January, but Human Rights Watch says the government has still not conducted a comprehensive investigation into the 238 deaths.
- Why it matters: the state attacks the people who count its failures. In April the President accused human rights groups and journalists of organising provocations, in May the Ombudsman called the Coalition Against Torture's UN submissions biased, and officials pushed for anti-NGO legislation.
- Why it matters: an abuse survivor was the one who went to prison. Elvira Erkebayeva was sentenced to three years restricted freedom on 25 August, and domestic violence is still not a standalone criminal offence.

## Strongest Dimensions

Action (2.4), Equity (2.2) and Systemic Thinking (2.2). Kazakhstan funds and is expanding a compulsory health insurance system, is raising health spending to 2.73 trillion tenge in 2026, and is paying contributions for over a million people who cannot.

## Weakest Dimensions

Accountability (1.6) and Empathy (1.6). No comprehensive investigation into the 238 deaths of January 2022, no repair for the families, a codified ban on so-called propaganda, and an abuse survivor sentenced rather than protected.

- The Toregozhina fine is used only as standing 2025 context at its true event date, not as a new finding.
- Health reform sources are tier-3 national and regional outlets rather than an independent evaluation, so Action and Equity carry medium confidence.

## Attribution Discipline

Where such harm occurred, what is measured is the state's own response to it. Every source date was checked against its event date, and publication dates were not treated as event dates.
