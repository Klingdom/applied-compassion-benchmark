## Key Findings

- Why it matters: an opposition presidential candidate was abducted and nearly killed. Lassy Mbouity was seized in Brazzaville by armed masked men on 11 May and found in critical condition on 20 May.
- Why it matters: police shot people at a detention site the state was not supposed to be running. Six people were reportedly killed and eight hospitalised on 23 May at the CNSS Texaco la Tsiemé facility, which civil society organisations describe as unlawful and inhumane.
- Why it matters: the election was decided before it happened. Denis Sassou Nguesso won a fifth term with 95 per cent of the vote on 15 March 2026, with the main opposition boycotting, activists arrested beforehand and the internet cut nationwide on polling day.
- Why it matters: oil money is growing and poverty is growing with it. Poverty edged up to 51.7 per cent at the $3.00 a day line, and independent assessment finds education and health conditions have stagnated.
- Why it matters: nobody has answered for 2016. Civil society groups estimate 15,000 people died and around 100,000 were displaced after the military assault on the Pool region, and no acknowledgement or repair has followed.
