## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Engineered Arts is the only lab in this cluster whose highest-scoring dimension is Integrity, and the reason is a refusal it has held for twenty years.
- Founder Will Jackson has positioned the company the same way since October 2004 — robots for communication and entertainment, explicitly not labour — and the company states Ameca is not meant to replace humans.
- That position has cost the company. It has held it for twenty years while the humanoid sector raised billions on the opposite promise, including through a December 2024 venture round.
- Twenty-nine Ameca units were deployed worldwide as of 2025, in museums, science centres, research labs and universities rather than in workplaces.
- Engineered Arts publishes no responsibility report, no workforce data and no failure disclosure. Its entire Accountability dimension scores 2.0 on absence of disclosure, not on located misconduct.

## Strongest Dimensions

Integrity (2.6), the highest Integrity score in the de-seeded cluster. Four of five Integrity subdimensions score 3 on the same durable fact: a stated non-labour commitment held for two decades, predating any reputational benefit, and reflected in every product shipped. Boundaries (2.4) carries the assessment’s highest single score, B3 Scope Clarity at 4/5, for the same reason.

## Weakest Dimensions

Accountability (2.0), where all five subdimensions sit at the low anchor because the company publishes nothing about its own performance, corrections, failures or repair. Action (2.2), Awareness (2.2) and Equity (2.2) follow: twenty-nine units worldwide is a narrow footprint and no outcome data of any kind is published.

## Evidence Gaps

Engineered Arts is a small private company and the evidence base is correspondingly thin. Two of the five sources are Wikipedia articles and two are secondary technology outlets, so the entire assessment rests on tier-2 evidence. The founder’s twenty-year non-labour position — which drives Boundaries, Systemic Thinking and Integrity, three of the eight dimensions — is characterised in a secondary source rather than quoted from a primary company statement, and it should be verified against a primary source before any future cycle relies on it. The December 2024 restructuring as a US company is reported but its effect on the Cornwall workforce is not disclosed anywhere. Twenty-six of the forty subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.
