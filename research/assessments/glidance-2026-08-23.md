## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Glidance, Inc. does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index, and no prior score, seed or placeholder existed for it. There is no published-comparison section because there is nothing to compare against. This report establishes the first number.

**Corporate status check — operating — pre-shipment.** Glidance, Inc. is operating but has not yet delivered its product at scale. It raised an oversubscribed US$1.5 million pre-seed round, and a further US$125,000 from ten NTAN members in October 2025. As of 2026 it is in beta testing around Seattle with roughly 1,000 preorders and a published plan to begin shipping to Pioneer and Founding Glider customers in spring 2026. **Flagged**, not held: the company plainly exists and trades, but it is pre-revenue and holds customer deposits against an undelivered product, so operating status should be re-verified next cycle.

**Disclosure-capacity note.** Glidance is a pre-seed startup that has raised roughly US$1.6 million in total. It has no legal obligation to publish an annual report, a bias audit, a turnover statistic or outcome data, and it publishes none. **A low score here measures the absence of those documents. It does not measure whether the company cares about blind and low-vision people — the entire product exists to serve them, and blind people helped design it.**

**⚠️ Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** No lawsuit, safety incident, regulatory action or consumer-protection finding against Glidance was located. Where a score rests on nothing being found, the row says so.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **28 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** (70%) and 12 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **Glidance scores 30.6 of 100, and the reason is that it is a pre-seed startup with no published outcome data — not that it lacks compassion.** The company has raised about US$1.6 million in total. It has no legal duty to publish an annual report, a bias audit or a turnover figure, and it publishes none. Twenty-seven of its 40 subdimensions rest on that silence.
- **Blind people helped design this product, and that is the strongest thing in the file.** Glidance runs monthly open calls for a community of more than 8,000 blind and low-vision members and takes prototypes to the American Council of the Blind and National Federation of the Blind conventions for live testing. The National Federation of the Blind hosts its own page on Glidance. That earns three of the four above-floor Awareness and Empathy scores.
- **The price is deliberately low and the company says so in public.** Glide starts at US$1,499 plus US$30 a month — roughly a phone plan, and far below the cost of training a guide dog. Instalment plans are offered. **No insurance or public-payer route was located**, which remains a real barrier for the people who need it most.
- **Glidance publishes its own delays.** The company maintains a dated product roadmap showing a revised spring 2026 production timeline. For a business holding roughly 1,000 preorder deposits, publishing slippage is unflattering. It is the only reason Accountability sits above the floor.
- **Nothing was located about privacy.** Glide navigates public pavements using cameras and sensors. No consent, data-retention or bystander-privacy disclosure was found.

## Strongest Dimensions

- **Awareness, 40.0** — monthly open community calls, convention field testing, and design shaped by the people who will use it.
- **Boundaries, 35.0** — the device is framed as a third option alongside the cane and guide dog, and the roadmap sets expectations before people pay.

## Weakest Dimensions

- **Action, 25.0 and Integrity, 25.0 — both exactly at the absence floor.** The product has not shipped at scale, so no outcome data exists yet.

## Evidence Gaps

- No outcome data, because the device has not shipped at scale.
- No privacy or consent disclosure for a camera-equipped device operating on public pavements.
- No insurance or public-payer reimbursement route located.
- No workforce data and no independent accessibility audit.
