## Key Findings

- The headline reason Milan was flagged does not survive date verification. Italy's Constitutional Court judgment No. 96 on CPR detention is dated 3 July 2025, a full year outside the window.
- The 21 July 2026 presidio is a civil-society gathering sourced to a WordPress blog and is not municipal conduct; it carries negligible scoring weight.
- The one piece of genuine in-window municipal conduct is the 23 July 2026 Gratosoglio clearance of four ALER units, which was carried out with the Comune's social services present alongside police — a structurally better pattern than enforcement alone, but still a displacement.
- Milan's standing profile — social services embedded in enforcement, published performance reporting, cross-agency coordination — supports the Functional band it already holds.
- Composite moves -0.9 to 56.9, within normal grid and judgement tolerance and far below the 5-point proposal threshold.

## Strongest Dimensions

- Systemic Thinking (3.6) — cross-agency coordination is genuine and visible, with policing, waste services, the housing agency and social services acting on a single operation.

## Weakest Dimensions
