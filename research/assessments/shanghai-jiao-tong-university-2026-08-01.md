## Determination

**CONFIRM 22.7.** Assessed 23.1, a difference of +0.4, far below the 5.0-point proposal threshold. **The flag is screened as a false positive on attribution.** The trigger is the US Department of Defense Section 1286 list update of 23 July 2026, which named Shanghai Jiao Tong University among 130 foreign academic and research institutions barred from Pentagon-funded research. That is a decision by the United States government about its own funding and national-security risk. It is not evidence about how Shanghai Jiao Tong University recognises, responds to or reduces suffering — the only thing this benchmark measures. A university does not become less compassionate because a foreign defence ministry restricts research contracts with it, and it would not become more compassionate if that listing were reversed. This is the first full assessment of this entity; it is scored on its own institutional conduct, and the listing is recorded only where it genuinely bears on a subdimension (S3, second-order effects of research relationships). **Paired-evidence note:** Fudan University is flagged on the identical listing and was assessed on it on 2026-07-30, where it was also screened as a false positive and confirmed.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: a blacklist is not a compassion finding.** The US Department of Defense named Shanghai Jiao Tong University on its Section 1286 list on 23 July 2026, barring Pentagon-funded research with it. That is a US funding decision, not evidence about how the university treats people.
- **Why it matters: the listing is broad, not targeted.** The update named 130 academic and research institutions in China, Russia and Iran, and is the largest single-cycle expansion in the list’s history. Eighty-eight are in mainland China.
- **Why it matters: the university does publish real support infrastructure.** Shanghai Jiao Tong University runs a psychological counselling centre with an online appointment system, an in-app booking route, and a dedicated international student support channel. That is why access design scores 3 of 5, the highest single score in the profile.
- **Why it matters: almost nothing is published about outcomes or failure.** No disaggregated outcome data, no complaint statistics, no disclosure of an unflattering finding and no reparative action were located. That is what holds accountability and integrity near the bottom.
- **Why it matters: the score does not move.** Shanghai Jiao Tong University is confirmed at 22.7, rank 100 of 100 in the universities index, band Developing.

## Evidence Gaps

- This is a substantially evidence-poor assessment. Most subdimensions are scored on the absence of a public record rather than on documented practice, and are marked low confidence accordingly.
- No independent audit, no student or staff testimony, and no third-party evaluation of Shanghai Jiao Tong University was located in any language accessible to this assessment.
- Chinese-language institutional disclosures were not searched, so some published practice may exist that this assessment did not reach. This is a known limitation of the universities index for mainland Chinese institutions generally.
- The Section 1286 list PDF was identified but its full contents were not parsed; the naming of the entity is corroborated by two independent secondary sources.

## Anti-False-Positive Screening

Attribution screening applied and decisive. The entity was surfaced on a US Department of Defense listing decision. A foreign government’s national-security determination is evidence about that government’s risk posture, not about the listed institution’s compassion conduct, and it is not scored as such. Directionality screening applied: the flag is nominally negative, so no upgrade is considered; but since the negative item is not the entity’s own conduct, no downgrade is supportable either. Double-count screening applied against Fudan University, flagged on the identical listing and already screened as a false positive on 2026-07-30. Conservative scoring applied throughout: where the public record is silent, the low anchor is used and confidence is marked low rather than inferring practice.
