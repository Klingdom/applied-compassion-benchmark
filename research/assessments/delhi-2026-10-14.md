## Delhi Police blocked a protest site and detained hundreds. The police answer to India's Home Ministry, so the city's score does not move.

On 10 October 2026 Delhi Police stopped Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) marchers from reaching Jantar Mantar and detained hundreds across the city (Onmanorama, 2026-10-10). The UN Human Rights Office urged their release the same day.

Delhi Police is a central force under the Union Home Ministry, not the city government. The conduct is scored against India. At city level the same measures (A4, B5, I1) were already at 2, 1 and 1 for the July 2026 protests.

Delhi measures 28.1 (Developing), 4.7 below its published 32.8, the same as in July and August. No proposal.
