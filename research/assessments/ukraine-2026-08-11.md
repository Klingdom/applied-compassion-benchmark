## Why this entity was assessed tonight

The scanner flagged Russian ballistic missile and drone attacks on Kyiv and surrounding regions overnight on 5 August 2026, and a UN finding that July 2026 was the deadliest month for Ukrainian civilians since April 2022. Ukraine has been flagged repeatedly on the same war, so repeat-flag discipline and the standing scanner year-confusion check both apply.

## Scanner date / attribution corrections

- **Year-confusion caught for the third time — the scan's 31 July figures are wrong.** The scan states the 5 August attack "follows a July 31, 2026 barrage on Kyiv that killed 31 and injured 179." Contemporaneous reporting for **31 July 2026** gives 3 killed and more than a dozen wounded in Kyiv, with a nationwide toll of at least 9. The 31-killed / 179-injured figures match the Kyiv strike of **31 July 2025**, exactly one calendar year earlier. This is the **third instance** of the scanner year-confusion failure class on Kyiv casualty data, after the 2026-08-01 catch. The open scanner-tuning issue is escalated.
- **"Deadliest month" claim — CONFIRMED this time, superseding the 2026-08-01 correction.** On 2026-08-01 the assessor corrected a scanner claim that July 2026 was the worst month since 2022, on the basis that a 14 July UN report gave June 2026 the record. UN News, dated 2026-08-05, now states directly: "July marking the deadliest month for Ukrainian civilians since April 2022." The UN source is dated, tier 4, and supersedes the earlier correction. Recorded as a correction to the benchmark's own prior correction.
- **Scan casualty figures for July not verified.** The scan cited "377 killed, 2,129 wounded" for July 2026. The UN News article confirms the deadliest-month finding but does not carry those month-level figures; it gives six-month totals of 1,396 killed and 7,978 injured. The July-specific figures are **not used** in scoring.
- **Date correction.** Scan evidence_date 2026-08-05 is correct and matches the CNN, Washington Post and Kyiv Independent URLs, all dated 2026-08-05. The third cited URL (cnn.com/2026/08/09) is a separate, later escalation story and is not the flag source.
- **Attribution held, as in prior cycles.** Russia is the perpetrator of these attacks. Ukraine is scored only on its own protective conduct toward civilians — air-raid warning, shelter provision, rescue and medical response — never on being attacked.

## Key Findings

- A Russian strike on Kyiv killed 17 people and not one ballistic missile was stopped. On the night of 5 August 2026 Russia fired 24 ballistic missiles, four other missiles and 115 drones at Kyiv and its region. Seventeen people died and 44 were wounded. Eight of the dead were at a train station in the village of Kvitneve.
- The UN confirmed July 2026 was Ukraine's worst month for civilians in over four years. UN News reported on 5 August 2026 that this was "July marking the deadliest month for Ukrainian civilians since April 2022."
- The benchmark caught the same scanner error for the third time. The scan claimed a 31 July 2026 strike killed 31 people and injured 179. The real 31 July 2026 toll was 3 killed in Kyiv and about 9 nationwide. The larger numbers belong to a strike on 31 July 2025, exactly one year earlier.
- Ukraine is not marked down for being bombed. Russia is the attacker. Ukraine is scored only on what it does for its own civilians: air-raid warning, shelters, rescue and medical response. Rescue crews were working during the attack and continued through the day.
- Ukraine let the bad news be published. The state does not suppress the UN monitoring mission that recorded 1,396 civilians killed and 7,978 injured in the first six months of 2026.

## Strongest Dimensions

Action (3.40/5) and Integrity (3.00/5). Rescue and medical response scaled to a simultaneous mass-casualty event across two cities, and civilian-protection commitments have held across four years of sustained attack.

## Weakest Dimensions

Equity (2.60/5). Shelter and compensation are materially better in Kyiv than in outlying settlements such as Kvitneve, and no disaggregated data show who actually receives them.

## Evidence Gaps

- The scan's July 2026 month-level casualty figures (377 killed, 2,129 wounded) could not be verified from the UN source and were excluded from scoring.
- No disaggregated data on shelter access or compensation delivery by region or population were located.
- Independent assessment of Ukrainian emergency-responder sustainability across four years of war was not available.
- Compensation programme delivery rates for destroyed housing are not published in a verifiable form.
