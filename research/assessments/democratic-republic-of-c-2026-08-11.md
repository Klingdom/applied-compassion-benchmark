## Key Findings

- More than 300 children have died of Ebola in eastern Congo. UNICEF reports children are almost one third of all deaths while making up nearly a quarter of confirmed cases — they are dying at a higher rate than adults who catch it.
- The outbreak is accelerating, not slowing. Total deaths passed 2,000, and half of those occurred in just the preceding 20 days. The Congolese health ministry reported 4,566 confirmed cases and 2,128 deaths through 11 August 2026.
- Pregnant women are a second, largely invisible casualty. Maternal deaths in Ituri province have nearly doubled since the outbreak began.
- People are attacking the clinics meant to save them. At least a dozen Ebola treatment facilities have been attacked, driven by fear, misinformation and deep community mistrust of outside authorities. That is a failure of trust-building, and it is the response's biggest obstacle.
- There is real effort here, and it is mostly not the state's. About 13,000 community workers have been deployed by the health ministry together with Africa CDC, UNICEF and the World Health Organization. The resourcing is overwhelmingly international.

## Strongest Dimensions

Awareness, Action, Boundaries and Systemic Thinking, all at 1.20/5. Case surveillance is publishing real numbers, and 13,000 community workers are deployed in genuine coalition with Africa CDC, UNICEF and WHO.

## Weakest Dimensions
