## Lisbon's first evidence-based score is 35.0, far below its unassessed 60.9. The deadly Glória funicular crash and its slow aftermath weigh most.

Thousands marched in Lisbon on 10 October 2026 over housing costs (Euronews, 2026-10-11). That protest is aimed mostly at national policy. This first assessment looks at what the city government and its companies control.

On 3 September 2025 the Glória funicular, run by the city-owned company Carris, crashed and killed 16 people. The state investigator (GPIAAF) found a non-certified cable in use since 2022. It also found maintenance logged as done that was not done. Nearly a year on, only four compensation payments had been made.

Lisbon gets credit for an annual homeless count, a 2024-2030 homeless plan and housing investment. It measures 35.0 (Developing). The published 60.9 has never been assessed: it is a starting value, not a measurement. A proposal is filed as a first assessment.

**First assessment.** This entity has no prior individual report. The published value is a seed (an unassessed starting value), not a measurement. ## Key Findings
