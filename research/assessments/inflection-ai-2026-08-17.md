## Key findings

- Inflection AI has the highest Empathy score in this study, at 2.8 out of 5, and it is earned. Pi was built to be warm and judgment-free, and independent reviewers consistently describe it working that way.
- The same reviewers name the danger. Pi has no crisis support, cannot diagnose or treat, and is not appropriate for serious or worsening symptoms — yet it sounds enough like a therapist that users can start treating it as one.
- Conversations are stored on company servers, may be used to improve the model, and are not protected by medical-privacy rules. For a product designed to draw out emotionally significant disclosure, no enhanced consent practice was found.
- The company itself is hollowed out. Microsoft hired the founders and much of the staff in March 2024 and paid about $650 million to license the technology. Pi remains online but active development has slowed significantly.
- The score falls 18.4 points. The published 48.4 was never measured, and no source above tier 3 exists for this entity in either direction.

## Strongest dimensions

## Weakest dimensions

## Evidence gaps

- 32 of 40 subdimensions are scored at 2 because no evidence was located in either direction. Under the fixed standard used across all 15 labs in this study, absence of disclosure scores 2, not 1.

- This is a desk-based assessment. If Inflection AI operates grievance channels, disaggregated outcome review or reparative processes without publishing them, this assessment understates it.
