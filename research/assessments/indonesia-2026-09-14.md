## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**THE SCANNER'S CASUALTY EVIDENCE IS FROM 2025, NOT 2026.** The riots that began on 25 August over legislator allowances, the police tactical vehicle killing motorcycle-taxi driver Affan Kurniawan on 28 August, and the roughly 10 deaths are the AUGUST 2025 protests. The Jakarta Post (11 September 2025), The Diplomat and Al Jazeera (3 September 2025) record them, along with the officer's dishonourable discharge in September 2025. The scan had itself noted the casualties were 'outside this scan's window', but attributed them to 2026. EXCLUDED.

**AUGUST 2026 PROTESTS WERE DIFFERENT AND SMALLER.** Bloomberg (28 August 2026, paywalled; headline only) describes late-night Jakarta protests a year after the 2025 violence. It is pre-window, and no casualties were located; not scored.

**FOREIGN POLICY ARTICLE NOT VERIFIABLE.** The foreignpolicy.com URL dated 2026/09/02 returned HTTP 403, so its content and year could not be confirmed. It is not scored.

**A SECOND YEAR MIX-UP CAUGHT IN MY OWN SEARCHES.** A search synthesis described police killing a protester in Manokwari and arresting 23 in Sorong in 'August 2026'. The matching source (Indoleft, 3 September 2025) shows these were 27 August 2025 events. EXCLUDED.

**WHAT REMAINS.** Human Rights Monitor's Papua Monitor for April-June 2026 (published 28 July 2026, tier 3) is pre-window but recent and never scored. It moves one subdimension (AB5), where the impunity facts are established; the killing counts themselves are recorded as allegations ('alleged', 'reportedly') and held.

**SCREENING.** The surfacing evidence is void. The measured -2.8 is sub-threshold. If fuller desk scoring of the Papua evidence produced a larger gap, it would still not be filable: that would be pre-window calibration of a 27-country placeholder (check 5, and the 3M precedent on out-of-window conduct).
