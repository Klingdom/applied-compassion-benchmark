### Recommendation

**CONFIRM**. The composite delta of -4.6 points is inside the 5-point confirmation threshold, so the published score of 35.9 is confirmed. A watch flag is recorded: the delta is close to the threshold and the next cycle should re-test it.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Waymo said it had fixed a safety problem, and the problem kept happening. That gap between remediation and result is what the July 2026 evidence adds.
- On 4 July 2026, dozens of Waymo vehicles stalled in San Francisco gridlock with depleted batteries; some were towed. NHTSA then gave robotaxi operators until the end of July 2026 to fix emergency-responder detection.
- The pattern is long-running. San Francisco firefighters have filed at least 31 internal reports since April 2025 about robotaxis obstructing emergency operations.
- Waymo’s published score already reflects the December 2025 school-bus recall and the January 2026 child-strike investigation. The assessed score of 31.3 is 4.6 points below the published 35.9 — inside the threshold, so the score is confirmed with a watch flag on the end-of-July NHTSA deadline.
