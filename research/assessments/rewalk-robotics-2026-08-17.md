## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the index publishes this company under a name it stopped using two years ago, in the benchmark’s highest band, while the company itself warns it may not survive the next twelve months.
- ReWalk Robotics Ltd. became Lifeward Ltd. on 10 September 2024. The index entry is stale. Any published score should be re-issued under the current legal name.
- Management disclosed substantial doubt about the company’s ability to continue as a going concern, citing a $295.5 million accumulated deficit. For people whose daily mobility depends on the device, that is a Boundaries and Follow-Through risk, and it is scored as one.
- The company’s best work is structural. It helped establish a Medicare benefit category for personal exoskeletons — the final rule took effect on 1 January 2024 and reimbursement was finalised on 11 April 2024. Competitors now bill under the same code.
- Accountability scores 2.0 of 5. Adverse events are on the public record through the FDA MAUDE database because reporting is mandatory. No voluntary harm acknowledgment or repair was located.
