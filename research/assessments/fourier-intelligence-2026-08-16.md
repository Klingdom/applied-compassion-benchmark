## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Fourier Intelligence has the strongest mission-level alignment in the cluster — its core business is helping stroke and spinal-injury patients walk again — and almost no institutional disclosure to verify how it operates.
- Fourier’s rehabilitation products are deployed in more than 2,000 institutions across more than 40 countries, a genuine scale of reach for a therapy technology.
- An independent peer-reviewed meta-analysis of 13 randomised trials covering 424 stroke patients finds robotic therapy gives a moderate, statistically significant gain over conventional rehabilitation — and limited benefit in the chronic phase. That unflattering second finding is published by researchers, not by Fourier.
- The GR-3 "Care-bot", unveiled 6 August 2025, carries a soft-shell body and 31 distributed pressure sensors specifically so it can touch frail people safely, and is aimed at nursing homes.
- Fourier publishes no responsibility report, no workforce data, no patient-outcome data of its own and no failure disclosure. Its whole Accountability dimension scores 2.0 on absence of disclosure, not on located misconduct.

## Strongest Dimensions

Action (3.0). The intervention reaches 2,000-plus institutions in 40-plus countries, the resource commitment exceeds $210 million, therapy intensity is genuinely calibrated to patient impairment, and independent researchers have measured the benefit. Awareness (2.6) and Systemic Thinking (2.6) follow, on clinical detection pathways and on the decade-long progression from exoskeleton to eldercare robot.

## Weakest Dimensions

Accountability (2.0), where all five subdimensions sit at the low anchor because Fourier publishes nothing about its own performance, corrections, failures or repair. Boundaries (2.2) and Integrity (2.2) follow, held down by expansive product claims with no stated limits and by an unresolved tension between the clinical mission and the general-purpose humanoid pivot.

## Evidence Gaps

Fourier Intelligence is a private Chinese company with no located responsibility report, no workforce disclosure, no self-published clinical outcome data and no independent audit. Twenty-two of the forty subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure. The strongest efficacy evidence is a peer-reviewed meta-analysis of the intervention class, not of Fourier’s own products, and that distinction is stated explicitly at AC3. The GR-3 specifications and design philosophy come from a company press release and from trade coverage of it; no independent evaluation of GR-3 exists because it is not yet in service at scale.
