### Score Difference Analysis

The brief predicted UBTECH might hold or move up because it is Hong Kong-listed with real reporting obligations. It fell, but by less than any entity except Kawasaki Heavy Industries. That is the reporting obligation working: UBTECH genuinely publishes an annual ESG report with a three-level management structure and quantified environmental metrics, which lifts Accountability and Awareness marginally above the baseline. What it does not publish is anything on the human-facing anchors. Nothing was located on complaint handling, worker treatment, product-safety incidents, accessibility, consent or repair. And an independent rater, Sustainalytics, judges the management of ESG material risk **Weak** — a third-party view that caps how far mandatory disclosure alone can carry a score.

### Recommendation

The published score is modestly overstated and should be revised down to 26.2. The band does not change. The composite delta of -9.7 clears the filing threshold. This is largely a calibration correction: no regulatory action, safety finding or labour case against UBTECH was located.

## Scoring Conventions Applied

- **Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is used only where positive documented evidence of a specific failure exists. This is the "2-convention", the more conservative of the two conventions live in this repository.
- **A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review. A score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.**
- 38 of 40 subdimensions sit at 2. Of those, approximately 30 rest on absence of disclosure rather than on located evidence of a practice.
