### Math-hygiene reconstruction

### Score Difference Analysis

There is no difference to analyse: every dimension is carried forward. Two corrections to the scan record are worth stating, because both would have inflated a delta if taken at face value. First, the scan summary reports 32 killed and almost 180 injured in Kyiv on 31 July 2026. Contemporaneous reporting for that attack gives far lower figures — the Kyiv Independent records one person killed and three injured in Kyiv city for the 30 July wave, with at least eight killed and more than 50 injured across Ukraine, and wire coverage of the 31 July wave reports three killed and more than a dozen wounded in the capital. The 32-killed figure closely matches the attack of 31 July 2025, a year earlier, and appears to be a year-confusion in the scan summary. Second, the scan summary states that July 2026 saw the highest monthly civilian casualty toll since 2022. The UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine reported on 14 July 2026 that it was June 2026 that was the worst month since April 2022, with at least 293 civilians killed and 1,990 injured. Neither correction changes Ukraine’s score, because neither bears on Ukraine’s own conduct, but both are recorded so the inflated figures do not propagate.

### Recommendation

Confirm the published score of 50.0, band Functional. No proposal. Watch flag retained on civilian protection capacity: the UN records civilian casualties from long-range weapons rising 60 per cent between January and June 2026 against the same period in 2025, which puts sustained pressure on Ukraine’s own shelter, medical and emergency systems. A degradation in those systems, evidenced independently, would be a Ukraine-attributable finding. Being attacked is not.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: the attacker is not the entity being scored.** Russia struck Kyiv with ballistic missiles and drones overnight into 31 July 2026. Ukraine is scored on what Ukraine does, so this attack does not lower Ukraine’s score.
- **Why it matters: Ukraine’s own response was protective.** Ukraine’s State Emergency Service ran rescue operations at damaged residential buildings, and thousands of Kyiv residents sheltered in metro stations before the attack.
- **Why it matters: the scan overstated the casualty figures.** The scan summary reported 32 killed in Kyiv on 31 July 2026. Contemporaneous reporting gives one to three killed in the capital and about eight to nine killed nationwide. The higher figure matches the attack of 31 July 2025.
- **Why it matters: the worst month was June, not July.** The UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine reported on 14 July 2026 that June 2026 was the deadliest month for civilians since April 2022, with at least 293 killed and 1,990 injured.
- **Why it matters: nothing changed in the score.** All eight dimensions are carried forward from 25 July 2026. The composite stays at 50.0.
