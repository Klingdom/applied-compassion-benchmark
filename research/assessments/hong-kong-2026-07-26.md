## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Hong Kong handled a real storm well, and that is not the same as a change in how it treats people. Typhoon Noul made landfall on 26 July 2026 with the No. 8 signal in force, and the government published numbered situation reports the same day.
- The government opened 29 temporary shelters across districts. Everyone injured was treated at accident and emergency departments of public hospitals.
- Reported figures moved through the day as snapshots were updated: 18 injured and 153 fallen-tree reports at one point, 188 trees and a higher injury count at another. Rather than pick a headline number, each figure is attributed to the report that carried it.
- This operational competence is already carried in the published Action score of raw 3.4, the city's highest dimension. Under anti-false-positive screening, one moderate hazard event handled well is not evidence of structural change and was not used to raise the score.
- The floor of the profile is unchanged: Boundaries and Integrity both sit at raw 1.4, reflecting the civic-space restrictions that operational competence does not offset.
