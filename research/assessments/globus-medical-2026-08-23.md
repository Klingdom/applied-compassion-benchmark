## Key Findings

- **Globus Medical scores 25.0 of 100 — the exact floor for an entity with no located disclosure — despite being a US$2.9 billion listed company with a large public record. It lands there because a regulator found its harm-detection system did not work.**
- **The single most serious finding in this batch: the US Food and Drug Administration found that Globus Medical did not investigate complaints that screws had been misplaced inside patients during surgery.** The warning letter of 15 July 2024 also found the company failed to meet requirements for reporting serious injuries on time.
- **Blind Spot Mitigation scores 1 of 5 — the only score of 1 given to a non-consumer company in this batch.** The FDA wrote that Globus Medical used no additional data analysis to understand trends or detect ongoing issues related to misplaced screws. That is a documented finding that no process existed, not an absence of disclosure.
- **The warning letter was still open 19 months later.** Globus Medical's own annual report, filed 24 February 2026, states the July 2024 letter remains open. The company discloses this, which is why Transparency scores 3 while Correction Willingness scores 2.
- **Disclosure did not save this score, and that is the point.** Globus Medical publishes more than most companies in this batch. It scores at the floor because what it published, and what a regulator published about it, describes a failure to look at reports of patients being harmed.
