## Key Findings

- Why it matters: this is a federal accusation, not a court ruling, and the university denies it. The Department of Justice Civil Rights Division announced on 20 July 2026 that UC San Diego’s School of Medicine unlawfully used race in admissions in violation of Title VI.
- Assistant Attorney General Harmeet K. Dhillon said that rather than rely on MCAT scores or GPA, San Diego Med’s shadow application process unlawfully judged applicants for admission based on their race.
- UC San Diego responded that all applicants to the school’s Doctor of Medicine program must meet strict academic thresholds, a requirement that is and was applied uniformly to all applicants.
- The Department of Justice is seeking a voluntary resolution agreement and says it will sue if talks fail. No court has decided the question.
- Under the pre-adjudication screen, a contested agency finding cannot move the score on its own. The assessed composite of 44.4 is 0.9 points below the published 45.3, which is a confirmation with a watch flag on any settlement or suit.
