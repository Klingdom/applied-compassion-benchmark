# Compassion Benchmark Near-Floor Confirmation: Meta Platforms

**Entity type:** Company
**Assessment date:** 2026-09-20
**Outcome:** confirmation; new tier-3 evidence corroborates the existing near-floor score
**Published:** 7.8/100 (Critical), fortune-500 rank 445
**Carried measurement:** 8.1/100 (Critical), measured 2026-08-26

## Why this entity was assessed

Amnesty International published a briefing on 8 September 2026 finding that Meta ineffectively enforced its own content-moderation policies on Facebook in Hungary, leaving anti-LGBTI hate speech visible after it was reported. This is a distinct topic from the recurring headcount-reduction claim already tracked for this entity in the misdated-claims ledger, and is treated as genuine new-topic news.

## New in-window evidence

Amnesty International's researcher Daniela Schnidrig states: "Meta contributed to adverse human rights impacts experienced by LGBTI people in Hungary through its policy decisions, and failed to take adequate steps to prevent the resulting harm."

Amnesty's method was interviews with LGBTI community members plus harmful-content examples collected by Háttér Society covering posts published between January 2023 and December 2024, together with social-listening analysis. The briefing documents that harmful posts "remained accessible for a period of time after being reported, including content that was still available when checked in March 2026". Unremoved examples included calls for violence ("Beat them to death immediately") and dehumanising language comparing LGBTI people to "rats", "mutants" and "scum". Amnesty calls on the European Commission to "robustly enforce the DSA" and investigate Meta's compliance.

## Disposition: CORROBORATES, DOES NOT MOVE

Meta Platforms already sits at 7.8, rank 445 of 447, in the Critical band. The relevant subdimensions are already at or beside the floor: A5 Anticipatory Awareness, E3 Non-Judgment, EQ1 Universality, AC1 Responsiveness and AB2 Correction Willingness all sit inside dimensions scored between 1.0 and 1.6 raw.

The Amnesty finding is exactly the behaviour those anchors already describe. Anchor 1 for AB2 reads "Harmful practices continue even when documented", and Amnesty documents content still live in March 2026 after being reported. The evidence confirms the published placement; it does not add new information that changes it, because the score is already at the level the evidence implies.

## Screening checks

- **SOURCE TIER.** Amnesty International is a watchdog NGO, tier 3. The finding is a credible independent assessment, not a court or regulator determination. Meta has not been found in breach of the Digital Services Act; Amnesty asks the European Commission to investigate.
- **NO DOUBLE-COUNT.** This is a content-moderation finding, separate from the layoffs claim in `known-misdated-claims.json`. The 2026-05-26 applied downgrade covering the integrity-team layoff is not re-counted here.
- **DIRECTIONALITY.** Negative surfacing, no upward movement. Consistent.
- **NO FILING TRIGGER.** Delta +0.3 against the 2026-08-26 measurement, band unchanged (Critical).

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Amnesty International says Meta left anti-LGBTI hate speech up in Hungary after people reported it. Some of that content was still visible when Amnesty checked in March 2026.
- Why it matters: the posts were not borderline. They included "Beat them to death immediately" and language calling LGBTI people "rats", "mutants" and "scum".
- Why it matters: Amnesty says Meta's own policy choices caused harm, and that Meta failed to assess and reduce a risk it could have foreseen.
- Why it matters: the score does not move because it is already near the bottom. Meta Platforms ranks 445 of 447 Fortune 500 companies at 7.8 out of 100. The finding confirms that placement.
