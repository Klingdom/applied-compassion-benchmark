### Score Difference Analysis

**Awareness (-12.5 points scaled).** The published seed implies some proactive detection. What the record shows is the opposite: contractors reached decision-makers by writing to United States senators, and the Department of Labor opened an investigation in August 2024 before dropping it in May 2025 with no public rationale. Problems surfaced through litigation, journalism and political appeal, which is anchor 1 or 2 territory. Every Awareness subdimension scores 2.

**Equity (0 points scaled — unchanged, and that is the point).** The seed already put Equity at the low anchor. The evidence supports keeping it there. Hourly rates run from about 90 cents in Venezuela to $10 to $25 in the United States for the same platform work, and the Business and Human Rights Resource Centre records Philippine workers reporting withheld payments and 'digital sweatshop' conditions.

**Accountability (-12.5 points scaled).** The only located repair is a compelled settlement carrying no admission. Under the Reparative Action anchors, repair that exists only as a legal settlement is anchor 1 or 2; it was scored 2.

**Systemic Thinking (-2.5 points scaled) held up best at 2.4.** Scale's Safety, Evaluations and Alignment Lab publishes SEAL leaderboards that the wider AI field uses, and it changed its own evaluation methodology from human-expert to a hybrid approach. That is a genuine public contribution and it was scored as one.

**Nothing was scored as proven harm.** Every claim against Scale AI is undecided. Each was scored at the absence anchor of 2, never at the floor.

### Recommendation

**The published 35.9 is overstated and should be revised down to 26.9. The band does not change.** Unlike most entities in this cluster, the correction rests on documented conduct at tier 4 and tier 5, not on silence. It should still be published as a calibration correction with the undecided status of every allegation stated plainly.

- **A $12.5 million settlement was preliminarily approved on 25 July 2026** in McKinney v. Scale AI, San Francisco Superior Court case CGC-24-620481, covering claims of misclassification, unpaid overtime and minimum wage, missed meal and rest breaks and sick-leave violations from 10 December 2020 to 28 February 2026.
- **Nothing is decided.** The final approval hearing is set for 30 October 2026 and the settlement records that 'Defendants strongly deny violating any laws'. No allegation was scored as proven harm.
- **The same platform work pays about 90 cents an hour in Venezuela and $10 to $25 in the United States.** The Business and Human Rights Resource Centre records Philippine workers reporting low wages and withheld payments.
- **Contractors had to write to United States senators to be heard.** That is why Signal Amplification scores 2: an internal channel that worked would have made the letter unnecessary.
- **CORPORATE-STATUS ITEM: three chief executives in fourteen months.** Alexandr Wang left for Meta in June 2025 after a $14.3 billion investment for a 49 percent stake, Jason Droege served as interim, and Francis deSouza became chief executive on 10 August 2026.

## Evidence Gaps

No worker grievance data, pay transparency data or contractor turnover data was located. The Department of Labor's rationale for dropping its investigation in May 2025 was never made public, so it cannot be read as exoneration or as anything else. The Kenya Remotasks shutdown rests on a tier-3 source and would benefit from primary confirmation.

## Scoring Convention Applied

**Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is reserved for positive documented evidence of a specific failure. This is the majority convention across the three most recent de-seeding studies and was applied identically to all seven labs in this study. **Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** Where this company does not publish, the low anchor was scored and said so explicitly. A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review; a score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.
