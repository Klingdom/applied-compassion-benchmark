## Key Findings

- This cycle's evidence supports the pending cut to Spain's score. Measured at 51.2, Spain sits close to the 50.6 already awaiting review, and well below its published 60.0.
- Spain is protecting migrant children in Ceuta. It refused to deport unaccompanied minors and is moving about 500 children to the mainland.

- More than 5,300 people went back to Morocco after 10 August 2026, which Spain calls voluntary. No independent body has checked that claim.
