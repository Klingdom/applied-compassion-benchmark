## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Franka Robotics GmbH does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index, and no prior score, seed or placeholder existed for it. All eight index files were searched for the entity name before assessment and returned no match. There is no published-comparison section because there is nothing to compare against. This report establishes the first number.

**Corporate status check — operating-flagged.** **Status flagged, and the reason matters.** Franka Emika GmbH filed for insolvency in September 2023, citing irreconcilable shareholder differences. Agile Robots AG acquired the company on 2 November 2023 — roughly two months into preliminary insolvency, for a reported sum above €30 million — retaining about 100 staff. The business now trades as Franka Robotics GmbH within Agile Robots SE. It is currently **operating**: it produced the 1,000th Franka Research 3 robot at Agile Robots' Kaufbeuren site in February 2025 and is listed as a Hannover Messe 2026 exhibitor. **Agile Robots SE holds no published composite in any of the eight Compassion Benchmark indexes**, so under R-SUB-1 the separately incorporated, separately branded subsidiary may hold its own record. The flag is carried because the entity passed through insolvency and a change of control within the last three years and part of its public record now sits on the parent's channels. **Re-verify next cycle.**

**Disclosure-capacity note.** Franka Robotics is a roughly 100-person German company that was insolvent three years ago and is now a subsidiary. It has no obligation to publish a social report and does not publish one. This is silence by constraint far more than by choice.

**⚠️ Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** Where a score rests on nothing being found, the row says so in plain words. No lawsuit, recall, regulator action or safety incident against Franka Robotics or Franka Emika was located. The 2023 insolvency is a corporate-status fact, not a compassion finding.

## Key Findings

- **Franka Robotics GmbH scores 25.6 of 100. Its one above-floor score comes from something unusual: it gave researchers low-level access to its robot, and that made shared scientific work possible across laboratories it will never sell to.**
- **An independent IEEE peer-reviewed survey calls the Franka platform a standard platform in robotics research**, adopted across AI, manipulation, control, human-robot interaction and motion planning. Coalitional Compassion scores 3 of 5 on that basis.
- **Corporate status is flagged, not clean.** Franka Emika GmbH filed for insolvency in September 2023 and Agile Robots acquired it on 2 November 2023 for a reported sum above €30 million, keeping about 100 staff. The business now operates as Franka Robotics GmbH inside Agile Robots SE, produced its 1,000th Research 3 unit in February 2025 and exhibits at Hannover Messe 2026.
- **Agile Robots SE holds no published composite anywhere in the benchmark**, so the separately branded subsidiary may hold its own record under R-SUB-1. The flag exists because the entity passed through insolvency and change of control within three years.
- **Thirty-seven of 40 subdimensions rest on absence, and this is silence by constraint.** A 100-person German company that was insolvent three years ago has no legal duty to publish an annual report, a bias audit or a turnover statistic, and publishes none.
