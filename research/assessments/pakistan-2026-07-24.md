### Recommendation

**CONFIRM**. The composite delta of -0.3 points is inside the 5-point confirmation threshold, so the published score of 17.2 is confirmed.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Operation Shaban is not a new kind of event for Pakistan. It is the same pattern the critical-band score already describes, running at higher intensity through July 2026.
- The state reported 129 militants killed between 5 July and 16 July 2026. Baloch groups dispute those counts and say some of the dead were civilians who had earlier been forcibly disappeared.
- The scale of disappearance is independently documented. The Human Rights Council of Balochistan recorded 234 enforced disappearances and 87 killings in February 2026 alone.
- The assessed score of 16.9 is 0.3 points below the published 17.2, so the score is confirmed with no change.
