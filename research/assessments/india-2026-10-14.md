## India detained hundreds of protesters in Delhi and rejected the UN's concern. The rejection lowers one measure; the score is unchanged.

On 10 October 2026 the UN Human Rights Office said it was concerned by mass detentions of protesters, lawyers and journalists in Delhi. It asked India to release everyone arbitrarily detained. Delhi Police sources put the number detained at about 1,000; all were released the same evening (WebIndia123/IANS, 2026-10-10).

India's Permanent Mission in Geneva called the concern 'misplaced' and 'unwarranted interference' (DTNext, 2026-10-11). That moves Validation (E4) from 2 to 1. Other affected measures were already at 1 or 2 for the same kind of conduct in July and August.

India measures 15.6 (Critical), equal to its published 15.6. No proposal.
