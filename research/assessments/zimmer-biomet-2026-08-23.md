**Corporate status check — operating.** Zimmer Biomet Holdings, Inc. is operating and listed on the New York Stock Exchange as ZBH. Its Form 10-K for the year ended 31 December 2025 was filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 19 February 2026 and reports full-year net sales of US$8.232 billion, up 7.2% on the prior year. Corporate status was confirmed from the audited exchange filing, not from a company press release. **R-SUB-4 finding, recorded as the scope study requires.** Zimmer Biomet is a genuine Fortune 500 company that is absent from the benchmark's loaded `fortune-500.json` (447 of 500 rows). ## Key Findings

- **Zimmer Biomet Holdings scores 32.5 of 100 — the highest of the fifteen companies in this batch, and still inside the Developing band.** The score is driven almost entirely by things other institutions did: a US Food and Drug Administration recall classification, four national joint replacement registries, and a Department of Justice compliance monitor.
- **Independent joint registries, not the company, are the reason anyone can check whether Zimmer Biomet's knees work.** The Australian Orthopaedic Association National Joint Replacement Registry has published outlier implants with higher-than-anticipated revision rates since 2004, and a cross-registry review found 15 data points on Zimmer Biomet's NexGen and Persona knee systems across four registries.
- **The company recalled a robot that could move to the wrong position inside a patient's skull — and it is also a repeat bribery offender.** The ROSA Brain 3.0 Class I recall covered 66 US devices after five complaints and one patient injury. Separately, Biomet entered a deferred prosecution agreement in 2012, kept using a distributor already named for paying bribes, and needed a second agreement and roughly US$30.5 million in 2017.
- **The patient never chooses the robot.** No patient-facing consent disclosure, robot-use notification or opt-out was located for the ROSA line. The hospital is the buyer; the person in the operating theatre is the one affected.
- **Twenty-six of 40 subdimensions rest on absence — and this is a company that can afford to publish.** With roughly US$8.2 billion in sales, a compliance department and board-level oversight, Zimmer Biomet's silence on grievance channels, staff turnover, disaggregated patient outcomes and reparative process is silence by choice.
