## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Papua New Guinea prosecuted protest rather than answering it. Police arrested six men for burning the national flag at a demonstration against the Bush Wara eviction in Port Moresby, reported in the 16 to 22 July 2026 Pacific Islands weekly roundup.
- The six were charged under the National Identity Act for improper use of the national flag and with unlawful assembly under the Criminal Code Act.
- Bush Wara had been home to about 5,000 people who built permanent structures there. The site is being cleared by its owner, the superannuation provider Nambawan Super Ltd, for redevelopment.
- No relocation or resettlement programme for displaced residents has been documented, so follow-through and refusal ethics both score low.
