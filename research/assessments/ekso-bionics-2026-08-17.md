Ekso Bionics publishes none of those things. 20 of the 40 subdimensions here score at the low anchor of 2, which under this study's fixed rule means **no evidence was found in either direction** — not that harm was found.

### Recommendation

The published score is **overstated** and should be revised down to 41.3, band Functional. This is a seed correction, not a conduct downgrade.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Ekso Bionics has genuine, independently checkable evidence that its product helps people — but it does not have the audited, community-verified practice that an "Exemplary" band asserts.
- The strongest single finding is access, not technology. A revised US Department of Veterans Affairs policy lets qualified veterans receive the Ekso Indego Personal exoskeleton at no cost to the veteran.
- Ekso holds four separate FDA clearances covering stroke, spinal cord injury, acquired brain injury (2020) and multiple sclerosis (June 2022). Each clearance extended the device to a group previously excluded.
- The company is financially fragile. It ran a 1-for-15 reverse stock split effective 2 June 2025 after a December 2024 Nasdaq notice that its share price was too low. A depleted foundation is a Boundaries problem, and it is scored as one.
- Accountability is the weakest dimension at 2.0 of 5. Adverse events appear in the FDA MAUDE database because reporting is legally required. No voluntary harm acknowledgment, correction or repair was found.
