## Key findings

- Isomorphic Labs is the clearest case in this study of real anticipatory work. Its bioresilience programme with Google DeepMind builds outbreak-detection infrastructure and screens for model misuse before harm occurs, with more than 15 government agencies and biosecurity organisations engaged.
- It is also the clearest case of closing access. More than 1,000 scientists signed a letter objecting that AlphaFold3 was published without usable code. Isomorphic's successor engine, IsoDDE, was described in a 27-page report in February 2026 and not released at all.
- No Isomorphic drug has reached a patient. First-in-human trials slipped from 2025 to the end of 2026. The company disclosed the delay itself, which is worth credit; but it means there is no clinical outcome evidence to score.
- A mission described as solving all disease sits against a decision to withdraw tools from the scientists who would help do it. That tension is documented in Science and Nature and has not been publicly reconciled.
- The score falls 26.5 points. Most of that is the gap between a placeholder asserting published data and independent verification, and a company at the pre-clinical stage with none.
