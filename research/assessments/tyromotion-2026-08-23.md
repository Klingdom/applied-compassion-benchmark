## Key Findings

- **Tyromotion scores 27.5 of 100, and the single reason it sits above the floor is that other people study its devices.** Independent peer-reviewed research — a systematic review of the Amadeo in upper-limb stroke rehabilitation, a published treatment protocol in the Journal of Neurologic Physical Therapy, and referral prediction models in the Journal of Hand Therapy — supplies the only outcome evidence in this file.
- **Thirty-two of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.** Tyromotion is a private Austrian company. It has no legal duty to publish workforce data, a bias audit or a social report, and it publishes none. **No adverse finding of any kind was located either.**
- **Six of eight dimensions sit at or within 0.2 of the absence floor.** Empathy, Equity, Accountability and Integrity all score exactly 25 of 100.
- **The device is built for the most impaired patients, which counts for less here than it should.** The Amadeo is designed for people with barely any grasp ability or none. That is a genuine access choice, but with no barrier mapping, cost analysis or public-payer coverage decision located, the anchor caps it at 2 of 5.
- **Nothing was located about the patients' side of the transaction.** Clinics buy the devices; patients use them. No patient consent architecture, complaint route or independent testimony was found.
