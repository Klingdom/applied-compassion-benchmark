## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Poland (priority 57) on registered Ukrainian refugees falling from 974,500 (April 2026) to 868,000 (early September 2026). It linked this to a legal change removing people from the temporary-protection registry over documentation. Poland was last assessed on 2026-07-23 (confirmation at 42.5).

## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**FACTS VERIFIED.** RFE/RL (11 September 2026, fetched directly after the globalsecurity.org mirror returned HTTP 403) confirms the figures and the 1 September loss of residence rights. It adds a fact NOT in the scan: the policy was later amended to provide a six-month grace period. UNHCR Poland (4 March 2026) confirms the 31 August 2026 travel-document deadline.

**ATTRIBUTION.** The registry removals and border-registration rules are state conduct. The fall in public support for refugees (94% in 2022 to 42% in July 2026, per CBOS polling) is public opinion, not state conduct, and is NOT scored. The 106,500 fall is not solely caused by the September rule; RFE/RL also attributes part of it to the March border-declaration requirement.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE.** The published 42.2 was confirmed on 2026-05-04 and 2026-07-23. There is no applied change, and it is individually assessed (not a placeholder).

**BALANCE.** One down-move (scope clarity: people learning of ineligibility only at the office) and one up-move (correction willingness: the grace period) net to zero.

### Recommendation

CONFIRM 42.2 (Functional). Measured 42.5 (+0.3). A harmful documentation cliff edge on 1 September was partly corrected by a six-month grace period.

## Key Findings

- Poland stripped some Ukrainian refugees of legal residence on 1 September 2026. They had registered without a valid passport and missed a 31 August deadline to show one.
- Poland then eased the rule. It added a six-month grace period, a correction the scanner had not picked up.
- Registered refugees fell by about 106,500 between April and early September 2026, from 974,500 to 868,000.
- Some refugees only found out at the immigration office that border guards had logged them as tourists.
