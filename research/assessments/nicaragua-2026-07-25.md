## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Nicaragua sits near the bottom of the scale, and the newest evidence confirms that placement rather than changing it. The UN human rights chief said on 22 July 2026 that the government is holding 46 people in arbitrary political detention.
- Bishop Abelardo Mata, aged 80, was detained on 29 June 2026 after praying for the persecuted church. The UN High Commissioner has had to publicly demand that Nicaragua disclose his whereabouts and condition. He has diabetes, vision problems and a pacemaker.
- The government revoked the credentials of a number of lawyers with no legal basis and no official explanation, cutting detainees off from representation.
- Nicaragua has never acknowledged the 2018 crackdown that left more than 300 people dead. Historical harm acknowledgment scores at the floor.
