## Why this entity was assessed

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ACTOR VERIFIED — this is STATE legislation, not federal (gate warning per the Lagos precedent).** The 2026-08-18 cycle had to reject a Lagos flag because the acting party was the Nigerian **federal** police, not the city. That check was run here first. The Prioritizing Patient Access to Care Act was **passed by the Massachusetts Legislature and signed by the Governor of Massachusetts**, and is announced on **mass.gov**, the state's own official channel. It is unambiguously state conduct and is properly scored against Massachusetts.

**DATE MISMATCH RESOLVED (gate warning 3).** The scanner recorded `evidence_date: 2026-08-10` against a URL carrying **2026-08-11**. There is no conflict: the **signing date is 10 August 2026**, confirmed by Boston.com (URL path /2026/08/10/) and by the mass.gov announcement. WBUR (2026-08-11) and Axios Boston (2026-08-12) are next-day and second-day reports. The 10 August conduct date is used.

**CLAIM DISCIPLINE.** The scanner's summary described Massachusetts as "the 11th state with no gestational abortion limits". Sources disagree: TheGrio says tenth, the scanner's Catholic World Report source says eleventh. **The count is not load-bearing and is not used in scoring.** What is scored is the state's own act. Advocacy framings on both sides ("abortion up to the moment of birth") were checked against a Snopes fact-check and are **not** treated as findings; the operative statutory language is that "an abortion may be performed by a physician based upon the professional judgment of the physician". The Act takes effect 90 days after signing.

**COUNTERVAILING EVIDENCE SOUGHT AND FOUND.** A balanced assessment requires looking for negative state conduct in the same period. It exists: Massachusetts capped its emergency-assistance family shelter caseload at **4,000 families at a time — about a one-third reduction** from the 6,012 families on the caseload as of 30 January — while funding the programme at $276 million in the FY2026 budget signed in July. That cap is scored against the state in Equity and Boundaries.

### Analysis

The assessed composite of 59.4 reproduces the published 59.4 exactly, with a delta of 0.0 and the Functional band sustained. The internal composition shifts slightly and in opposite directions: Boundaries rises from 2.4 to 2.6 because the new law relocates the decision to the patient and physician, while Equity falls from 3.6 to 3.4 because the emergency-assistance family cap is allocation not following need. These offset. No filing trigger is met. The published score is confirmed.

## Key Findings

- Massachusetts holds at 59.4 of 100 and stays third among US states. The score does not move at all.
- Governor Maura Healey signed a law on 10 August 2026 removing the state's 24-week limit on abortion. Care after 24 weeks now rests on the physician's professional judgment rather than on four statutory categories. The law takes effect 90 days after signing.
- This is state legislation, verified against the state's own official channel. That check matters: two days earlier this pipeline had to reject a flag because the actor turned out to be a federal agency, not the local government.
- A balanced look at the same period found real countervailing conduct. Massachusetts capped its emergency family shelter caseload at 4,000 families — about a third below the 6,012 families it was serving in January.
- The two moves cancel out. Boundaries rises because patients gained decision authority; Equity falls because families lost shelter places. The net change is zero.

## Strongest Dimensions

Awareness (AWR, raw 3.80) and Systemic Thinking (SYS, raw 3.80) are jointly strongest. Massachusetts detects need through multiple formal channels and legislates upstream, including on a nationally contentious issue in an election year.

## Weakest Dimensions

Boundaries (BND, raw 2.60) remains the weakest dimension by a wide margin, as in the published vector. Two subdimensions sit at 2/5: no published monitoring of frontline state staff wellbeing, and no guaranteed alternative for families capped out of the emergency-assistance system.

## Evidence Gaps

- The new law takes effect 90 days after signing, so no access-outcome data exists yet. Its effect is scored on design, not on results.
- Sources disagree on whether Massachusetts is the tenth or eleventh state without a gestational limit; the count was excluded from scoring.
- No published state monitoring of public-employee wellbeing or turnover was located; scored at the absence-of-disclosure anchor rather than as evidence of harm.
