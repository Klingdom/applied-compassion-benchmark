## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the first strike in history against a humanoid robot happened on the day Boston Dynamics changed hands. Hyundai Motor Group closed its full acquisition on 20 July 2026, and on the same day 35,000 union workers at the Ulsan complex escalated a strike demanding written guarantees before any Atlas robot enters a Korean production floor.
- The strike is against Hyundai Motor, the parent, over a deployment decision that is Hyundai's to make. Under the attribution rule most of it is not Boston Dynamics conduct. What is Boston Dynamics conduct is designing and marketing a humanoid for shared factory floors with no worker-consent pathway built in.
- The regulatory gap is real. No OSHA standard and no finalised ISO standard yet governs humanoid-robot and worker coexistence on a production floor.
- One detail did move the Equity score. Atlas is planned for first factory deployment at Metaplant America in Georgia from 2028, a plant that, unlike Ulsan, is not unionised.
- A date correction was made. The prior assessment of 22 July 2026 recorded the acquisition as completing on 16 July. Multiple dated sources place the closing on 20 July 2026, the contractual deadline for SoftBank to exercise its put option; the June transaction was the purchase of the remaining 9.65 per cent stake.
