### Score Difference Analysis

Every dimension difference in the table above is an artefact of the seed, because no dimension of this company was ever measured under the Halodi name. The published Empathy score of 4.0 asserted that people confirm they feel heard. The published Accountability score of 3.5 asserted transparency about performance and failures. Neither is supported by anything located. A concurrent assessment of the identical company under its current name, 1X Technologies, reached 26.9 on 2026-08-17. This assessment reached 25.0 independently. The 1.9-point gap between two separate assessments of one company is a useful reliability datum: it comes entirely from whether secondary reporting of 1X's consent claims is treated as scorable. This assessment took the stricter view.

### Recommendation

Do not revise this score. Delete this record. Publishing two composites for one company, 18.9 points apart and in two different bands, is more damaging to the benchmark than either number being wrong. If the record is retained pending a decision, 25.0 is the defensible value.

## Scoring Conventions Applied

- **Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is used only where positive documented evidence of a specific failure exists. This is the "2-convention", the more conservative of the two conventions live in this repository.
- **A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review. A score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.**
- 40 of 40 subdimensions sit at 2. Of those, approximately 37 rest on absence of disclosure rather than on located evidence of a practice.
