## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Florida has executed more people this year than every other state combined, and it also leads the country in death row exonerations. Both facts are true at once, and the state has treated only one of them as significant.
- Florida carried out 10 executions in 2026 by mid-July. Dennis Sochor, executed 15 July 2026, was the 29th person put to death by the state in 19 months.
- The 2025 total was 19 executions, more in a single year than under any Florida governor since the death penalty was reinstated in 1976. The superlative was corroborated against dated reporting from 15 and 16 July 2026 and against the Death Penalty Information Center state record before being scored.
- The scan deliberately excluded a second pair of executions scheduled for 28 July 2026 as outside the window. That exclusion was preserved: no future-scheduled execution is scored or referenced as a completed event.
- The finding sharpens two subdimensions — EQ3 bias awareness and AB4 systemic learning — because a state that produces the most wrongful capital convictions in the country and neither pauses nor reviews its capital process is not learning from failure.
