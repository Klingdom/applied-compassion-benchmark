### Score Difference Analysis

The published vector asserted that Figure has formal pathways for detecting distress, structured perspective-taking, published response data and transparency about failures. None of that was located. Figure publishes engineering announcements and funding news; it does not publish institutional data, a responsibility report, grievance statistics or outcome measurement. The one genuinely creditable finding is the safety engineering of Figure 03 for home use, which is specific, technical and checkable. The whistleblower suit is legitimate evidence that a dispute exists, but it has not been decided in court and this assessment does not treat its allegations as facts.

### Recommendation

The published robotics-labs score is overstated and should be revised down to 25.6. Separately, and more urgently, the benchmark should decide which index owns Figure AI. Publishing 48.4 in one index and 31.3 in another for the same company is a governance failure that no score revision fixes.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: the benchmark publishes two different scores for Figure AI.** The ai-labs record was assessed and moved to 31.3 on 19 June 2026. The robotics-labs record still sits at the untouched 48.4 placeholder — a 17.1-point gap for one company.
- **A safety engineer's lawsuit against Figure AI has not been decided in court.** Robert Gruendel filed suit on 21 November 2025 alleging he was fired for raising safety warnings. Figure says he was let go for poor performance. This assessment records the dispute and does not score the allegations as established.
- **Figure does publish real safety engineering detail for its home robot.** Figure 03 uses soft washable textiles, multi-density foam over pinch points, and a battery certified to UN38.3. This is the company's single strongest compassion-relevant disclosure.
- **Figure publishes no responsibility report, grievance data or outcome measurement.** Roughly 32 of the 40 scores rest on absence of disclosure.
- **The correction here is a measurement fix, not an accusation.** No regulatory finding, safety recall or court judgment against Figure AI was located.

## Scoring Conventions Applied

- **Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is used only where positive documented evidence of a specific failure exists. This is the "2-convention", the more conservative of the two conventions live in this repository.
- **A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review. A score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.**
- 39 of 40 subdimensions sit at 2. Of those, approximately 32 rest on absence of disclosure rather than on located evidence of a practice.
