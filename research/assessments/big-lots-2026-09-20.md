## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the scored company may no longer exist. Big Lots filed for Chapter 11 in October 2024, announced in December 2024 that it would liquidate and close all remaining stores, and sold 200 to 400 stores to Variety Wholesalers, a private North Carolina retailer.
- Why it matters: that is an identity question, not a score question. The Fortune 500 entry measures Big Lots Inc.; the stores now trading as Big Lots are operated by a different, private owner. This is referred to the coordinator for entity-identity review.
- Why it matters: the wind-down did fund severance. About $125 million was set aside for the wind-down budget covering severance, professional fees and taxes, and the sale preserved an estimated 5,000 to 10,000 jobs.
- Why it matters: headquarters staff were not spared. Big Lots began laying off 555 employees at its Columbus, Ohio headquarters from 29 December, including the chief executive.
- Why it matters: the score itself barely moves. The measured composite is 23.1 against a published 23.4, a 0.3-point difference inside the same Developing band.
