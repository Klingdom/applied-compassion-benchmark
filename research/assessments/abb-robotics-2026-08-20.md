## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. ABB Robotics does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index. It is listed as a named business unit whose parent, ABB Ltd, holds no published composite anywhere in the benchmark.

**Corporate status check — flagged.** ABB Robotics is operating, but **its ownership is changing.** On 8 October 2025 ABB announced the sale of its Robotics division to SoftBank Group for about $5.375 billion. The deal is expected to close **mid-to-late 2026**, subject to regulatory approval in the European Union, China and the United States. The division has roughly 7,000 employees and had 2024 revenue of about $2.3 billion, or 7% of ABB group revenue ([ABB news release](https://new.abb.com/news/detail/129685/abb-to-divest-robotics-division-to-softbank-group); [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/abb-group-sells-abb-robotics-softbank-5-3b/); [SoftBank Group announcement](https://group.softbank/en/news/press/20251008)).

**Two consequences the index must handle.** First, ABB's own 2025 sustainability reporting states that the Robotics division, being divested in 2026, **is excluded from the 2025 target**. Much of the group-level disclosure this assessment reads therefore already excludes the unit being scored. Second, on completion the entity's disclosure regime changes wholesale — ABB Ltd's reporting will no longer cover it. **This record should be re-verified at the next publication cycle and, on completion, renamed and re-scoped under §2.4 of the expansion criteria.**

**Evidence base and its limits.** ABB Ltd publishes group-level sustainability disclosure of relatively high quality — a Sustainability Statement and a public disclosure dashboard. That is self-published corporate material and does not count toward independent verification under this index's tightened test. Third-party evidence located: the SoftBank transaction record, ISO 10218 safety standards work, and trade-press coverage of the education programme.

**Absence versus conduct.** No adverse conduct finding, safety scandal, labour ruling or regulatory action against ABB Robotics was located. **Where a score below 3 rests on absence of disclosure, the row says so.**

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **28 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 12 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **ABB Robotics is being sold, and that is the most important fact about this record.** SoftBank Group agreed on 8 October 2025 to buy the division for about $5.375 billion, with completion expected mid-to-late 2026. Roughly 7,000 employees move with it.
- **The parent's own reporting already excludes the unit being scored.** ABB's 2025 sustainability target explicitly leaves out the Robotics division because it is being divested. Much of the disclosure that would otherwise support this assessment does not cover the entity.
- **The compassion practices found belong to the parent, not the unit — so they may not survive the sale.** Grievance channels, materiality assessment and target disclosure are ABB Ltd structures. Resilience of Care scores 2 of 5 because nothing found is demonstrably embedded in the Robotics business itself.
- **The clearest positive evidence is the education programme.** ABB certifies teachers, supplies curriculum and sells training cells to schools at reduced cost in more than 40 countries. The school runs the course afterwards without ABB, which is capacity-building with an exit.
- **Twenty-eight of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.** No adverse conduct finding against ABB Robotics was located in any dimension.

## Strongest Dimensions
- **Awareness (40.0)** and **Systemic Thinking (35.0)** — group grievance channels, a published materiality process, mandatory ISO 10218 pre-market assessment, and a 40-country education programme.

## Weakest Dimensions
- **Empathy, Equity and Integrity, all at 25.0** — the absence floor. Nothing was found in either direction on complaint validation, coverage, disparity investigation, costly commitments or internal culture.

## Evidence Gaps
- No unit-level disclosure. Everything social is reported at ABB group level, and the unit is now excluded from group targets.
- No independent assurance of social metrics.
- No published data on employment effects of ABB robots in customer plants.
- No visibility into which practices transfer to SoftBank ownership.
