### Score Difference Analysis

The -4.1 difference is the largest confirmation in this cycle and it is mostly real rather than grid rounding. Movement sits in Equity (2.0 to 1.8), Boundaries (2.0 to 1.8), Accountability (2.0 to 1.8), Integrity (2.0 to 1.8) and Empathy (2.5 to 2.2). The Wire reported on 20 July 2026 that Mumbai Police detained more than 300 protesters across the city ahead of a planned rally, including an 11-year-old boy, school-going girls and college students, holding people for several hours beyond 10pm. Detentions occurred around Shivaji Park and Chaityabhoomi with people taken to the Worli, Mahim, Sion and Bhoiwada police stations. Protesters said they were "indiscriminately picked up" without warrants or explanations, that their phone numbers, addresses and photographs were recorded, and that some phones were confiscated with data transferred. Police invoked the Maharashtra Police Act to prohibit assemblies of five or more people citywide through 6 August, and stated: "No permission is granted for holding any protest at Chaityabhoumi", citing apprehension of a "likelihood of breach of peace and disturbance of public tranquility". A civil-society fact-finding team held a press conference on 18 July 2026, at which rights activist Firoze Mithiborewala said police gave no reason for the detention and held people for hours. FIRs were subsequently registered against participants. Attribution is clean: the Brihanmumbai Police is a city commissionerate, so this is municipal conduct, unlike several other flags in this cycle. What holds the score up is Action at 2.8: Mumbai delivers water, waste, health and transport to a metropolitan population above 20 million on the largest municipal budget in India.
