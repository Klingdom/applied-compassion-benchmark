## Key Findings

- **Noah Medical scores 26.3 of 100. It measures whether its robot actually finds lung cancer, and it reports the number using the strict definition rather than the generous one.**
- **The multicentre MATCH II study reports 96.7% diagnostic yield under American Thoracic Society strict definitions, and a further analysis presented at CHEST 2025 reports 89% strict diagnostic yield at the first procedure.** Choosing the strict definition lowers your own headline number, which is why Efficacy scores 3 of 5 rather than 2.
- **An independent site checked the claim.** A separate evaluation of 100 consecutive patients at the Cleveland Clinic reported 90% diagnostic yield in routine clinical practice, at a hospital Noah Medical does not control.
- **Nothing was located about the institution behind the product.** No grievance channel, no staff data, no consent architecture, no repair process, no coalition work. Thirty-six of 40 subdimensions rest on absence.
- **No harm was located.** No lawsuit, recall, warning letter or safety incident against Noah Medical was found.
