## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Esper Bionics does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index, and no prior score, seed or placeholder existed for it. There is no published-comparison section because there is nothing to compare against. This report establishes the first number.

**Corporate status check — operating.** Esper Bionics is operating. The European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, a multilateral institution, made a direct equity investment of US$1.25 million (of a US$2.5 million total commitment) in the company in 2024 under project 55187, and the project record remains published. A development-bank investment record is a dated institutional source of the required class. The company also holds a US FDA establishment registration from August 2021 and an FDA GUDID device identification record. No acquisition, insolvency or wind-down was located.

**Disclosure-capacity note.** Esper Bionics is a small company that manufactures in a country under full-scale invasion. It has no legal obligation to publish an annual report, a turnover statistic or a bias audit, and it does not. Its low scores measure that silence. **They do not measure whether the company cares about the amputees it serves.**

**⚠️ Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** No lawsuit, safety incident, regulatory action or labour finding against Esper Bionics was located. Where a score rests on nothing being found, the row says so.

## Key Findings

- **Esper Bionics scores 33.7 of 100 — the highest in this batch of 15 — and almost all of it comes from a development bank.** The European Bank for Reconstruction and Development invested US$1.25 million in 2024 and published a project record naming exactly who the prosthetics market was leaving out. That document, not the company's own material, carries five of this assessment's eight above-floor scores.
- **The clearest finding is about women.** The EBRD record states that 20 to 30 per cent of female amputees in Ukraine cannot use the larger, heavier prostheses on the market, and that Esper's lighter device and the investment's supply commitment are meant to close that gap. An identified group being missed, with a corrective response, is rare in this cohort.
- **Esper Bionics gives away its main product to people who cannot pay, and has done so for four years.** The Esper for Ukraine programme has run since May 2022. More than a hundred of the roughly 200 hands produced went to Ukrainians injured by the invasion, funded by donors. Voice of America, CNN and EBRD all report it independently.
- **No outcome data exists for the device itself.** FDA establishment registration and a GUDID listing are administrative records. No published study measures whether an Esper Hand improves function or quality of life. Efficacy scores 2 of 5 for that reason.
- **Accountability sits at 25 of 100 — the absence floor.** No harm acknowledgment, correction, failure analysis or repair was located. **No adverse finding was located either.** This is a small company at war, with no legal duty to publish any of it.

## Strongest Dimensions

- **Equity, 45.0 — the highest single dimension anywhere in this batch of 15.** Driven by the free Ukrainian programme, the documented female-amputee access gap, and FDA plus PDAC coding.
- **Awareness, 40.0** — the EBRD record supplies both a contextual adaptation and a genuine blind-spot finding.

## Weakest Dimensions

- **Accountability, 25.0 — exactly at the absence floor.** All five subdimensions rest on nothing being found.
- **Empathy, 25.0** — media profiles are not systematic testimony.

## Evidence Gaps

- No clinical outcome data for the Esper Hand.
- No workforce or staff wellbeing data.
- No consent or data-handling disclosure for the Esper Platform, which collects behavioural data from each user's prosthesis.
- No company-published report of any kind disclosing an unflattering finding.
