A London Central employment tribunal ruled, in a judgment made public on 29-30 July 2026, that Goldman Sachs discriminated against Jonathan Reeves on grounds of sex and unfairly dismissed him after he took six months of parental leave under a policy the bank itself offers. The award is £1.45 million, about $1.93 million, and includes compensation for injury to feelings; the tribunal found the stigma of the lawsuit had hampered his job search. ## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: a court has now found that Goldman Sachs punished a man for using the bank’s own parental leave policy.** A London employment tribunal ruled that Goldman discriminated against compliance manager Jonathan Reeves on grounds of sex and unfairly dismissed him in 2022 after six months of parental leave. The judgment became public on 29-30 July 2026.
- **Why it matters: this is a finding, not an allegation.** Unlike most employment matters surfaced in this cycle, a tribunal has decided it. That is why it moves the score at all.
- **The award is £1.45 million, about $1.93 million**, and covers injury to feelings and past and future economic loss. The tribunal said the stigma of bringing the case had hampered his search for another job.
- **The tribunal cut the award by half.** It applied a 50% Polkey reduction because it found there was still a chance Reeves could have been dismissed fairly in any event.
- **Goldman said it "strongly disagree[s] with this decision"** and declined to say whether it would appeal. That posture is what holds Accountability at anchor 2 on harm acknowledgment.

## Strongest Dimensions

**Action (3.4).** Goldman operates well-resourced formal processes with defined standards, and it engaged the tribunal process fully across a multi-year matter.

## Weakest Dimensions

**Equity (2.4).** Even before this ruling, Goldman’s published Equity score was the lowest of its eight dimensions. The tribunal finding is direct evidence that a benefit formally available to all was not equally safe to use. **Empathy (2.8), Accountability (2.8) and Integrity (2.8)** follow.
