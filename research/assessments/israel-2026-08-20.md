## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Israel on the UN Deputy Special Coordinator's Security Council briefing of 11 August 2026, which reported sharply escalated violence in the occupied West Bank by Israeli forces and settlers. Israel was last assessed on 2026-07-25 and sits at the absolute floor of the countries index.

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ATTRIBUTION — the benchmark's standing practice is applied, not re-decided.** This benchmark attributes harm to the acting party. That practice was established and applied to Israel across the 2026-05-14, 2026-05-15, 2026-05-28 and 2026-05-29 floor confirmations (see `research/APPLIED_CHANGES.md`), including the 2026-05-29 formalisation of the AUTHORIZED-RESUMPTION-WITH-SYSTEMATIC-DENIAL sub-category. It is applied here on the same terms and for the same reason: consistency. Acts of Israeli forces are scored as Israeli state conduct. Acts of Israeli settlers are scored as Israeli state conduct **only where the state's own responsibility is engaged** — and the UN record engages it directly, reporting that settlers and Israeli security forces have "often" acted together, and that many incidents occurred in the presence of Israeli forces. Acts of Palestinian armed groups are attributed to those groups and are not scored here.

**DATE-MISMATCH RESOLVED (gate warning 3).** The scanner recorded `evidence_date: 2026-08-11` while listing a un.org/unispal URL carrying **13 August 2026**. Both are correct and there is no conflict. The **conduct and briefing date is 2026-08-11** — the Security Council open debate on West Bank settler violence, confirmed by UNSCO's own page ("Security Council Briefing - 11 August 2026 (West Bank)") and by UN News story 1168113. The 13 August unispal item is an NGO Action News compilation published two days later that reports the same debate. The 11 August date is used.

**Year check.** UN News story path is /en/story/2026/08/; UNSCO's page title carries "11 August 2026". Explicit 2026. No year-confusion found.
