**DATES VERIFIED.** Reuters (via The Spokesman-Review, 2 September 2026): the FAA proposed the penalty in September 2025, Boeing paid in January 2026, and the payment was first disclosed on 2 September 2026. The underlying violations date from September 2023 to February 2024.

That is regulator-verified improvement and moves two subdimensions up. **BASELINE PROVENANCE.** 14.1 is shared by 10 Fortune 500 companies (including Chevron, Exxon Mobil and Sturm Ruger). It was confirmed on 2026-06-09 and 2026-06-10, with no applied change.
