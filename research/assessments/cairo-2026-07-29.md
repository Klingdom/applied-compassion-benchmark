## Assessment Context

- **In-window triggering event verified.** EIPR's press release documents a security crackdown that began 22 June 2026 against at least 20 Shia Muslims in Cairo. EIPR lawyers attended interrogation sessions before the Supreme State Security Prosecution on 14-15 July 2026, after which 19 of the 20 were remanded for a further 15 days pending investigation in Case No. 5635 of 2026, with one released. The interrogations focused on the detainees' religious beliefs.
- **Detention basis.** Before appearing before the prosecution the 20 were held under a preventive-detention order issued under Anti-Terrorism Law No. 94 of 2015, which permits detention for up to 14 days, renewable once, without presentation before a judicial authority.
- **Attribution.** The Supreme State Security Prosecution and the security services are national organs operating in Cairo. Because Cairo in this index is scored as the seat of national administration as well as a municipality, this conduct is scored against the entity. Harms inflicted by non-state actors are not.
- **Rounding note.** The assessed composite is +0.3 against the published value. This is a 0.2-step grid artifact — the published dimension values of 1.5 are not reachable on a five-integer subdimension mean — and is explicitly not an improvement signal. The same artifact appeared for Guayaquil and Mauritania on 2026-07-28.
