## Tennessee's second failed execution of 2026 led to a halt. The halt is real; the system that failed twice was already supposed to be fixed.

On 30 September 2026 Tennessee tried to execute Christa Pike and failed. She received two doses of pentobarbital, survived, and was taken for life-saving care. Governor Bill Lee then halted executions for the rest of his term and ordered a third-party review (WPLN, 2026-10-01).

This was Tennessee's second failed execution in 2026. In May, officials could not place an IV line for Tony Carruthers; the governor gave a one-year reprieve but declined a moratorium. Both failures came after a 2022 independent review found drugs had not been properly tested for years and the system was overhauled.

The halt is credited: Correction Willingness (AB2) stays at 3. The repeat failure after an overhaul lowers Systemic Learning (AB4) from 3 to 2. Tennessee measures 29.4 (Developing), 0.6 below the published 30.0. No proposal.
