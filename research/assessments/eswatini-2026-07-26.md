## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the men were sent to Eswatini by the United States, but they are being held by Eswatini. Under the attribution rule the removal itself is US conduct and does not lower Eswatini's score. The detention without charge, and the denial of confidential access to a lawyer, are Eswatini's own conduct and do.
- Amnesty International reported on 16 July 2026 that at least 30 men have now been transferred under the arrangement, including 11 who arrived on 8 July 2026, and that five of the original men have been held at Matsapha Correctional Complex for a full year.
- Matsapha is a maximum-security prison. None of the men has been charged with an offence in Eswatini.
- The one partial remedy in the past year came from Eswatini's Supreme Court, not from the government. Amnesty called the ruling limited relief.
- Eswatini remains an absolute monarchy in which political parties cannot contest elections, which holds Equity, Accountability and Integrity at the 1.0 floor.
