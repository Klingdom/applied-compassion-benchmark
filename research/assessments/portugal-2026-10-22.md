## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Portugal's published score of 60.6 sits just 0.6 points above the line between Established and Functional. New evidence about how police handled housing protests takes it below that line.
- Before dawn on 20 October 2026, police cleared housing-protest camps in Lisbon and Porto in one national operation. They detained 55 people (Reuters).
- The national police (PSP) called the clearance 'calm, proportionate'. Protesters said they were hit on the head. Amnesty International asked on 15 October 2026 for an independent inquiry into an earlier Porto clearance.
- Parliament backed a bill on 30 September 2026 that would speed up evictions and end rent controls on new leases.
- Portugal measures 59.4 of 100 (Functional). This is a band crossing, so a proposal is filed. Its sources are news reports and Amnesty, none at tier 4 or above, so it is filed as flag-for-review.
