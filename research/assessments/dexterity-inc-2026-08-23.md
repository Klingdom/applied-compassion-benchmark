## Key Findings

- **Dexterity, Inc. scores 25.0 of 100 — the structural floor for a company with no located disclosure and no located harm. Thirty-eight of its 40 subdimensions rest on absence.**
- **Its product replaces one of the most physically punishing jobs in logistics, and nobody has published what that does to the people who did it.** Independent trade reporting describes the dock door as having elevated injury rates. No injury, strain or employment data from Dexterity, FedEx, GXO or Sagawa was located.
- **This is silence by choice, not by constraint.** Dexterity has raised about US$291 million and is valued at US$1.65 billion. It has the resources to commission and publish a worker-outcome study and has not.
- **Compare Exotec in Batch 2, which commissioned an outside consultancy to study operator strain and musculoskeletal risk across client sites.** That single decision moved Exotec above its logistics peers. Nothing equivalent was located for Dexterity.
- **No harm was located.** No lawsuit, recall, regulator action or safety incident against Dexterity was found. A score of 25.0 records silence, not wrongdoing.
