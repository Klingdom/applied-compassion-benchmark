## Key Findings

- **Doosan Robotics scores 26.3 of 100. Its one above-floor practice is that outside bodies have certified how safely its robots behave around the people working next to them.**
- **The certification is specific and independent: ISO 10218-1 compliance, safety-stop and emergency-stop functions rated Performance Level e Category 4 — the highest in the standard — and functional safety certification from TÜV SÜD.** Anticipatory Awareness scores 3 of 5 on that basis.
- **The company publishes bad news because the Korea Exchange requires it.** FY2025 revenue fell 29.58% to KRW 32.98 billion and the net loss widened 51.8% to KRW 55.50 billion. Transparency scores 3 of 5 for that disclosure.
- **The clearest correction in its record was forced.** Doosan Group withdrew the plan to merge Doosan Bobcat into loss-making Doosan Robotics only after retail investors, financial institutions, the National Pension Service and the Financial Supervisory Service objected. Correction Willingness scores 2 of 5 — correction under pressure, not self-initiated.
- **The worker beside the robot never appears in the record.** No consent, data-handling or workplace-effect disclosure was located for a company whose entire product category is defined by robots sharing space with people. Thirty-five of 40 subdimensions rest on absence.
