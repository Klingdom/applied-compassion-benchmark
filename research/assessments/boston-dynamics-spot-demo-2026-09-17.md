# Screening Note: Boston Dynamics (SPOT demo)

**Assessment date:** 2026-09-17
**Outcome:** screened, not assessed. No proposal on either Boston Dynamics row.

## Open founder decision

This row is covered by waiver `D23-boston-dynamics-spot-demo`. The waiver describes it as a deployed configuration misfiled in an organisations index. It is 45.3 points below the company row, and its removal to the Deployed AI Audit product awaits a founder decision (expires 2026-12-14). Nothing in this note pre-empts that decision.

## Why it was flagged

The scanner flagged the row (priority 63) because US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) plans to spend up to $2 million on Boston Dynamics Spot robots. Boston Dynamics said ICE must follow its usual terms, including the ban on weaponising Spot.

## What the evidence shows

- **It is a plan, not a purchase.** ICE's procurement forecast gave an expected solicitation start of 4 September 2026. Boston.com (9 September) reports "the government has not yet issued an RFP for this." WBUR (31 August) calls it the planning stage. No contract, delivery or use was located.
- **The only in-window act by Boston Dynamics is restating existing policy.** Boston.com quotes: "any attempted weaponization of Boston Dynamics' robots is strictly prohibited". WBUR reports the company "will not authorize nor partner with those who wish to use our robots as weapons or autonomous targeting systems," and that law-enforcement customers must adopt a public use policy before deployment.
- **Criticism.** The Boston Globe quotes the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) warning that robots could make ICE raids more reckless. That is advocacy concern about possible future use.

## Which row the evidence attaches to

**The company row, "Boston Dynamics" (65.6), not the SPOT demo row.** The sales decision and the usage terms (weaponisation ban, public-use-policy rule) are company policy. The company row already scores them: B4, S4, S5, I1 and I4 rest on the 2022 non-weaponisation pledge (2026-07-26 confirmation). The SPOT demo row scores a demonstration configuration. An agency's forecast to buy the product is not conduct by that configuration.

**No conduct change on either row.** Screening rule 3: a procurement forecast by a third party (ICE) is not enacted conduct by Boston Dynamics. Restating a standing policy is not new positive conduct. Nothing supports an upgrade or a downgrade. No proposal is filed on either row, so the two rows cannot conflict.
