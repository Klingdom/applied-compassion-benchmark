## Assessment Context

- **In-window event.** On 27 July 2026 a former JPMorgan Chase vice president in leveraged finance, Chirayu Rana, filed an amended 82-page complaint in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York, expanding an existing suit with new claims and new individual defendants and bringing 21 claims under federal and New York law.
- **Allegations are unproven.** The complaint alleges racist mockery of the plaintiff's South Asian and Nepali heritage on an otherwise all-white team, sexual coercion by a senior colleague, retaliation and family-leave violations. JPMorgan has responded that it does not believe there is any merit to the claims, and the individual defendant at the centre of the coercion allegation has denied them and countersued for defamation. Under the standing rule that unresolved litigation is treated as a signal rather than as established fact, these allegations do not on their own drive any dimension below anchor 2.
- **What is scored.** The scoreable institutional conduct is the company's response posture — a blanket denial issued alongside an active countersuit by a serving executive — and the fact that a complaint of this specificity reached federal court rather than being resolved internally.
