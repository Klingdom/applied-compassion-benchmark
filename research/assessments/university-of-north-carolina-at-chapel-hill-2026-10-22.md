## Key Findings

- Why it matters: UNC-Chapel Hill settled 18 federal civil-rights investigations without paying money or admitting fault. The deal locks in the end of its diversity programmes until December 2028.
- Under the 19 October 2026 deal, UNC must hand over Title VI complaints from October 2023 onward and report to the Justice Department every quarter (Higher Ed Dive).
- UNC had already closed all its diversity offices and cut 61 positions by August 2026 (ABC11). It kept the Carolina Covenant, which lets its lowest-income students graduate debt-free.
- UNC measures 28.1 of 100 (Developing), against a published 43.8 (Functional). This is its first measurement.
- 21 of 40 subdimensions have no usable evidence. Scored at 3 instead of 2, UNC would be 41.3, which is Functional. So the band change depends on an unsettled rule.
