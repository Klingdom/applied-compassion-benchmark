# Screening Note: Lithuania

**Assessment date:** 2026-09-24
**Outcome:** screened, not assessed. No proposal. **This is the expected outcome, as the coordinator anticipated.**

## The flagged item is outside the instrument

On 21 September 2026 Lithuania's foreign minister said the European Union has no reason to ease sanctions on Belarus, arguing that Alexander Lukashenko's government continues to repress opponents, violate human rights and support Russia's war in Ukraine.

That is a **diplomatic position about a third state's conduct.** The benchmark measures how an institution recognises, responds to and reduces suffering among the people it is responsible for. It does not score foreign-policy statements about other governments.

## The one subdimension that could conceivably apply, and why it does not

S4 Structural Critique rewards a public position that carries institutional risk (anchor 3) or advocacy against the entity's own short-term interest (anchor 4).

Maintaining an existing EU sanctions line meets neither test. The position is the current EU consensus, it is Lithuania's long-standing stance, it costs Lithuania nothing new, and it is aligned with rather than against Lithuania's own security interest. Restating a standing position is not new conduct — the same reasoning this cycle applied to Boston Dynamics restating its weaponisation ban.

Scoring this would also set a bad precedent: any government's press statement criticising another government would become a scoring event, which would reward rhetoric over treatment of people.

## What was checked and not found

Lithuania's own in-window record was searched for, not only the flagged statement. **No in-window conduct by the Lithuanian state toward people in its care was located** — no finding on the Belarus border pushback practice that has drawn criticism in prior years, no asylum or detention decision, no domestic rights finding dated inside 2026-09-10 to 2026-09-24. The absence of adverse evidence is recorded as an absence and is not scored as a positive.
