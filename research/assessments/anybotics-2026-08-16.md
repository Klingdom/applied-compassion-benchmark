## Key Findings

- Why it matters: AnyBotics has the clearest measurable worker-safety benefit in the cluster, and almost no institutional disclosure. Those two facts pull its score in opposite directions.
- One ANYmal robot completed more than 33,000 inspections at Vigier Ciment’s 150-year-old Swiss cement plant since early 2025, working nights across 450 checkpoints in narrow stairways, dust and heat — work that previously exposed people to those conditions.
- ANYmal X is described as the world’s first Ex-certified legged robot, meaning an independent body certified it for atmospheres containing flammable gas, vapour or dust. That is the only third-party product certification found in this cluster.
- AnyBotics publishes no responsibility report, no workforce data, no failure disclosure and no reparative-action record. Its entire Accountability dimension scores 2.0 on absence of disclosure, not on any located misconduct.
- No adverse conduct of any kind was located for AnyBotics — no lawsuits, no safety incidents, no labour findings.

## Strongest Dimensions

Awareness (3.0) and Action (3.0). Hazard detection is literally the product, and the deployment record is quantified by third parties: 33,000-plus inspections at one plant, over 200 robots shipped, thousands of weekly inspections at Equinor, ENI, Petrobras and Northern Lights. Systemic Thinking (2.8) follows, because fugitive-emission detection connects worker safety to climate harm.

## Weakest Dimensions

Accountability (2.0), the only dimension where every one of the five subdimensions scores at the low anchor. AnyBotics publishes nothing about its own performance, failures, corrections or repair. Equity (2.2), Boundaries (2.2), Empathy (2.2) and Integrity (2.2) are close behind for the same reason.

## Evidence Gaps

This is a private Swiss company with no published responsibility report, no workforce disclosure and no independent audit. Twenty-four of the forty subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure. Two of the strongest positive findings — the "world’s first Ex-certified legged robot" claim and the $150 million funding total — rest on tier-2 sources, a company-partner press release and a trade aggregator, and the Ex certificate itself was not obtained. The 33,000-inspection figure comes from a tier-3 trade outlet and was not verified against Vigier Ciment.
