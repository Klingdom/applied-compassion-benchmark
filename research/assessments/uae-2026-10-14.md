## Human Rights Watch asked the EU to press the UAE on Sudan, migrant workers and jailed critics. This is advocacy, not new UAE conduct; the first subdimension scoring confirms Critical.

Human Rights Watch (HRW) wrote to EU leaders on 7 October 2026 ahead of the EU-Gulf summit. It asked the EU to press the UAE to end support to Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (RSF). It also said at least 83 people are jailed for peaceful expression.

The letter is advocacy, not a new UAE act. But the UAE had no subdimension-level sidecar: its June 2026 reports scored dimensions only. This cycle scores all 40 subdimensions for the first time, using HRW's World Report 2026 chapter and the letter.
