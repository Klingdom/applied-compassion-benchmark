## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Yemen on the UN Emergency Relief Coordinator's Security Council briefing of 13 August 2026, which reported that more than half of Yemen's population faces acute food insecurity with the humanitarian appeal only 20 percent funded. Yemen was last assessed on 2026-07-16 and is ranked last of 193 countries at the absolute floor.

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ATTRIBUTION — three distinct actors must be separated in Yemen, and this assessment separates them.**

1. **The Houthi de facto authorities** control the areas where most of the population lives and are responsible for aid-access obstruction, detention of UN and NGO staff, and — per the scan — missile strikes on the Red Sea port of Mocha. This is scored as governing-authority conduct.
2. **The internationally recognised government (IRG)** controls the south and east and holds the formal seat. Its own conduct — currency collapse, unpaid public-sector salaries, service breakdown in areas it administers — is scored against it.
3. **Foreign actors** (regional coalition members, external strike campaigns) are third parties. Harm they cause is attributed to them, not to Yemen, per the benchmark's standing attribution practice.

The published 0.0 reflects the aggregate of the first two. Nothing in this cycle changes that split.

**Date check.** The OCHA briefing page carries "13 August 2026" in its own title and URL slug. The GlobalSecurity mirror is dated 2026-08-13. Both are inside the window and both carry an explicit 2026 year. No year-confusion found. Direct retrieval of unocha.org returned HTTP 403 to the automated fetcher, so quotes below are limited to what is reproduced in the page title and in independently dated secondary reporting; no quote is reconstructed from memory.

- Yemen holds at 0 of 100 and remains ranked last of 193 countries. No further decline can be recorded.
