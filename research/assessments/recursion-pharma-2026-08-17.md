## Key findings

- Recursion is the only lab in this study that killed its own lead drug because the data said so. It stopped four programmes, including REC-994, saying the totality of the data supported discontinuing the studies outright — and it published the null result from the SYCAMORE extension rather than burying it.
- It gives its data away. Six open datasets and the OpenPhenom-S/16 foundation model are free on HuggingFace and Google Cloud, and RxRx3-core was deliberately kept under 18Gb so that researchers without large infrastructure could use it.
- Being a listed company matters here. Securities and Exchange Commission filings disclose the negative trial data, the pipeline cuts, the 20% workforce reduction and its $11 million cost. That is published institutional data including failures — something no private lab in this study provides.
- The cost side is real: about 160 of roughly 800 employees were cut in March 2026, the second reduction in sixteen months.
- Recursion scores 40.0, second highest in the study, and still falls 20.9 points from the published 60.9. The placeholder was never measured.
