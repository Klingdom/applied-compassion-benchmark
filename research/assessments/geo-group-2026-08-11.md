## Why this entity was assessed tonight

The scanner flagged GEO Group on the death of Edwin Lopez-Cornejo, 41, at the company-operated Delaney Hall Detention Facility in Newark, New Jersey on 1 August 2026 — the second death at that facility since it reopened. The scan gate attached a blocking condition: the accompanying record-setting claim ("ICE detention deaths at a 22-year record pace") rested partly on undated sourcing, and had to be independently corroborated with a dated primary source before it could move a score.

## Scanner date / attribution corrections

- **Record-setting claim: CORROBORATED, with a scope limit.** TIME, dated 2026-08-10 in its URL, states that "The fatality rate in ICE custody reached a 22-year high in the opening months of this fiscal year," sourcing the finding to a study published in JAMA. This clears the mandatory pre-scoring resolution. However, the claim describes **U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement system-wide**, not GEO Group specifically. It is scored as context, not as GEO conduct. GEO-specific conduct evidence is the Delaney Hall death, the family's medical-neglect allegation, and the Human Rights Watch findings.
- **Date check: no error found.** The scan recorded evidence_date 2026-08-01 against URLs dated 2026-08-04 (CNN) and 2026-08-10 (TIME). The underlying event — the death — did occur on 1 August 2026, and the URLs carry publication dates of follow-on coverage. The scanner's evidence_date is correct as an event date; both URL dates are correct as publication dates. No correction required.
- **Math-hygiene gap carried forward, NOT a conduct finding.** GEO Group's published composite is 6.6, but canonical reconstruction from its own published dimensions (all eight at 1.5) returns 12.5 — a 5.9-point gap first recorded on 2026-07-27. This is an index-arithmetic defect requiring coordinator-level correction. It is deliberately kept separate from tonight's conduct assessment, which is scored against the published 6.6.

## Key Findings

- A man died in a GEO Group facility after his lawyers had already raised medical alarms. Edwin Lopez-Cornejo, 41, died on 1 August 2026 at Delaney Hall in Newark, New Jersey, after about six weeks in custody. He was the second person to die at that facility since it reopened.
- The company would not answer questions from Human Rights Watch. HRW wrote to GEO Group with preliminary findings and an offer to reply for its 25 June 2026 report "Dying in Detention." GEO did not respond.
- Deaths in immigration detention are at a 22-year high — but that is a government-wide number, not a GEO number. TIME reported on 10 August 2026 that "The fatality rate in ICE custody reached a 22-year high in the opening months of this fiscal year." That statistic describes ICE, so this assessment counts it as context, not as GEO conduct.
- Revenue rose as deaths rose. GEO Group and CoreCivic together reported about $1.4 billion in quarterly revenue while the detained population grew roughly 70%, and ICE cut back its public reporting of deaths.
- The published score of 6.6 does not match the benchmark's own arithmetic. Rebuilding it from GEO's eight published dimension scores returns 12.5. That is a maths error in the index, not a finding about GEO, and it needs a separate correction.
