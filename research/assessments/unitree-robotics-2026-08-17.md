### Score Difference Analysis

Unitree has just acquired a real public disclosure surface. Its Shanghai STAR Market listing on 6 August 2026 forced audited financials and explicit risk warnings into the open, which lifts Accountability slightly above the baseline. Set against that is the most concrete consent failure found anywhere in this study. CVE-2025-2894 describes a hidden remote-access service in Go1 firmware that started on boot and handed anyone with the right key a live camera, microphone and sensor feed. Independent researchers found it running on robots inside four universities. Unitree did not find it, and no acknowledgment or remediation statement was located. That produces the study's only two scores of 1 outside the accountability dimension, and it drags Awareness and Boundaries to 1.8. This assessment supersedes the backfill of 2026-07-29, which reached 34.4 using five sources all concerning the same US import-ban story. That file's near-agreement with the seed reflected a narrow evidence base rather than a test of it.

### Recommendation

The published score is overstated and should be revised down to 24.4. The band does not change. The composite delta of -11.5 clears the filing threshold. Unlike most entities in this study, part of this movement is a genuine conduct finding: a documented covert remote-access capability in shipped consumer hardware.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: Unitree shipped robots that could be watched and listened to by strangers.** CVE-2025-2894 describes a hidden "CloudSail" service in Go1 firmware that started on boot and gave anyone with the right key full camera, audio and sensor access.
- **Independent researchers found it, not Unitree.** They confirmed the service active on robots at MIT, Princeton, Carnegie Mellon University and the University of Waterloo.
- **No acknowledgment or fix statement from Unitree was located.** That is why this scores as a consent failure rather than as absence of disclosure.
- **Unitree is now a listed company with real disclosure duties.** It priced its Shanghai STAR Market listing on 6 August 2026 at a roughly $9 billion valuation, with the retail tranche oversubscribed more than 8,000 times.
- **The US closed its market to new Unitree devices on 28 July 2026.** The Federal Communications Commission added foreign advanced robotic devices to its Covered List, blocking the authorisation needed for import or sale.

The STAR Market prospectus itself was not read; its figures come from news coverage. No Chinese-language sources were searched, which may understate this entity.

## Scoring Conventions Applied

- **Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is used only where positive documented evidence of a specific failure exists. This is the "2-convention", the more conservative of the two conventions live in this repository.
- **A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review. A score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.**
- 37 of 40 subdimensions sit at 2. Of those, approximately 26 rest on absence of disclosure rather than on located evidence of a practice.
