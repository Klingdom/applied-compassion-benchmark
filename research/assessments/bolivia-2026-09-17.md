## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**BASELINE PROVENANCE (read first).** Bolivia's 6.3 came from applied downgrades between 2026-05-26 and 2026-06-14, each on the government's own conduct. That conduct includes the June law authorising military force against protesters and the state of exception declared on 20 June. A 2026-07-11 upgrade proposal to 15.9 (`bolivia.json`) was REJECTED. The Critical-band calibration flag opened on 2026-07-11 is still open. No proposal is pending.

**WHAT IS NEW AND ENACTED.** The executive approved the 90-day extension on Monday 14 September (EFE, 15 September). The Legislative Assembly approved it by absolute majority early on 17 September, with 100 of 158 members in favour after a nine-hour session (EFE, 17 September). The extension continues an emergency regime already priced into 6.3. It does not add a new category of harm.

**WHAT IS ONLY CONTEMPLATED.** A state of siege, possibly with a curfew, was described as an option the decree allows. It has not been declared. Per screening rule 3 and the coordinator's caution, a plan is not scored as conduct.

**LEGISLATIVE REVIEW IS NOT AN UPGRADE.** The Assembly's vote shows a procedural check working. But it ratified the extension, and the rule bars an upgrade on anything other than new positive conduct. No subdimension moves up.

**SCORING CONVENTION.** Starts from the 2026-09-14 sidecar (grid rounding of the published profile). Measured 5.0 against published 6.3.
