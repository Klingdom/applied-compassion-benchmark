## Key Findings

- **Avidbots Corp. scores 25.0 of 100 — the structural floor for a company with no located disclosure and no located harm. Thirty-eight of its 40 subdimensions rest on absence.**
- **Avidbots says its robots free cleaners for higher-skilled work rather than replacing them, and publishes nothing to show it.** No employment data, workload study or custodial-worker survey was located. Independent trade reporting notes that staff anxiety about displacement is the most consistent barrier to adoption — the worry is documented, the reassurance is not.
- **Neo navigates hospitals, airports and malls using cameras and 3D sensors, and no data-handling policy for that was located.** The buyer is the facility; the people recorded are cleaners, patients and passengers.
- **Its most-cited evidence is an award about somebody else.** Avidbots reports that six of the world's top ten cleanest airports use Neo. That ranks the airport, not the robot's effect on the people who work there.
- **No harm was located.** No lawsuit, recall, regulator action or safety incident against Avidbots was found. A score of 25.0 records silence, not wrongdoing.
