- **Why it matters: nothing happened in this window, and that is the finding.** A dedicated search across lawsuits, labour, safety, regulatory and humanitarian signals found no compassion-relevant Mauritius evidence between 16 and 30 July 2026.
- **Why it matters: Mauritius spends more than a third of its budget on social protection.** The Bertelsmann Transformation Index 2026 country report records that Mauritius has consistently consolidated its welfare state, with over one third of the budget going to social security, education and health.
- **Standing context (February 2026, outside the window): the Chagossians remain poor and stigmatised.** Human Rights Watch reported that Chagossians forcibly displaced to Mauritius before independence continue to suffer poverty, stigma and discrimination half a century later.
- **Standing context (2024, outside the window):** the UN Human Rights Council Working Group on the Universal Periodic Review noted that the Equal Opportunities Act fails to protect transgender persons from discrimination.
- **Mauritius ranks 41st of 193 countries at 60.0 out of 100 — Functional.** Its strongest dimension is Boundaries at 3.8; its weakest is Equity at 2.8, driven by the Chagossian situation and by gaps in anti-discrimination law.
