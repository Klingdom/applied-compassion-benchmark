## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Beijing Geekplus Technology Co., Ltd. does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index, and no prior score, seed or placeholder existed for it. There is no published-comparison section because there is nothing to compare against. This report establishes the first number.

**Corporate status check — operating.** Beijing Geekplus Technology Co., Ltd. is operating and publicly listed. It listed on the Main Board of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong on 9 July 2025 under stock code 2590, in the largest robotics initial public offering on that exchange to date, raising HK$2.358 billion at HK$16.80 per share. It files with HKEXnews and published full-year 2025 results reporting revenue of RMB 3.171 billion, up 31.6 per cent, and gross margin of 35.5 per cent. An exchange listing and filing record is the strongest available class of status source. **Note the naming trap: the trading name "Geek+" reduces to the bare token `geek` under the canonical slugger, so the full legal name is used and the slug is `geekplus`.**

That obligation gives it a disclosure floor its private competitors do not have. It is the reason its Accountability score sits above the absence floor — not a finding that it behaves better.

## Key Findings

- **Geekplus scores 25.6 of 100, and the only reason it clears the absence floor is that Hong Kong makes it file.** Beijing Geekplus Technology listed on the HKEX Main Board on 9 July 2025 and is bound by the exchange's Environmental, Social and Governance Reporting Code. Its audited filings disclose unflattering material — adjusted EBITDA turned positive at only RMB 11.62 million on RMB 1.025 billion of half-year revenue.
- **Compelled disclosure is worth about half a point here.** Seven of eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor of 25.0. Only Accountability rises, to 30.0, on filings the company must publish by law.
- **Geekplus describes its own effect as shifting logistics away from manual labour, and publishes no analysis of what that does to workers.** The phrase appears in company ESG communications. No employment figures, working-conditions study or transition provision were located.
- **The people who work next to these robots did not buy them.** The warehouse operator does. No consent or worker-data disclosure was located for systems that set human retrieval pace.
- **A naming trap worth recording.** The trading name "Geek+" reduces to the bare token `geek` under the benchmark's canonical slugger. The full legal name is used and the slug is `geekplus`, to avoid a collision that would be very hard to unpick later.
