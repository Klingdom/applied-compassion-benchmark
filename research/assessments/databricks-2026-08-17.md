### Band-change evidence test

**Result: PASS.** Federal court rulings in Nazemian et al. v. Databricks (N.D. Cal.) reported by Bloomberg Law and Publishers Lunch (tier 4), plus the company's published AI Governance Framework and Responsible AI documentation (tier 3). Multiple independent sources, at least one at tier 4.

## Key findings

- Databricks publishes more governance material than any other lab in Cluster B. Its AI Governance Framework and responsible AI documentation set out an ethics, transparency and interpretability pillar, and it gives them away for the whole field to use.
- None of it is measured. There is no outcome data on whether the framework changes anything, no workforce data, no failure disclosure, and no independent audit. Databricks is private and discloses accordingly.
- Authors sued Databricks in March 2024 alleging its MosaicML models were trained on Books3, a dataset of nearly 197,000 pirated books. Courts have split the case: the direct claims over DBRX were dismissed, and the claims over MPT continue. Nothing is decided and nothing here is scored as proven harm.
- The published 48.4 implied formal pathways and some outcome data across all eight areas. Databricks has framework documents and no outcome data at all.
- The score falls 17.1 points, the smallest fall in Cluster B, because Databricks genuinely publishes more than its cluster peers.

## Strongest dimensions

- **Awareness (AWR) at 2.4** and **Boundaries (BND) at 2.4**. These are the only dimensions where located positive evidence exceeds the absence baseline by a clear margin.

## Weakest dimensions

- **Integrity (INT) at 2** and **Accountability (ACC) at 2.2**. These rest almost entirely on absence of disclosure rather than on located failure.

## Evidence gaps

- 30 of 40 subdimensions are scored at 2 because no evidence was located in either direction. Under the fixed standard used across all 15 labs in this study, absence of disclosure scores 2, not 1.
- No independent audit and no beneficiary or community testimony of a working compassion practice was located. Nothing in this assessment scores 5 on any subdimension.
- This is a desk-based assessment. If Databricks operates grievance channels, disaggregated outcome review or reparative processes without publishing them, this assessment understates it.
