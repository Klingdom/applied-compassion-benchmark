## Key Findings

- Why it matters: more than half of Pakistan's monsoon deaths came from houses falling on people, and that is a building problem, not a weather problem. The National Disaster Management Authority recorded 109 dead and 362 injured since 26 June 2026, reported on 27 July 2026, with house collapses the leading cause.
- This assessment is anchored to the Pakistan assessment of 24 July 2026, which scored 16.9. Only genuinely new evidence was scored, so the 24 July baseline is not re-litigated.
- What is new: the toll roughly doubled from 47 on 22 July to 109 by 27 July; the authority published cause-of-death and provincial breakdowns; and it issued a forward warning for early August.
- Positive in-window conduct is real. Rescue teams carried out 130 operations and rescued 3,841 people nationwide. That holds Awareness at raw 2.0.
- Assessed composite 16.3 against a published 17.2. Delta -0.9, below the 5-point proposal threshold.

## Strongest Dimensions

- Awareness and Empathy (both raw 2.0). The disaster authority publishes running, disaggregated national figures and issues forward warnings.

## Weakest Dimensions

- Accountability (raw 1.0, the floor). No acknowledgment that house-collapse deaths reflect a housing and building-standards failure, and no systemic review after previous seasons.
