- The OCC cited "systemic breakdowns in its suspicious activity monitoring and reporting processes" and said the bank failed to timely identify and report "approximately $13 billion of suspected trade-based money laundering activity" ([OCC NR 2026-87](https://occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-87.html)).
- Banking Dive dates that activity "between June 2014 and May 2025" and reports a concurrent Federal Reserve cease-and-desist. CEO Stephen Squeri said: "While we have made meaningful progress, we know there is more work to do." ([Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/amex-dinged-by-fed-occ-must-pay-350m-for-aml-failures/832570/), 2026-10-08, tier 2).
