## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Somalia on heavy fighting in Baidoa city centre on 17 August 2026 between Somali federal troops and armed opposition loyal to the South West state's former president, in which at least two to four civilians were killed by stray bullets. Somalia was last assessed on 2026-07-10 and sits near the floor of the countries index at 4.7 of 100.

## Evidence-date and attribution checks

**ATTRIBUTION — the assault was launched by a non-state militia; what is scored is the state's conduct in responding.** Militias loyal to former South West state president Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen attacked federal positions before dawn on 17 August 2026 using vehicles laden with explosives. That attack is **not** state conduct and is not scored against Somalia. What **is** scored is the Somali federal government's own conduct: choosing to fight a pitched urban battle in the centre of a city of over a million people that hosts hundreds of thousands of displaced people, and the resulting civilian deaths from stray bullets. Al-Shabaab conduct elsewhere in Somalia is likewise third-party conduct and is not scored here.

**Date checks.** US News carries 2026-08-17 in its URL path; Al Jazeera carries /2026/8/17/; Times of Earth carries /2026/08/18/. All explicit 2026, all inside the window. No year-confusion found. The MSF Baidoa malnutrition survey is dated to July 2026 by the scanner and is used as context, not as the triggering event.

**Casualty range.** Sources differ: Al Jazeera and US News report at least two civilians killed in their homes by stray bullets; a local elder is quoted describing ten dead including four civilians. The assessment uses the lower, directly reported figure of two civilians and notes the higher elder account as unverified.

## Floor limitation

**Somalia is near the arithmetic floor.** Five of its eight published dimensions — Equity, Boundaries, Accountability, Systemic Thinking and Integrity — are already at 1.0, the minimum anchor. Only three dimensions (Awareness, Empathy, Action) carry any headroom at all, and each sits at 1.5. **Meaningful further downgrade is very nearly unavailable**: the maximum possible remaining downward movement from 4.7 is 4.7 points, less than the 5-point filing trigger. In practice Somalia can only be confirmed or moved upward.

### Analysis

The assessed composite of 5.0 sits 0.3 points above the published 4.7 and in the same Critical band. No filing trigger is met. The small upward difference comes from crediting two genuine state acts that the published vector does not appear to reflect: the November 2025 national drought emergency declaration (a detection and anticipatory act made before a famine declaration) and the negotiated March 2026 transfer of Baidoa. Those credits are offset by the Empathy and Action dimensions scoring at or below their published values, because the federal government neither acknowledged the civilian deaths it caused in Baidoa nor measures any outcome of its response.

## Key Findings

- Somalia holds at about 5 of 100. Five of its eight dimensions are already at the minimum, so the most the score could fall is 4.7 points — less than the 5-point threshold needed to file a change. In practice this entity can only be confirmed or moved up.
- Somali federal troops fought a pitched battle in the centre of Baidoa on 17 August 2026. At least two civilians were killed by stray bullets in their own homes.
- The attack was launched by a militia loyal to the former state president, not by the government. What the benchmark scores is the government's choice to fight in a city hosting hundreds of thousands of displaced people, and the civilian deaths that followed.
- One in every two children screened at Baidoa displacement sites is acutely malnourished. One in four is severely malnourished. Those numbers come from Doctors Without Borders, not from the state.
- Somalia's government did declare a national drought emergency in November 2025 before a famine was declared. That is real anticipatory action and it is credited here.

## Strongest Dimensions

Empathy (EMP, raw 1.60) and Action (ACT, raw 1.40) are marginally the strongest, reflecting the negotiated March 2026 transfer of Baidoa and a fast military response to the August assault. Both remain deep in the Critical band.

## Weakest Dimensions

Equity (EQU), Boundaries (BND), Accountability (ACC) and Integrity (INT) all sit at exactly 1.00 — every subdimension at the minimum anchor. Accountability is the most firmly evidenced: no acknowledgment, no data, no repair for civilians killed by state forces.

## Evidence Gaps

- Civilian casualty figures for the Baidoa fighting differ between sources; the assessment uses the lower directly reported figure of two and treats a local elder's account of ten dead as unverified.
- No Somali federal government statement on civilian casualties was located.
- The MSF Baidoa survey date is taken from the scanner as July 2026; the MSF page itself does not carry a machine-readable date, so it is used as context and is not load-bearing.
