## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the harm here was federal, and Detroit’s officials objected to it. About a week before 15 July 2026, three Detroit officials wrote to the Department of Homeland Security urging an end to dangerous vehicle pursuits in residential neighbourhoods.
- District 7 police commissioner Victoria Camille said she did not see why a vehicle stop would be necessary for someone regarding simply an immigration matter at all.
- Immigration and Customs Enforcement paused non-urgent traffic stops after two people were killed in separate ICE-related encounters in under a week. President Trump publicly criticised the pause and it was reversed.
- The attribution rule applies. Federal enforcement conduct and its reversal are not Detroit’s conduct and do not move Detroit’s score in either direction.
- The Detroit officials’ letter is dated about 8 July 2026, before the 13 July window opened. It is recorded as labelled background, not as in-window positive conduct, so no upgrade was derived from it.
