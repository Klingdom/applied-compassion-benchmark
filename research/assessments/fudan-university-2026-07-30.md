## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: Fudan was flagged for something the Pentagon did, not something Fudan did.** The U.S. Department of War added Fudan University to its research-security list on 23 July 2026. That bars U.S. federally funded researchers from working with Fudan. It says nothing about how Fudan treats students, staff or the public.
- **Why it matters: importing that judgment would be a category error.** A national-security listing by one government is not evidence about an institution’s compassion practice. The claim was screened out and no score moved.
- **The listing was large and not specific to Fudan.** It covered 130 institutions across China, Russia and Iran, including 88 in mainland China, and was the largest single-cycle expansion in the list’s history.
- **Standing context (December 2019, outside the window):** Fudan revised its charter to remove the phrase "freedom of thought", which had been in it since 2005, and inserted a pledge of loyalty to the Communist Party of China. Students staged a rare on-campus protest chanting "freedom of thought". This remains the strongest available evidence on Fudan’s own conduct and it is more than six years old.
- **Fudan is large and highly ranked.** It reports 15,420 undergraduate students, 36,012 graduate students and 3,647 teaching and research staff, and placed 30th in the QS World University Rankings 2026.
