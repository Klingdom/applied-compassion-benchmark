### Recommendation

**CONFIRM**. The composite delta of -1.9 points is inside the 5-point confirmation threshold, so the published score of 12.5 is confirmed.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the border-war evidence that flagged Cambodia this cycle was mis-dated. Human Rights Watch places the fighting that displaced around 300,000 people in July 2025, with further clashes in December 2025 — not in July 2026.
- Cambodia’s in-window record is still severe. Human Rights Watch’s World Report 2026 finds that "Civil society’s ability to operate is now almost entirely constrained".
- The anti-scam crackdown looks like activity rather than help. Fewer than one in five identified scam compounds was investigated, and some trapped victims were not released.
- The assessed score of 10.6 sits 1.9 points below the published 12.5 — inside the 5-point threshold, so the critical-band score is confirmed.
