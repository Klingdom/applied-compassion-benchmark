## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the ransomware claim is not evidence. A criminal group posted a breach claim on 17 September 2026 and plaintiffs' lawyers are investigating. AECOM has not confirmed it and no forensic source has. It scores nothing.
- Why it matters: AECOM has a real, proven safety record. Its total recordable incident rate fell 27% and its lost workday case rate 33% since fiscal 2020, and it holds the RoSPA Order of Distinction after 16 straight Gold Awards.
- Why it matters: it also has a proven fraud record. AECOM paid $11.8 million on 24 October 2023 to settle United States claims that it knowingly submitted false claims to FEMA for Hurricane Katrina rebuilding.
- Why it matters: the published 48.4 is a shared seed value. Eighty-three Fortune 500 companies carry exactly 48.4. The measured 47.5 is within one point of it, so the seed holds up on evidence in this case.
