## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Romania (priority 77) on a livestock farmers' protest in Bucharest on 15 September 2026. The protest was over the handling of peste des petits ruminants (PPR) and sheep pox outbreaks and the extended ban on live sheep exports. Gendarmes used tear gas after protesters broke through a cordon. Romania was last assessed on 2026-06-03 (rotation confirmation, no acute event).

## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**BASELINE PROVENANCE (read first).** Published 37.5 (Developing, rank 74) has never moved. `APPLIED_CHANGES.md` has no Romania entry, and there is no Romania change proposal on disk. The 2026-06-03 rotation confirmation held the profile unchanged (EQU 2.0 laggard on Roma treatment). The vector is near-uniform (six of eight dimensions at exactly 2.5), so it is a seed-style profile. No stale-baseline argument is used here.

**ATTRIBUTION: the export ban is an EU measure, not a Romanian one.** Balkan Insight and Al Jazeera both attribute the live-export ban to the European Commission, imposed after PPR and sheep pox outbreaks. The ban is not scored as Romanian conduct. Only the Romanian state's own conduct is scored: the policing of the protest, the government's statements, its outbreak and compensation response, and its joint initiative with other member states.

**POLICING.** Al Jazeera and France 24 (both 15 September) report tear gas, 26 detained and 24 hurt, including 8 gendarmes. Balkan Insight reports the gendarmes acted after demonstrators 'broke through security cordons and threw objects at officers'. Radio Romania International (16 September) later reports 47 people taken into custody. No source located reports live ammunition, deaths, or an independent finding of excessive force. The response is not scored as a new harm.

**PLANS ARE NOT CONDUCT.** The acting Agriculture Minister said a draft outbreak action plan and a draft sheep-sector law with compensation were ready for a Council of Ministers meeting at month end. These are drafts. They do not move any subdimension upward (screening rule 3).

**DIRECTIONALITY.** Romania surfaced on negative evidence. No move upward was made. No downward move is supported either, because the state conduct located is a contested but not unlawful public-order response.

## Key Findings

- Romania's published score of 37.5 out of 100 holds. On 15 September 2026 about 2,000 livestock farmers protested in Bucharest, and gendarmes used tear gas after a cordon was breached.
- The live-sheep export ban behind the protest was imposed by the European Commission, not by Romania. It is not scored against Romania.
- Farmers were promised 100 euros per hectare in drought aid but will get 19 euros. That broken promise is recorded, but its date is unknown, so it does not move the score.
- The government's new outbreak plan and sheep-sector law are still drafts. Drafts are not counted as help delivered.
