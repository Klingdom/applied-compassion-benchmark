## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. Exotec does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index, and no prior score, seed or placeholder existed for it. There is no published-comparison section because there is nothing to compare against. This report establishes the first number.

**Corporate status check — operating.** Exotec is operating. Founded in 2015 and based in Croix, near Lille, it reports roughly €300 million in annual revenue with Skypod systems deployed at more than 200 customer sites, and became a unicorn in 2022 after a US$335 million Series D that valued it at about US$2 billion. Independent trade and financial reporting confirms continued trading, including a Decathlon deployment across seven European sites. No acquisition, insolvency or wind-down was located.

**Disclosure-capacity note.** Exotec is a private French company. France's corporate duty-of-vigilance law applies only to much larger employers, so Exotec has no obligation to publish a social report and does not publish one. Its low score measures that absence.

**⚠️ Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** No lawsuit, safety incident, regulatory action or labour ruling against Exotec was located. Where a score rests on nothing being found, the row says so.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **32 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** (80%) and 8 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **Exotec scores 26.9 of 100 and is the strongest of the three warehouse-automation companies in this batch, for one reason: it paid someone to go and look at what its system does to workers.** Exotec states that the consultancy Action Spinoza studied the social impact of Skypod across its client sites in 2025 and found reduced musculoskeletal disorder risk and physical strain.
- **That is the only human-outcome measurement located anywhere in this batch's logistics group.** Locus Robotics claims injury reduction and publishes no data. Geekplus describes shifting logistics away from manual labour and publishes no employment analysis. Exotec commissioned a study.
- **The study is company-commissioned and company-reported, and the underlying report was not located.** That caps it at 3 of 5 rather than 4. If Exotec published the full study, the same evidence would score higher.
- **Exotec says the human role changes rather than disappears — and unusually, it did not simply assert that.** Most of this batch makes a version of that claim with nothing behind it.
- **Thirty-one of 40 subdimensions still rest on absence.** No workforce data, no consent disclosure for worker-facing systems, no report containing an unflattering finding, and no independent verification of anything were located. **No adverse finding was located either.**
