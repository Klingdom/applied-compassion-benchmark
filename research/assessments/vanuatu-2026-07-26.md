## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Vanuatu won a legal argument that its people cannot yet drink, switch on or shelter behind. Commentary published on 24 July 2026 found the International Court of Justice victory has not yet produced repaired water systems, restored electricity or coastal protection for communities facing recurring floods and landslides.
- That gap is not new conduct and does not move the score. Vanuatu's published score was raised on 23 May 2026 on the strength of the General Assembly follow-up resolution it led. The delivery gap is already carried in low Action and Equity values of raw 2.4 and 2.2.
- No new positive conduct was found inside the window either. Under anti-false-positive screening, the absence of new harm is not evidence for an upgrade, so no movement was manufactured in either direction.
- Vanuatu is a rare case where Systemic Thinking, at raw 3.4, is the highest dimension and Equity, at raw 2.2, is the lowest. The country is better at changing the rules than at delivering under them.
- A math-hygiene warning is raised for index maintenance: the published composite of 41.4 does not reconstruct from the published dimensions, which yield 42.8.
