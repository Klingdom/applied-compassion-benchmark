## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. AgiBot does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index.

**Corporate status check (required before assessment).** AgiBot, also known as Zhiyuan Robotics, is operating under that name, headquartered in Shanghai and founded by Peng Zhihui. It shipped 5,168 units in 2025 — about 39% of the global humanoid market according to Omdia — and announced its 10,000th robot on 30 March 2026, three months after reaching 5,000 ([AgiBot, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgiBot); [TechTimes, AgiBot at WAIC 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320899/20260718/agibot-debuts-four-robots-waic-2026-global-export-push-meets-chinas-spy-law-obligation.htm)). No acquisition, rename or insolvency event was found.

**⚠️ Almost every low score rests on absence of disclosure, not on evidence of harm.** No lawsuit, safety incident, labour ruling or regulatory enforcement action against AgiBot was located. **Where a score rests on absence, the row says so.**

**A structural risk, recorded but not scored as harm.** Independent analysis notes that AgiBot units carry a legal obligation under China's National Intelligence Law that creates structural data-access exposure, and that proposed US legislation — the American Security Robotics Act of March 2026 — names AgiBot in sponsor statements. **This is a legal-context finding, not a documented instance of misconduct**, so it does not push any subdimension below the absence baseline. It is recorded because it bears directly on consent and data handling and should be re-checked as legislation progresses.

**Disclosure-regime caveat.** AgiBot is headquartered in a jurisdiction that does not require the social reporting this benchmark reads. Part of the drag on transparency-dependent subdimensions is structural rather than behavioural.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **39 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 1 rests on positive located evidence. **This is the thinnest entity in the batch.**

## Key Findings

- **AgiBot is the thinnest entity in this batch: 39 of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure.** It ships more humanoid robots than any company in the world — about 39% of global share, 10,000 cumulative by March 2026 — and publishes essentially nothing this benchmark can read about their effect on people.
- **This is a disclosure finding, not a conduct finding.** No lawsuit, safety incident, labour ruling or regulatory enforcement action against AgiBot was located anywhere.
- **The single piece of positive evidence is a safety certification.** China's CR001 certification covers mechanical and electrical safety, electromagnetic compatibility and functional safety — a real third-party pre-market assessment, and the only reason Awareness sits above the floor.
- **A structural data-access risk is recorded but not scored as harm.** Independent analysis notes AgiBot units carry obligations under China's National Intelligence Law, and proposed US legislation names the company. **No misuse was documented**, so the benchmark does not treat this as evidence of harm. It is flagged for re-checking.
- **A company building general-purpose labour robots publishes nothing about labour.** No employment-effects analysis, no worker channel, no transition programme. Systemic Thinking sits at the absence floor.
