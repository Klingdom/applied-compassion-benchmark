## Disposition: FLOOR CONFIRMED, NO MOVEMENT POSSIBLE OR WARRANTED

xAI carries a formal floor designation. Every dimension is at raw 1.0 and the composite resolves to 0.0. Two things follow.

**Downward movement is arithmetically impossible.** The entity is already at the floor.

**Upward movement is not supported.** Being named as a defendant in an antitrust complaint is not positive conduct evidence about xAI. The suit alleges that xAI's leader publicly agreed with a safety-deceleration proposal on 12 September 2026. A single public statement of agreement, reported only through a complaint that seeks to characterise it as anticompetitive, is not the sustained, verified, independently confirmed conduct that lifting a floor designation requires. The screening rules are explicit that an upgrade needs specific new positive conduct by the entity itself, and that the absence of new harm is not positive evidence.

The floor designation rests on a documented systemic harm pattern across multiple assessment cycles: deliberate removal of safety guardrails, public deployment of a model producing antisemitic and violent content on demand, founder-directed alignment toward propaganda objectives, absence of any system card, model card or red-team report, and no incident-disclosure process. Nothing in window addresses any of that.
