## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Iran is already near the bottom of the scale, and its own July 2026 conduct confirms that placement rather than changing it. About 1,500 prisoners under sentence of death began a hunger strike at Ghezel Hesar Prison on 13 July 2026.
- The strike began after six cellmates were moved to solitary confinement for execution. Some prisoners sewed their lips shut and began a dry hunger strike, and several are reported in critical medical condition.
- More than 500 family members rallied outside the prison on the seventh day of the strike. No official response has been recorded.
- Iran carried out 2,167 executions in 2025, a record, and accounts for 79 per cent of the world's recorded drug-related executions.
