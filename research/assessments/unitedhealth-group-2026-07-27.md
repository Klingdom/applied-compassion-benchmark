## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the number that matters is older than the headline. The federal finding that UnitedHealthcare denied 71% of long-term acute-care hospital admission requests was published by the HHS Office of Inspector General on 8 June 2026, not in July.
- The in-window event is oversight, not adjudication. Senators Richard Blumenthal and Josh Hawley of the Senate Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations wrote to UnitedHealthcare chief executive Tim Noel in mid-July 2026 demanding records on post-acute care denials since 2023.
- The senators wrote that because of an absence of comprehensive reporting requirements, Medicare Advantage insurers are able to hide the full extent of denials of care resulting from their abuse of prior authorization.
- For context, the federal comparison figure was 42% for 16 other Medicare Advantage organisations. CVS Health was 80% and Humana 72%.
- A congressional records demand is not a court finding. Under the pre-adjudication screen already applied to this entity on 6 July 2026, an investigation cannot move the score on its own, and the denial-of-care pattern is already priced into the published 10.2.
