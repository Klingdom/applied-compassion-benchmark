## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the decision that cost 494 people their jobs was made in May, not in the week it made the news. Amazon confirmed in filings reported 20 to 23 July 2026 that it will cut 494 of about 850 jobs at its Port St. Lucie fulfilment centre, having announced the closure in May and notified staff in June.
- The July items are the state notice and its coverage, not a new decision. That materially limits how much new conduct there is to score.
- Separations run 17 September to 17 December 2026. The site closes for a two-year, $200 million automation rebuild and is expected to reopen in 2028 with about 1,000 jobs.
- Mitigations are real but thin: transfer offers to other South Florida warehouses, four weeks of severance, and transition healthcare. Four weeks is well below what other large employers offered in the same period.
- Assessed composite 12.5 against a published 12.8. Delta -0.3, below the 5-point proposal threshold.
