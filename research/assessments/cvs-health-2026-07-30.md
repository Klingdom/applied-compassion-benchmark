### Score Difference Analysis

Henry Ford Hospital and others filed suit against CVS Health Corporation in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan on 16 July 2026, inside the evidence window. Together the two systems allege CVS improperly retained about $95 million in 340B drug pricing savings — $29 million and $66 million respectively — that was intended to fund care for low-income and uninsured patients. The complaint alleges CVS refused an audit required under its contract with Henry Ford and then terminated the health system’s 340B contracts in retaliation for uncovering the scheme. These are allegations and no court has ruled. ## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: the money was supposed to pay for care for people who cannot afford it.** Two Michigan hospital systems say CVS Health kept about $95 million in 340B drug pricing savings meant to fund care for low-income and uninsured patients. Henry Ford Health puts its loss at more than $29 million since 2020; University of Michigan Health puts its at $66 million.
- **Why it matters: the most serious allegation is not about the money.** The complaint says CVS refused an audit its own contract with Henry Ford required, and then cancelled the health system’s 340B contracts after Henry Ford uncovered the alleged scheme.
- **Why it matters: none of this has been decided.** Henry Ford filed on 16 July 2026 in federal court in Michigan. CVS has not answered and no court has ruled. The scores move by fractions of a point, not by bands.
- **How the alleged mechanism worked.** The complaints say CVS used its vertically integrated pharmacy benefit manager and specialty pharmacy operations to retroactively reclassify claims and keep the reimbursement spread the hospitals say was theirs.
- **CVS already sits near the bottom of the Fortune 500 index at rank 320 of 448.** The published composite of 25.6 out of 100 is Developing. This assessment confirms it.

## Strongest Dimensions

**Action (2.2) and Systemic Thinking (2.2).** CVS operates at national scale with documented programme structures and pharmacy access reach, which is the only part of the profile above the rest.

## Weakest Dimensions

If proven, that is the Equity anchor at 1 and the Accountability anchor at 1. Scored conservatively as allegations, it holds both dimensions at the bottom of the Developing band.
