## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Naver Labs has real governance machinery through its parent, and a documented workplace-safety failure at that same parent. The two pull in opposite directions and both are scored.
- NAVER Corp. published AI Ethics Principles with Seoul National University in February 2021 and an AI Safety Framework it has revised twice, most recently as ASF 2.0 on 8 July 2026 — sustained, externally presented anticipatory-harm work.
- South Korea’s Ministry of Employment and Labor inspected Naver after a 2021 employee death and found that more than half of 1,982 respondents had been bullied at work within six months. It demanded culture change and planned a fine.
- A second Naver programmer, aged 37, died by suicide in September 2022. Her family alleged she was denied legally required flexible working after maternity leave. Naver said it "could not find any proof during its internal investigation."
- Naver Labs runs 1784, a headquarters where roughly 100 robots operate among about 5,000 staff — an unusual case where the people exposed to the robots are the company’s own employees.

## Strongest Dimensions

Systemic Thinking (3.2) and Awareness (3.0). Naver publishes a 2040 Carbon Negative target, a KRW 1 trillion 2025-2030 investment plan and an AI safety framework it has revised twice, and it co-developed its ethics principles with an outside university. Action (2.8) follows from the size and specificity of the published investment commitment.

## Weakest Dimensions

Empathy (2.0) and Integrity (2.0). Both are driven by the same evidence: a regulator found majority-level workplace bullying, two employees died, and in the second case the company denied the allegations and closed its own investigation without a finding. E4 Validation, EQ5 Historical Harm Acknowledgment, B1 Self-Sustainability, AB5 Reparative Action and I3 Internal Consistency all sit at the floor of 1, where further downward movement is arithmetically unavailable.

## Evidence Gaps

No Naver Labs-specific labour, safety or governance disclosure was located. All governance evidence is parent-level, and all labour-harm evidence is parent-level. No independent audit of Naver Labs and no community testimony were obtainable. The 2021 and 2022 worker-death cases are four and five years old respectively, which is outside the preferred two-to-three-year evidence window; they are scored because the ministry finding is the only regulator-verified evidence about the group’s internal culture and because no published remediation outcome was found.
