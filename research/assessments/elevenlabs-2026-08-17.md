### Score Difference Analysis

**Equity (+15 points scaled) is the largest rise and the highest dimension at 2.6.** The Impact Program offers free lifetime access to anyone with permanent voice loss, across more than 20 countries and more than 150 nonprofit partners, and patients and clinicians can now apply directly rather than through an intermediary. Universality, Priority for Vulnerable and Access Design all score 3.

**Action (+2.5 points scaled) and Awareness (-2.5 points scaled) barely moved.** The programme is real but its results are not published. The headline figure — one million people — is a target, not an outcome. No data on how many people regained usable speech was located, which caps Efficacy at 2.

**Accountability (0 points scaled — the seed already had it low, and it stays there).** All five Accountability subdimensions score 2. The link between ElevenLabs and the January 2024 Biden robocall was established by Pindrop and Berkeley researchers, not by the company; ElevenLabs acted only after being told. Consumer Reports found in March 2025 that only two of six voice-cloning products took steps to combat misuse, and ElevenLabs was not one of them.

**Integrity (+5 points scaled).** Non-Performance scores 3, because the Impact Program runs at real cost and is corroborated by an independent ALS nonprofit rather than only by company communications.

**The published 32.8 is close to correct for ElevenLabs and no revision is warranted.** The assessed 34.4 sits 1.6 points above it, well inside the 5.0-point filing threshold, and the band does not change. ## Key Findings

- **ElevenLabs gives its core product away for life to people who cannot speak.** The Impact Program has run since August 2024 with more than 150 nonprofit partners in over 20 countries, starting with ALS and motor neurone disease and extending to multiple system atrophy and mouth cancers.
- **An independent nonprofit corroborates it.** ALS United Mid-Atlantic wrote on 22 August 2024 that 'this news from ElevenLabs is an exciting breakthrough for the future of ALS care.'
- **Consumer Reports found the safety side wanting.** Its March 2025 test of six voice-cloning products found only two took steps to combat misuse; the others, including ElevenLabs, required only a self-attestation checkbox.
- **Congress is asking questions.** A United States Joint Economic Committee letter dated 16 April 2026 asked chief executive Mati Staniszewski whether the company's no-go voices policy actually prevents unauthorised cloning of public figures.
- **ElevenLabs publishes a limit that embarrasses it.** The company states its own detection classifier reads only the first minute of a sample and does not reliably classify audio from its newest model. That voluntary disclosure is why Scope Clarity scores 3.

## Strongest Dimensions

**Action (2.6) and Equity (2.6)** are the strongest. Both rest on the Impact Program: free lifetime access calibrated to a severe need rather than to ability to pay, with a direct application route and independent nonprofit corroboration.

## Weakest Dimensions

**Accountability (2.0)** is the weakest, at the absence baseline across all five subdimensions. Harm acknowledgment followed external detection, the corrections after the Biden robocall were still found inadequate fourteen months later, and no repair to anyone whose voice was cloned without consent was located.

## Evidence Gaps

The Impact Program's scale is stated by the company and not independently verified beyond individual nonprofit endorsements. No published outcome data on how many people regained usable speech was located. The Joint Economic Committee letter PDF could not be parsed directly and its existence and date were confirmed through the committee's own file listing and through Biometric Update coverage. No workforce or internal culture data was located.

## Scoring Convention Applied

**Absence of disclosure scores 2, never 1.** The floor of 1 is reserved for positive documented evidence of a specific failure. This is the majority convention across the three most recent de-seeding studies and was applied identically to all seven labs in this study. **Absence of disclosure is not evidence of harm.** Where this company does not publish, the low anchor was scored and said so explicitly. A score of 4 requires published institutional data, a regulatory decision or third-party peer review; a score of 5 requires independent audit plus community testimony.
