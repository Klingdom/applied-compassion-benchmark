# Screening Note: Bucharest

**Assessment date:** 2026-09-24
**Outcome:** screened, not assessed. No proposal on the Bucharest row.
**Published:** 39.1/100, Developing, rank #79 of 250 global cities. Canonical reconstruction returns 39.1 exactly — no math-hygiene issue.

## The double-count risk, addressed first

Romania was assessed on 2026-09-17 on this exact protest. `research/assessments/romania-2026-09-17.md` measured Romania at 35.6 against a published 37.5 and confirmed the score. It scored the gendarmes' use of tear gas, the detentions (26 on the day, later reported as 47), the 24 people hurt including 8 gendarmes, and Balkan Insight's account that gendarmes acted after demonstrators "broke through security cordons and threw objects at officers." It found no source reporting live ammunition, deaths, or an independent finding of excessive force, and did not score the response as a new harm.

It also settled the attribution question at national level: the live-sheep export ban behind the protest was imposed by the **European Commission**, not by Romania, and was not scored against Romania.

**This note does not re-score any of that.**

## Attribution: the policing is national, and no municipal conduct was found

The force that used tear gas is the **Jandarmeria Română**, the Romanian Gendarmerie, a national force under the Ministry of Internal Affairs. Bucharest City Hall (Primăria Municipiului București) has no command over it and no role in public-order policing of national demonstrations.

The other in-window items are national as well. The outbreak-management plan and the farmer compensation scheme are drafts by the national government, and the Romania assessment already recorded that drafts are not counted as help delivered.

**No conduct by the Bucharest municipality was located in this window, in either direction.** The protest happened in the city. Nothing the city itself did or failed to do was reported.

## What would have been assessable, and was not found

A city row can be moved by a protest when the city's own conduct is implicated: local police (Poliția Locală) behaviour, refusal of an assembly permit, failure to provide medical or sanitary support to a large gathering, or a municipal response to injured people. Neither source reports any of it.

## Why no full assessment was run

Bucharest has never been individually assessed (`last_assessed: null`) and its published 39.1 rests on a near-uniform vector — six of eight dimensions at exactly 2.5 — which is the signature of a placeholder. A first full assessment of the row would be worthwhile. It should not be triggered by, or built around, an event that is not the city's conduct, because that imports a national finding into a municipal score. `last_assessed` is not stamped.

## Trigger that would change this

Evidence of Bucharest municipal conduct — Poliția Locală involvement, a permit decision, a municipal service failure during the demonstration — would make this assessable on the city row.
