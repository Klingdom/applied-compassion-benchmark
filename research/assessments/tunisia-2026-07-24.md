## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Tunisia’s July 2026 evidence is a worsening of conditions for prisoners the benchmark already counted, not a new category of harm. The score holds.
- On 23 July 2026 a witness saw Rached Ghannouchi, aged 85, faint at Mornaguia prison. The reported temperature inside was 52 degrees Celsius, and detainees are confined for most of the day to cells no larger than 16 square metres.
- The wider crackdown continued. At least 14 people were arrested in Djerba and Tunis during July 2026 and six were sentenced, while 37 opposition figures remain detained after proceedings the UN calls politically motivated.
- The assessed score of 23.1 is 0.7 points below the published 23.8, so the developing-band score is confirmed.
