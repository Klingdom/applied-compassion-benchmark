### Score Difference Analysis

The -4.7 difference is carried forward unchanged from 27 July 2026 and is concentrated in Boundaries (2.0 to 1.8), Accountability (2.0 to 1.8) and Integrity (2.0 to 1.8), with Equity holding at 2.0. It is deliberately kept below the proposal threshold because the largest part of the documented harm — batons, tear gas, facial-recognition surveillance and the mobile internet shutdown ordered on 20 July 2026 — was carried out by a force the city government does not command. Attributing that in full to Delhi as a municipal entity would be an attribution error. One element of the scan record also does not survive checking: the renewal of National Security Act detention powers for the Delhi Police Commissioner was issued on 7 July 2026 and effective from 19 July, before the protest, as a routine renewal, and lawyers for the protest movement subsequently confirmed all detainees had been released.

### Recommendation

Confirm the published score of 32.8, band Developing, with a watch flag. No proposal. The conversion trigger for a city-level move would be evidence of Delhi municipal institutions — hospitals, transport, or the city administration — failing those injured, or a city-level refusal to acknowledge what happened within its jurisdiction.

## Key Findings

- **Why it matters: the police force involved is not the city’s.** Delhi Police reports to India’s Union Home Ministry, not to the Delhi city government. Most of the harm documented on 20 July 2026 belongs to the national score, not the municipal one.
- **Why it matters: the underlying event is serious and already scored.** Human Rights Watch reported on 23 July 2026 that authorities used tear gas and batons against largely peaceful student and youth protesters, with one young woman protester in critical condition.
- **Why it matters: the city went dark.** Telecommunication providers reportedly received orders to shut down mobile internet in parts of central Delhi on 20 July 2026, and metro services were suspended.
- **Why it matters: this is the second look at one protest.** Delhi was assessed on 27 July 2026 on this material and confirmed at 28.1. Scoring it again would turn one event into two downgrades.
