### Recommendation

**CONFIRM**. The composite delta of -1.3 points is inside the 5-point confirmation threshold, so the published score of 62.5 is confirmed.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: South Korea’s labour picture in July 2026 pulls in two directions at once, and the two effects roughly cancel out.
- The structural gain is real. The Yellow Envelope Law took effect on 10 March 2026 and extends union rights to subcontractors and gig workers for the first time.
- The July pullback is also real. On 21 July 2026 President Lee Jae-myung said union demands for a fixed share of operating profit may not count as a lawful subject of labour disputes.
- The assessed score of 60.0 is 2.5 points below the published 62.5, inside the 5-point threshold, so the established-band score is confirmed.
