# Compassion Benchmark Attribution Screening Note: Georgia

**Entity type:** State  
**Assessment date:** 2026-09-22  
**Outcome:** confirmation; no dimension moves. The raid was federal conduct; the State of Georgia’s own response was real but narrow, and is consistent with the scores already carried.  
**Published:** 26.9/100 (Developing), us-states rank 51  
**Carried measurement:** 26.9/100 (Developing), measured in `research/assessments/georgia-us-states-2026-07-19.md`

## Why this entity was assessed

The scanner flagged Georgia because more than 300 South Korean workers detained in the 2025 federal immigration raid at the Hyundai electric-vehicle plant near Savannah filed a federal legal claim on 15 September 2026 — the first major legal action over one of the largest immigration-enforcement operations of this administration. Georgia was last assessed on 19 July 2026 at 26.9.

## The attribution question, which the scanner itself flagged

**The raid was conducted by federal agencies.** On 4 September 2025, Department of Homeland Security forces detained 475 people at the Hyundai Metaplant in what the agency called the largest workplace operation in its history. Many detainees were South Korean nationals in the country to install equipment.

**The 15 September 2026 claim is against the United States government**, not against the State of Georgia. Georgia is not a defendant, and the conduct challenged is federal.

**Governor Brian Kemp's office was not told in advance.** Reporting establishes that Kemp was not apprised of the federal action beforehand and had to contact the President's aides for details.

**So the raid itself moves nothing.** This is the same discipline applied to Ukraine, Oakland and Los Angeles in recent cycles: a federal enforcement action at a site inside a state's borders is not that state's conduct.

## What the State of Georgia itself did, which is what is scoreable

Three documented state actions:

1. **Delayed public comment.** Kemp broke his silence on 16 September 2025, twelve days after the raid, calling for changes to the visa system. Reporting describes his communications team debating whether to distance the governor from the automaker and cutting the word "excited" from an early draft.
2. **Diplomatic repair aimed at investors.** Kemp undertook a week-long trip to South Korea and Japan to steady relations, and said he had discussed the concerns of South Korean officials and of other international companies operating in Georgia with the White House and the President directly.
3. **A defence of the employer's compliance.** Kemp stated publicly that the company had been following state laws.

**What is absent from the record is any state action directed at the welfare of the 475 detained people themselves** — no legal aid, wage recovery, interpretation, family support or reception assistance was located. The state's documented response was organised around the investment relationship and the visa system, not around the people detained.

## Why no dimension moves

Because that pattern — a state that responds to harm to workers by protecting the commercial relationship — is precisely what the existing near-floor scores already encode. The 19 July 2026 assessment measured **EMP at 1.8 raw, EQU at 1.6 and INT at 1.8**, all in the Critical band at dimension level. E1 and E4 sit inside an Empathy score of 1.8; EQ1 sits inside an Equity score of 1.6.

There is also a small countervailing fact that should be named rather than suppressed: Kemp did publicly call for visa-system reform, which is a position with some political cost and is mildly relevant to S4. It is not remotely enough to move SYS, which sits at 2.2, and it is not scored, because a single statement is not documented advocacy.
