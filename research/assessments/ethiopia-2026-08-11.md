## Key Findings

- War came back to Tigray, and no one is left to investigate it. Fighting resumed on 1 August 2026 between Tigrayan forces and the Ethiopian National Defence Force — the worst since the 2020-2022 war. Amnesty International warned on 3 August 2026 that "The resumption of violence in Tigray region puts residents at renewed risk of human rights violations."
- Ethiopia helped shut down the bodies that were watching it. The UN International Commission of Human Rights Experts on Ethiopia lost its mandate after the government lobbied against renewal, and the African Commission's inquiry into Tigray also ended.
- Six years after the atrocities, no senior official has been tried. Human Rights Watch found the government's transitional justice process effectively stalled, with no high-level perpetrators prosecuted.
- A second war is escalating at the same time. Clashes between the Fano militia and government forces in Amhara are forecast to intensify through at least September 2026, while food access declines.
- Ethiopia's score cannot go much lower, and that is itself a problem. The published 4.7 already puts four of eight measures at the absolute minimum of 1.0. Even severe new evidence cannot move the score by the 5 points needed to trigger a change.

## Strongest Dimensions

Awareness (1.40/5), and only because the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission still functions and national early-warning data still exist. No dimension reaches the Developing band.

## Weakest Dimensions

Action, Equity, Accountability, Systemic Thinking and Integrity are all at the 1.00 floor. The state is a party to the fighting, has terminated the mechanisms that investigated it, and has tried no senior perpetrator.

## Evidence Gaps

- The Human Rights Watch World Report chapter and the OHCHR ICHREE page carry no machine-verifiable date and are used for claims only.
- No independent casualty figures for the 1 August 2026 Tigray clashes were available at assessment time.
- Ethiopian government responses to the Amnesty statement were not located.
- Floor compression means several subdimensions are scored at 1 on the basis of absence of any protective practice rather than on documented conduct.
