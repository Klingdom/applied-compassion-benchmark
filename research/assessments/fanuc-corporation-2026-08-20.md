## Baseline-establishment note

This is a **first-ever assessment**. FANUC Corporation does not appear in any published Compassion Benchmark index.

**Corporate status check (required before assessment).** FANUC Corporation is operating under that name, headquartered at Oshino-mura, Yamanashi, Japan, and listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange. It published an Integrated Report for the year ended 31 March 2025 and a Sustainability Report 2025 together with an ESG Data Book on 26 December 2025 ([FANUC Sustainability Report](https://www.fanuc.co.jp/en/sustainability/report/); [FANUC Integrated Report 2025](https://www.fanuc.co.jp/en/ir/annualreport/pdf/integratedreport2025_e.pdf)). No acquisition, rename or insolvency event was found.

**Evidence base and its limits.** FANUC's evidence is almost entirely **self-published**: an integrated report, a sustainability report, an ESG data book referencing GRI Standards and ISO 26000, and a compliance page describing the whistleblowing system. Under the tightened evidence test this index now applies, self-published corporate material does not count toward independent verification. The two genuinely third-party items located are an independent ESG risk rating from Sustainalytics and a NOCTI-certified education programme.

**Two things this score does not mean.** First, **many low scores here rest on absence of disclosure, not on evidence of harm.** No adverse conduct finding, safety scandal, labour ruling or regulatory action against FANUC was located. Second, FANUC operates in a disclosure regime that does not require the social reporting this benchmark reads, so part of the drag is structural rather than behavioural.

**Disclosure density.** Of 40 subdimensions, **28 rest primarily on absence of disclosure** and 12 rest on positive located evidence.

## Key Findings

- **FANUC is the largest industrial robot maker in the world and the benchmark can read almost nothing about how it treats people.** Twenty-eight of 40 subdimensions rest on absence of disclosure. That is a finding about disclosure, not about conduct — no safety scandal, labour ruling or regulatory action against FANUC was located.
- **The strongest evidence is a training programme, not a report.** FANUC's Certified Education Robot Training runs in more than 1,700 schools in the Americas and awards certifications built with NOCTI, an independent body. Those credentials belong to the worker, not to FANUC. That lifts Boundaries and Systemic Thinking.
- **FANUC does not publicly engage the question its own product raises.** Nothing was found in which the company discusses what automation does to employment in its customers' plants. Interconnection Awareness scores 2 of 5 as a result.
- **Almost all of FANUC's own evidence is self-published.** A sustainability report, an ESG data book and an integrated report are not independent verification under this index's tightened test. The only genuinely third-party items located are a Sustainalytics ESG risk rating and the NOCTI certification.
- **Three of eight dimensions sit exactly at the absence floor of 2.0** — Empathy, Equity and Accountability. Nothing adverse was found in any of them either.

## Strongest Dimensions
- **Awareness (40.0)** and **Systemic Thinking (35.0)** — a global whistleblowing channel, a 2024 materiality revision, mandatory pre-market safety assessment under ISO 10218, and a large upstream education programme.

## Weakest Dimensions
- **Empathy, Equity and Accountability, all at 25.0** — the absence floor. No complaint-validation practice, no coverage or disparity data, no acknowledged harm and no repair case were located in either direction.

## Evidence Gaps
- No independently assured social reporting; almost everything is company-authored.
- No published data on whistleblowing volumes, outcomes or acted-upon findings.
- No published position or research on automation and employment.
- No located third-party assessment of workplace conditions at FANUC or in its supply chain beyond a compelled forced-labour filing.
