### Recommendation

**CONFIRM**. The composite delta of -4.8 points is inside the 5-point confirmation threshold, so the published score of 39.8 is confirmed. A watch flag is recorded: the delta is close to the threshold and the next cycle should re-test it.

## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Bangladesh is rolling back the very safeguards that earned it a score increase in June 2026. That is a warning sign, and the score is now near the edge of a downgrade.
- Human Rights Watch reported on 19 July 2026 the first known enforced disappearance since the July 2024 uprising. Miraj Sheikh, a 30-year-old fisherman, was seen being detained by Coast Guard officers on 10 April 2026; his family was told the next day that he had never been there.
- The elected government let the protections expire. HRW says it "allowed these ordinances to lapse, proposing instead laws under which the police would continue to investigate enforced disappearances", and the replacement bill would bar the national human rights commission from investigating security forces.
- The assessed score of 35.0 is 4.8 points below the published 39.8 — just inside the 5-point threshold. The score is confirmed, with a watch flag for the next cycle.
