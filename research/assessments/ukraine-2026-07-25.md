## Key Findings

- Why it matters: the July 2026 deaths in Ukraine were inflicted by Russian strikes, so they do not lower Ukraine's score. On 24 July 2026 a Russian ballistic missile hit an arms exhibition in Kyiv Oblast, killing at least 10 people and injuring more than 100.
- Ukraine did act on its own conduct. The Prosecutor General's Office opened criminal proceedings including for possible improper performance of official duties in organising the event that was struck.
- UN monitors verified 1,396 civilians killed and 7,978 injured in Ukraine in the first half of 2026, a 37 per cent rise on the same period in 2025 and a 114 per cent rise on 2024.
- Civic space is still working. Domestic protests over the attempted removal of the defence minister ran for eleven consecutive days across Kyiv, Dnipro and Lviv without being suppressed.
