## Vietnam expands free health care while jailing its critics, and now holds two foreign activists who say they were taken from Cambodia

Two activists from the pro-democracy group Viet Tan, Australian Hiep Tran (50) and Norwegian-Vietnamese Duc Thuan Nguyen (52), went missing in Phnom Penh on 18 September 2026. Vietnamese state media then reported them detained for illegally entering Vietnam from the Cambodian border. Viet Tan says they were abducted in Cambodia and forcibly taken across the border (ABC News, 2026-09-29). Australia's foreign ministry confirmed it is giving consular help. The detention is confirmed by Vietnam itself; the abduction is an allegation and is not scored as fact.

This is Vietnam's first evidence-based score. The published 34.4 was never assessed. Human Rights Watch's World Report 2026 records more than 160 political prisoners in 2025, at least 40 new arrests for criticising the government, and a political prisoner's death in custody. Against that, Vietnam abolished the death penalty for eight crimes in June 2025, and Politburo Resolution 72 commits to free annual health checks from 2026 and free basic hospital care by 2030, with health insurance coverage targeted above 95% in 2026.

Measured: 23.8 (Developing), down 10.6. Filed on the magnitude trigger.
