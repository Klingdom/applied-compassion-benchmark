## Key Findings

- Why it matters: Barbados responded to an exploitation allegation with law, not with public relations. That is the behaviour the established band is meant to describe.
- The Protection of Wages Bill was introduced on 7 July 2026 and debated on 21-22 July 2026. It makes payday a legal obligation and sets penalties of $30,000 or up to three years in prison for withholding deducted wages.
- The bill targets the weakest workers specifically. It bans "retention" deductions, bans interest on salary advances, and lets workers choose the bank their wages go to at no cost to them.
- The assessed score of 62.5 exactly matches the published 62.5, so the score is confirmed with no change.
