## Evidence-date, provenance, attribution and screening checks

**WHO DID WHAT TO WHOM (the decisive screen).** Four distinct actors appear and only one of them is the entity under assessment. The march was organised by Det Danske Folk, a private nationalist group. The blockade was mounted, unannounced, by anti-racism counter-protesters. The force was used by Kobenhavns Politi. The entity under assessment is the City of Copenhagen, the municipality. None of the three acting parties is the municipality.

**ATTRIBUTION: DANISH POLICE ARE NATIONAL, NOT MUNICIPAL.** Denmark has a single unitary national police service. Kobenhavns Politi is one of its districts, accountable to the Rigspolitiet and the Ministry of Justice, not to the Copenhagen municipal council. The City of Copenhagen does not command, discipline or set the rules of engagement for the officers who used batons. Attributing that policing to the municipality would be the same error as attributing United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement conduct to the City of San Francisco, which this cycle also declines. No policing conduct is scored against Copenhagen.

**WHAT THE SOURCES ACTUALLY SAY ABOUT THE FORCE.** Reporting states officers pushed protesters back and used batons to clear the blockade after protesters repeatedly ignored orders to leave, and that Copenhagen Police said the blockade had been removed by force. No source located reports injuries requiring treatment, arrests in numbers, deaths, or any independent finding of excessive force. Police opened an investigation into individuals who broke the law -- that is a police act, again not a municipal one.

**NO MUNICIPAL CONDUCT LOCATED.** No City of Copenhagen decision, statement, permit condition or service response was located in the window. With no attributable municipal conduct, there is nothing to move in either direction. The correct outcome is confirmation, not a score.

**BASELINE PROVENANCE (read first).** Published 100.0 (Exemplary, rank 1) has never moved. There is no Copenhagen entry in `APPLIED_CHANGES.md` and no Copenhagen change proposal on disk. The vector is six dimensions at exactly 5.0 and two at 4.5, which is a seed-style placeholder rather than an assessed profile. That is recorded as a calibration referral, not converted into a proposal (screening rule 5).

**CEILING LIMIT.** Grid rounding of the published vector still returns 100.0, because the formula computes 106.25 and clamps to 100. The score cannot rise and, absent attributable conduct, has no evidential reason to fall. Both directions are blocked this cycle.

## Key Findings

- Copenhagen's published score of 100 out of 100 is confirmed, on a narrow and important ground: nobody the benchmark is scoring did anything here.
- On 5 September 2026 police used batons to clear an unannounced blockade near a far-right 'remigration' march in Osterbro. The force came from Kobenhavns Politi, a district of Denmark's single national police service.
- Danish police answer to the national police commissioner and the Ministry of Justice, not to Copenhagen's municipal council. The city cannot direct or discipline those officers, so the benchmark does not charge their conduct to the city.
- Three other actors were involved and none of them is the city either: a private nationalist group organised the march, counter-protesters mounted the blockade, and the police opened an investigation afterwards.
- The real finding is about the benchmark, not Copenhagen. This is the only entity of 1,325 published at exactly 100.0, and it gets there only because the formula caps a raw 106.25 at 100. A ceiling with nothing above it cannot register improvement or decline. That is referred for calibration review.
