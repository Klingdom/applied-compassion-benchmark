# Compassion Benchmark Daily Digest — 2026-08-20

**SHORT GAP-RUN CYCLE (2026-08-19 through 2026-08-20).** 20 entities assessed, 7 change proposals generated, 13 confirmations, 0 errors. **Zero scores applied tonight.**

## THE LEAD STORY IS THE FLOOR, NOT THE PROPOSALS

Eight of twenty entities assessed tonight were evidence-insensitive at or near the bottom of the scale. Sudan, South Sudan, Yemen, Israel and Myanmar all sit at **0.0 with all 40 subdimensions at the 1.0 minimum** — every one of the benchmark's eight measured dimensions is already at its floor for each of these five countries, so further downgrade is arithmetically impossible no matter what the evidence shows. Somalia (5.0) and Haiti (3.1) have only about 5 points of downward room, at or below the 5.0-point filing threshold. DR Congo (2.5) has 2.5 points of room, less than half the threshold, and is already in the lowest band, so the band-crossing clause cannot apply either.

This reaffirms and extends a finding first recorded on 2026-08-11 and repeated on prior cycles: **the scale cannot distinguish Sudan today from any future deterioration.** It is now shown to hold across an entire eight-entity cohort in a single cycle — the first time this has been documented at this scale. This is the most important and most publishable finding of the night: a benchmark openly reporting a limit of its own instrument. It is a **methodology finding, not a score change**, and the public briefing does not write it as though these countries improved or worsened.

## Impacts and Insights

- **Tonight is the clearest demonstration yet that the benchmark's floor is a real measurement limitation, not a rhetorical one.** Eight separate, independently-sourced, often severe findings (a state-military strike on a civilian court; 36 aid-worker deaths; 1,430+ settler incidents; an air force killing 14 civilians aged 9-88) all produced zero score movement, purely on arithmetic grounds. This is honestly disclosed rather than papered over, and the disclosure is itself the most valuable output of the cycle.
- **The evidence-test discipline continues to function as a real constraint.** 4 of 5 band crossings filed tonight failed it and were recorded as failed rather than suppressed — consistent with the 2026-08-18 pattern (also 4 of 5 failed) and now a 2-cycle trend worth naming as its own recurring category.
- **Self-correction volume remains high and cuts in multiple directions**: an attribution correction that makes Sudan's floor confirmation more damning (state conduct, not paramilitary), a confirmation that rejects a plausible-sounding upgrade argument (Venezuela), and a scanner near-miss caught before publication (two stale-dated candidates).
- **The entity-record-defect category (mergers/acquisitions leaving stale standalone entries) is now a recurring pattern**, not a one-off: ADP (2026-08-17) and Interpublic Group (tonight) are the same defect class. Worth a dedicated coordinator-level sweep for other Fortune-500 M&A activity.
