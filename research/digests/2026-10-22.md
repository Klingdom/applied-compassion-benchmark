## Impacts and Insights

- **The gap between "seeded" and "measured" is structural, not episodic.** 15 of 16 first baselines read lower. For municipal rows the seed (60.9–65.6) sits near the Established floor while a default-2 first reading lands at 33–48. Published scores for never-assessed city rows are systematically overstated relative to any evidence-based reading, but the size depends on the unsettled gap convention.

That makes filings sensitive to where a seed happens to sit, which is itself a calibration problem.

- **Evidence gaps most limiting:** no tier ≥ 4 adverse source for Portugal or France; no City Hall statement on the riots for Paris; UNC's 21 undocumented subdimensions; Netflix's 35 defaults; Mauritius's index reconstruction issue.
- **Scanner date discipline held:** the Florida execution is 2026-10-13 (not Oct. 15); Portugal's Amnesty call (2026-10-15) concerns the 12 Oct Porto operation; the scan's "three suspended" officers in Mauritius was not found in fetched text and is not used.
