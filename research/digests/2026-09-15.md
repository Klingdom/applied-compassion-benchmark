### THE LEAD STORY — Wellington: A Statutory Review Proposes the Cycle's Only Band Crossing, and Most of the Number Is a Formula Artifact

Wellington, New Zealand's capital, has never been individually assessed; its published 83.0 (Exemplary, rank 13 of 250 in global-cities) is a never-checked placeholder. On 2 September 2026 the New Zealand government released a statutory Crown Review into the 4 February 2026 Moa Point wastewater plant failure, appointed under the Local Government Act 2002. It found the failure was "not caused by a single decision or isolated failure, but by longstanding weaknesses in governance, accountability, asset management, risk management and infrastructure resilience," and that Wellington City Council, as plant owner and consent holder, "remained ultimately accountable" — yet relied on Wellington Water's notifications rather than checking its own standards. RNZ: "no single organisation acted as though it was in charge." Mayor Andrew Little apologised, said under-performance "was seen as normal," and the council created a new role to monitor Wellington Water's Tiaki Wai programme — credited, holding Harm Acknowledgment at 5 and Correction Willingness at 4.

Five subdimensions move down (A5 Anticipatory Awareness, AC4 Resource Mobilization, AB4 Systemic Learning, S2 Long-Term Impact, I4 Values Alignment), each 4→3. A downgrade proposal is filed: 83.0 → 71.3 (-11.7), Exemplary → Established, confidence medium. **Filed, not applied** — this proposal awaits a founder decision; the published score remains 83.0.

**Mandatory magnitude disclosure.** About two-thirds of the -11.7 is not conduct — it is the canonical scoring formula's integration-premium cliff (documented as RISK-019: "all dimensions at 3.99 → 74.8 (Established), at 4.0 → 85.0 (Exemplary)"). But because five of Wellington's eight dimensions now sit below the 4.0 integration-premium threshold, the entire 8-point bonus disappears (-8.0). The proposal's own sensitivity check confirms the band crossing is real and would survive rejection of any single move (any one of A5, AC4, S2 or I4 alone still crosses the line at 79.8; only AB4 alone does not, landing at 81.8) — so this is not purely a calibration artifact, but the founder should read the -11.7 as roughly one-third conduct, two-thirds formula mechanics, not an 11.7-point deterioration in institutional behavior.
