## Significant Findings

### THE LEAD STORY — Chile: The Benchmark Caught Its Own Search Synthesis Inventing a Case

Chile is the largest movement of the night (−20.0, 62.5 → 42.5, Established → Functional) **and** the clearest self-correction in this cohort. The scan's search synthesis returned what appeared to be a 2026 UN Special Procedures communication to Chile ("unauthorised border crossing cannot be considered a crime"), together with specific claims that migrant administrative detention had been extended from 5 days to 60 (renewable to 180), and that healthcare centres, schools and social security agencies would be required to hand undocumented migrants' data to immigration authorities.

**On direct re-fetch, none of it survives.** The underlying Global Detention Project pages are dated **2023-06-01 and 2023-12-12**. The UN communication they quote is dated **2023-05-24** — three years outside this cycle's window. The real 2023 detention figures are different again (48 hours to 7 days, not 5 to 60). The healthcare/school data-sharing claim appears in **neither page**. All of it was excluded and none of it scored.

What survives is real and in-window: the Kast government's freeze on regularisation for ~182,000 already-registered migrants (MercoPress, 2026-03-31), the first deportation flights (2026-04-17), two bills before Congress limiting migrants' access to social security (NPR, 2026-05-23), and an eight-amendment constitutional package submitted 2026-08-18 that would allow a 240-day "public security" emergency without congressional approval, which HRW called "a dangerous power grab" (2026-09-01, tier 3).

**Consequence, recorded honestly:** with the 2023 material removed, **no tier-4-or-above source remains**, so Chile's `band_change_evidence_test` returns `passed: false`. Independent sources found: 3 (HRW tier 3, NPR tier 2, MercoPress tier 2). The band crossing (Established → Functional) is therefore filed as **unproven**, not confirmed — a genuine −20.0 movement on real conduct, carrying a band-crossing label the benchmark's own evidence bar does not yet support.

This is the second cycle running where the strongest story is the benchmark refusing its own inputs, and it pairs with Peru's retraction being caught a **second time** tonight (see Confirmations/Dropped Candidates below) — still circulating through wire pickups (Manila Times, Cambodianess dated 2026-08-26) that never caught up to ORAU's own retraction. **A correctly dated source can still be false.**

### THE SECOND STORY — Ethiopia: A Real, Evidenced Harm the Scale Cannot Record

Five of twenty entities tonight have **less downward room than the 5.0-point filing threshold**: Sudan and Israel at 0.0 (zero room), DR Congo at 2.3, Haiti and Ethiopia at 4.7. All five are already in the Critical band, so the band-crossing clause cannot fire for them either.

**Ethiopia is the demonstration case.** The Ethiopian National Defence Force conducted sustained drone strikes across Tigray since mid-August 2026 — the first sustained military confrontation there since the 2022 peace deal — including a strike on an out-of-session **school** in the village of Merewa on 10 August. Whether civilians were killed is disputed between AFP and the BBC (per Critical Threats); the benchmark does not score the disputed casualty count in either direction, but it does score the decision to strike a school in a populated village. The assessed movement is a real **−2.8**, from published 4.7 to assessed 1.9. Ethiopia only had 4.7 points of room before hitting the 5.0-point trigger. **No movement was manufactured; the deterioration goes unrecorded on the public score, and that gap is reported here rather than rounded away.**

A self-correction sits inside this finding too: the scan credited **Amnesty International** with describing the drone strikes. Amnesty's article contains no reference to drone strikes at all — it is dated 2026-08-03, 24 days before the scan's assigned evidence date, and its forced-conscription findings attach to **Tigrayan authorities**, a regional non-federal actor, not the federal government. The drone-strike evidence is Critical Threats (tier 2), correctly reattributed.

**Remedy recommended (methodology finding, not a score change):** a sub-floor severity annotation carried alongside the composite, so the record can distinguish an evidenced floor from a silent one and register deterioration below 0.0.

- Tonight's strongest signal is procedural, not substantive: twice in two consecutive cycles (2026-08-26 Peru, 2026-09-01 Chile) the benchmark's own evidence-verification step caught fabricated or stale-sourced material before it could move a published score.
