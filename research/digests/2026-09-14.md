### THE LEAD STORY — Hong Kong: A Third Escalation Completes a Pattern Flagged in July

Hong Kong falls 5.9 points (32.8 → 26.9, Developing sustained) on the sentencing of three Tiananmen vigil organisers — Chow Hang-tung (7y3m), Lee Cheuk-yan (7y) and Albert Ho (5y2m) — to prison terms for inciting subversion under the National Security Law, plus a HK$1.5M fine on the Hong Kong Alliance. The High Court itself said the case **did not involve violence or the threat of violence**. OHCHR (tier 4) called it "another regrettable outcome of the deeply flawed National Security Law"; High Commissioner Volker Türk called for the sentences to be quashed. The HKSAR Government (tier 5) rejected the criticism as smears and defended the law's structure directly: "Leadership by the Communist Party of China is the core of the fundamental system." This is the third escalation in a pattern the 2026-07-31 assessment (29.4, watch flag) explicitly anticipated within a four-month window — after bookstore raids and the prosecution of a vigil organiser with an intellectual disability. Confidence is medium, not high: the delta is only 0.9 above the filing trigger and the full sentencing reasons were not fetched.
