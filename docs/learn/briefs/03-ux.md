# Learn brief 03: UX, information architecture and page templates

Lens: routes, navigation, visual approach for young readers, page templates, experiment interaction, separation. Recommendations are numbered UX-R1 to UX-R16.

## What the existing code constrains

- The navbar is crowded: 8 `mainNav` items, an Indexes dropdown, a Tools dropdown, search and a "Contact Sales" CTA (`site/src/components/layout/Navbar.tsx`, `site/src/data/nav.ts`). It is sticky at 74px minimum, which costs about 10% of a 768px-tall Chromebook screen.
- The Footer embeds an email signup (`site/src/components/layout/Footer.tsx:29-30`). Learn cannot show it.
- `ChromeGate` already hides the global chrome for one namespace, `/nonprofit-alt`, so that route can render its own chrome (`site/src/components/layout/ChromeGate.tsx`, `site/src/app/layout.tsx:137-147`). This is the pattern to reuse.
- Umami is a hard-coded `<script>` in the root `<head>` (`layout.tsx:116-121`). Suppressing it on `/learn` needs a layout change, not a flag.
- `Button` fires `trackEvent` on tracked links and its default variant uses `text-text` (#e8eefb), which is invisible on a light surface (`site/src/components/ui/Button.tsx`). `Panel`, `Pill`, `Eyebrow` and `Callout` are also hard-coded for dark.
- `SelfAssessment` stores an email in `localStorage` and uses `window.confirm` (`site/src/components/assessment/SelfAssessment.tsx:56-70, 98`). Learn must not copy either behaviour.
- `globals.css` already has two precedents. A scoped namespace that changes no other page (`.pilot-*`, lines 88-92). A light-on-dark inverted surface (`.pilot-preview`, lines 105-114). It also has a print block that forces a light theme (line 193 onward). Band colours are "semantic encoding only" (line 22), and the pilot report avoids them entirely.

## Routes (UX-R1 to UX-R4)

**UX-R1. Band-first URLs.** The band is in the path so a build test can enforce separation (see UX-R14).

```
/learn                                  landing: routes by role and age band
/learn/explorers | investigators | analysts      band hubs
/learn/{band}/{topic}                   topic pages (5 topics, plain slugs:
                                        how-we-measure, finding-evidence, scores,
                                        what-it-shows, fixing-things)
/learn/{band}/experiments/{slug}        experiment pages
/learn/experiments                      overview index, grouped by band, titles only
/learn/educators                        educator hub
/learn/educators/plans/{slug}           lesson plan, teacher copy (printable)
/learn/educators/plans/{slug}/student   student sheet (printable)
/learn/educators/safety                 duty of care, privacy, licence
/learn/grown-ups                        interstitial to the full site
```

**UX-R2. Do not build a Researchers hub.** That band is adults who already use `/methodology` and `/research`. Fold it into a "Go to the full thing" block on the Analysts hub. Three student bands and one educator hub are enough to test.

**UX-R3. Landing routes by link, never by an age question.** Do not ask for a birth date or age. Two choices only: "I'm learning" and "I'm a teacher or parent". The first reveals three band cards, labelled by age and reading level ("Explorers, about 8-11, read with a grown-up"). Add "Not sure? Start with Explorers. You can switch any time." Every band page carries an "Other levels" row.

**UX-R4. Static route lists.** The routes are generated from a typed `learn` content registry, as `indexRegistry.ts` does. No hand-typed counts anywhere.

## Navigation and placement (UX-R5 to UX-R6)

**UX-R5. One entry point in the main site, none in the top bar.** Add "Learn (students and teachers)" as the first item of the Tools dropdown and to the footer's Research column. This touches `footerLinks.tools` in `nav.ts`, which feeds the desktop and mobile menus. After one term of use, consider promoting it to `mainNav`; do not do it now.

**UX-R6. Learn has its own chrome.**
- Extend the `ChromeGate` pattern. Under `/learn/**` the global Navbar and Footer are hidden, and `app/learn/layout.tsx` renders a LearnBar and LearnFooter.
- LearnBar: logo, "Learn home", "Educators", and "Grown-ups: main site" at the right. It is not sticky, to save classroom screen height.
- LearnFooter: licence, safety link, and no newsletter, no Contact Sales and no search. The global search leads to entity pages that name real harms, so the Learn chrome omits it.

## Visual approach (UX-R7 to UX-R8)

**Position: dark suits Analysts and the main site. It does not suit Explorers or Investigators. Use a scoped "paper" surface for Explorers, Investigators and Educators.**

Reasons, with their consequences:
1. Sustained reading at 8-14 and on dim, washed-out school Chromebook panels favours dark text on a light surface.
2. Printing is core to educators, and print is already forced to light.
3. A visible shift signals "this is the gentle version, not the institutional site", which supports the separation rule.

**UX-R7. Implement it as scoped CSS, not a theme change.**
- Add a `.learn-paper` block to `globals.css`, following the `.pilot-*` precedent. Use custom properties such as `--lp-bg` (warm off-white), `--lp-ink` (the existing #0b1220), `--lp-accent` and `--lp-line`.
- Do not touch `@theme` and add no new theme tokens. The site stays dark-only everywhere else.
- Keep the `:focus-visible` ring but override to a dark outline inside paper. #93c5fd on a light surface fails 3:1.
- Ink on paper must reach 7:1 or better for body text.
- Analysts use the existing dark theme inside LearnBar chrome.

**UX-R8. Build `components/learn/` primitives; reuse only `Container`.** `Button`, `Panel`, `Pill`, `Eyebrow`, `Callout` and `Band` are dark-only, and `Button` tracks clicks. Learn needs `LearnButton` (plain `Link`, no `trackAs`), `LearnPanel`, `LearnCallout` and `LearnBandChip`. The chip uses a shape plus a text label, with colour as decoration only. The existing band hexes (#fcd34d and similar) fail contrast on paper, so do not reuse them for text.

Type: system font stack (no web fonts, which school filters may block). Explorer body is 20px with a line height of 1.6, left-aligned, never justified, and 60 characters maximum per line. Sentences run 15 words or fewer, with a heading at least every 120 words. Investigators use 18px.

## Page templates (UX-R9 to UX-R11)

Design at 360, 768 and 1024px, in portrait and landscape. All touch targets are at least 44px (WCAG 2.2 requires 24px). Content must reflow at 320px and at 200% zoom.

**UX-R9. Lesson (topic) page.**

```
+------------------------------------------------------+
| LearnBar: [logo] Learn  Educators      Grown-ups ->  |
+------------------------------------------------------+
| Explorers > Step 2 of 5                              |
| H1  What is a score?              ~6 min read        |
| You will learn:  - ...  - ...  - ...                 |
|------------------------------------------------------|
| Short chunk (<=120 words) + simple picture w/ alt    |
| H2 chunk ...                                         |
| [ TRY IT: Build a score  ->  ]  (links to experiment)|
| Words to know  [tap to open: score][tap: evidence]   |
| Check yourself (self-marked, nothing recorded)       |
|   Q1 ...  (o) a (o) b  [Show answer]                 |
| Nothing you do here is saved or sent anywhere.       |
|------------------------------------------------------|
| < Step 1        Step 3 >        [Print this page]    |
| For the grown-up (collapsed): full version -> /methodology
+------------------------------------------------------+
```

- "Step N of 5" is static text. There is no progress tracking (UX-R12).
- Glossary terms open on tap in the page. Do not use the Radix hover `DefinedTerm` tooltip for these bands; hover does not exist on iPad.
- The "full version" link satisfies the honesty rule that every simplified page links to the full one.

**UX-R10. Experiment page.**

```
+------------------------------------------------------+
| Explorers > Experiments        [Start over]  [Print] |
| H1  Build a score          Needs: nothing / paper    |
| What to do (3 steps, plain text, visible without JS) |
|------------------------------------------------------|
| CONTROLS (left, 60%)         | RESULT (right, sticky)|
|  Care      [---o-----] 3 of 5|  Your score: 2.8      |
|  Fairness  [-o-------] 2 of 5|  Band: Developing [shape]
|  ...                         |  [Why did I get this?]|
|------------------------------------------------------|
| Empty: "Move a slider to see a score."               |
| Explain panel (opens below result, focus moves to it)|
+------------------------------------------------------+
   Portrait / phone: single column; the RESULT dock is a
   bar fixed to the bottom, with env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
```

Controls and result sit side by side only at 900px or wider. Below that, a bottom-docked result bar keeps the score visible while the child drags a slider.

**UX-R11. Educator pages.**

```
Hub:  H1 For teachers and parents
      [Plans by band: Explorers | Investigators | Analysts]
      [Safety, privacy and licence]   [Use it offline: print kit]

Plan (teacher copy):
 H1 Plan title    Band | 40 min | Prep 5 min | Needs: paper / 1 device per pair
 Learning goals (3)       Duty-of-care note (what not to say; skip options)
 Timed steps: 0-5 Hook | 5-20 Experiment (link) | 20-35 Discuss | 35-40 Wrap
 Discussion questions     Answer key (page break before)
 Licence + source line    [Print teacher copy] [Open student sheet]
```

Print rules: light and black on white, no fixed page size (`@page` must not hard-code A4, since US schools print Letter), print the hrefs of external links (as the pilot print block does), and `break-inside: avoid` on steps. The student sheet is its own route, so there is nothing to hide with CSS.

## Interaction patterns for experiments (UX-R12 to UX-R13)

**UX-R12. Global rules for every experiment.**
- **State lives in memory only.** There is no `localStorage`, no URL state and no cookie. Shared school devices would leak one child's work to the next. Say so on the page, and say that a reload starts fresh.
- **No `confirm()` dialogs.** A reset is free because nothing is saved.
- **Server-rendered fallback.** Instructions and the printable worksheet are in the HTML before hydration. With JS off, show a `<noscript>` note pointing to the worksheet.
- **Reduced motion.** With `prefers-reduced-motion`, results update instantly and nothing animates.
- **Live announcements.** Use one polite live region. Announce band changes and results, not every slider tick.
- **Scoring.** Results come from `site/src/lib/scoring.ts`. The explain text is derived from the function's own output, never from a second copy of the arithmetic.

**UX-R13. Per-pattern behaviour.**

| Pattern | Touch | Keyboard | Empty / reset / explain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Slider** (build a score) | Native `<input type="range">` with a 44px thumb, plus visible - / + step buttons for imprecise fingers | Arrows, Home, End, PageUp/PageDown from the native control; `aria-valuetext` reads "3 of 5, Developing" | Empty: result hidden, prompt "Move a slider to see a score." Sliders start at an unmarked neutral and the first interaction reveals the result. Reset restores defaults and returns focus to the first control. |
| **Drag-to-sort** (bands, evidence tiers) | Do not use HTML5 drag-and-drop; it does not work on iPad. Primary method is tap an item, then tap a bin. Pointer-event drag is an enhancement only. | Tab to an item, Enter or Space to pick up, arrows to move between bins, Enter to drop, Esc to cancel; each step announced ("Picked up 'library sign'. Bin 2 of 3.") | Empty: all items in a tray. "Check my answers" is an explicit button. A wrong item gets a neutral "try again" mark and a hint, never a red X. After two tries, "Show me why". Reset returns items to the tray. |
| **Reveal** (seed or measurement, predict then show) | A real `<button aria-expanded>`; never hover | Enter and Space; focus stays on the button and the revealed content follows it in DOM order | A prediction step is offered but never blocks: "Show me" always works. The reveal must not shift the button under the child's finger. |
| **Dice / uncertainty** | "Roll once" and "Roll 10 times" buttons | Both are ordinary buttons | Empty: "No rolls yet." Reset clears the tally. |

**"Explain my result" is on every experiment.**
- The button opens an explanation built from the child's own numbers.
- Explorers get one sentence and one picture.
- Investigators get a step list.
- Analysts get the full arithmetic and a link to the formula source.
- Near a band edge, add "You're 0.1 below the next band" to teach thresholds.

## Separation (UX-R14 to UX-R16)

**UX-R14. A testable allowlist.** Add a build test (`test:learn-links`, in the style of `test:no-stale-counts`) that fails if rendered Learn HTML contains a disallowed href.
- Explorers may link to `/learn/**`, `/methodology` and `/learn/grown-ups` only.
- Investigators add the public corrections page, if one is chosen (see Open questions).
- Neither may link to the Agent Hub, `/ai-models`, `/updates`, `/indexes`, any entity page, `/contact*`, `/services`, comments or any user-generated content.
- Analysts and educators may link to the full site.

**UX-R15. Adults reach the full site through an interstitial.** "Grown-ups: main site" in LearnBar goes to `/learn/grown-ups`. That page states, in plain words, that the main site covers hard topics such as war and detention and is written for adults, and offers "Continue to the main site". It is a speed bump, not an age gate, and it must say so. We do not verify age and must not claim to.

**UX-R16. Analytics: off on all of `/learn` in v1.** One rule is simpler to explain and to test than per-band exceptions: "No tracking on Learn." Moving the Umami script out of the root `<head>` into a route-aware client component is the required change. Use aggregate web-server counts for adoption. Verify that `trackEvent` is a no-op when the script is absent.

## Duty of care in the flow

Real entities and real harms appear only from Investigators upward. Each case study opens with a content note, a "Choose a gentler example" link and a click-to-reveal body, not an auto-shown paragraph. Review `site/src/components/model-benchmark/dutyOfCare.ts` for reusable wording.

## Top 3

1. **UX-R6 and UX-R16:** a dedicated Learn chrome, using the `ChromeGate` pattern, with no newsletter, no search, no tracking and no sticky bar. It removes the largest privacy and separation risks at once.
2. **UX-R7:** a scoped `.learn-paper` surface for Explorers, Investigators and Educators. Dark stays for the rest of the site.
3. **UX-R12 and UX-R13:** experiments that work with touch and keyboard alike, hold no persistent state, and carry an "Explain my result" panel built from the real scoring function.

## What I would refuse to build

- Accounts, saved progress, certificates, streaks, badges or leaderboards. They require identity or storage and invite gaming.
- An age gate or birth-date question. It would collect data and give false assurance.
- Any text input from a child, including "name your town" and free-text answers.
- Hover-only tooltips, HTML5-drag-only sorting, or `window.confirm` resets.
- Showing a child's result next to a real entity's score, or implying a score can be raised by learning (the education firewall).
- A Researchers hub that duplicates `/methodology`.

## Open questions (default in brackets)

1. Should Learn go in `mainNav` or only the Tools dropdown? [Tools dropdown plus footer; revisit after one term.]
2. Is there a public page for `CHANGELOG.md` corrections for Investigators to link to? [Create a read-only `/learn/investigators/corrections` summary page instead of linking to an external one.]
3. Is Umami cookieless and acceptable on any Learn page? [Off everywhere in v1; confirm before enabling on Educators only.]
4. Paper surface for Analysts too? [No; they keep the dark theme, with print-to-light.]
5. Content licence for lesson plans? [CC BY 4.0, subject to checking `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md` against the nonprofit document.]
6. Should the student sheet have an answers-free QR code to the experiment? [No; a printed URL is enough and avoids a generator dependency.]
