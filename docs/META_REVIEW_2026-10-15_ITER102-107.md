## 3. Selection discipline: is the ranked queue still steering?

Partly, and mostly by accident:
- The research cycle in 104 won on the formula.
- Iteration 104's V4 created BRF-MOVE (v2 18), and Iteration 106 shipped it.
- But in 107 the top eligible item, BRF-MOVE-2 (v2 17, live P+2), lost to TIER-1014 (v2 14) because a founder
  decision named it.
- In this window, a founder directive decided 4 of the 6 selections (103, 105, 106 and 107).

The pattern from Meta-review 11 §2 has returned. Directives arrive in bundles of 3–4 workstreams (105, 106), and each
bundle adds a decision packet: 6 code-blocking items (10-14 packet), 10 decisions (10-15 AI-models packet) and 5 asks
(MT-V2 §5). D-13 and D-43 to D-46 are still `proposed` (DECISIONS index).

The directives are legitimate, and they are the founder's to give. The failure is that nothing limits how many
founder-lane items can be open at once. So directives pile up as uncommitted work and as unanswered packets, while the
ranked queue's live defects (BRF-MOVE-2, LRN-FIX-2) wait.

**Proposed coexistence rule (R2):**
- **Two lanes.** Founder-directed work runs in its own lane, with a WIP limit of **one** uncommitted founder iteration.
- **One ranked loop in three.** At least one loop in every three takes the top eligible v2 item, unless the founder
  explicitly overrides that specific slot.
- **Forced items stay forced.** S3, S7 and S10 still pre-empt both lanes.
- **The next forced date is near.** S7 forces R-1 on **2026-11-09**, and builds fail from **2026-11-17** unless D-13
  is decided (backlog line 34; DECISIONS preamble).

The root causes are founder decisions, host memory, and judging that is done by hand.

**Does MODEL_TESTING_V2_PROPOSAL address them?** Mostly yes. §1 R1–R5 names exactly these causes:
- manual subagent judging;
- Claude-only judges;
- a 16 GB host;
- a protocol pace of 2 builds per wave;
- five or more approvals per wave.

The hybrid design cuts the per-wave touches to one. Three problems remain:

- **It adds asks rather than replacing them.** The same-day packet and the proposal give **conflicting defaults**:

  | Question | Packet | Proposal |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pace / protocol | M-FD-4: "Keep v1 at 2 per week" | ask 2: ratify `weekly-v2`, all eligible builds |
  | Hosted access | M-FD-7: "(iii) now", Ollama signin | ask 1: OpenRouter key |
  | Dashboard | M-FD-9: "Hold" D-29b | ask 4: ratify D-29b |

  A founder who accepts both sets of defaults has accepted a contradiction.
- **Local collection still needs about 6.6 GiB free.** Hosted routing helps only frontier models.
- **No agent work has started.** Item 1 (MT-V2-1, v1 14) needs no founder decision. It measures the judge-token cost
  that every price estimate depends on, and the proposal itself marks those estimates as unmeasured.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **R1: Ship the stack as one unit.**
   - **Owner:** the coordinator commits 107 and this review and runs the checks; the founder approves push and deploy.
   - **Steps:** commit 107 and this review; run `npm run prepush:clean` on the tip; then ask for one push and deploy
     of 104–107 together.
   - **Done when:** production `build-manifest.json` `git.sha` equals the tip, `/updates/2026-10-01` no longer
     contains "largest move Valencia", and `/updates/2026-10-14` returns 200.
2. **R2: A founder lane with a WIP limit.**
   - **Owner:** the coordinator.
   - **Rule:** S6 is amended to allow at most 1 uncommitted founder-directed iteration, and to require 1 ranked loop
     in every 3, as in §3.
   - **Gate:** a check reads each log entry's "Selected" line and fails on 3 consecutive founder-directed entries
     without a recorded override.
   - **Done when:** the rule is in the backlog header, and a planted three-entry log fails the check.
3. **R3: No research cycle is "validated" without the chain.**
   - **Owner:** the coordinator, plus the overnight-digest brief.
   - **Rule:** the research-cycle V2 list must include `npm test`, or the `--steps` slices that cover
     `test:source-tiers`.
   - **Done when:** the next cycle's log entry records the chain result, and a planted tier contradiction in a fixture
     briefing fails the listed steps.
4. **R4: Keep the registry honest.**
   - **Owner:** the coordinator, plus the founder for the hook.
   - **Steps:** add Iteration 106's DC-23 occurrence and fix the Count cell; append change-log lines for 10-14 and
     10-15; extend `test:health-freshness` (or a sibling) so the Count column must equal the numbered occurrences in
     each row; put HOOK-DC23 to the founder or give it a dated waiver.
   - **Done when:** a planted count mismatch fails the check, and HOOK-DC23 has a decision.
5. **R5: One model-testing decision sheet, and start the agent-doable work now.**
   - **Owner:** the coordinator (the sheet) and the backend-engineer (MT-V2-1).
   - **Steps:** replace the two documents with one sheet that has one default per question and resolves the three
     conflicts in §5; build MT-V2-1 on pilot-3 data.
   - **Done when:** the sheet has no conflicting defaults; MT-V2-1 meets the proposal's §6 item 1 acceptance
     criteria, with one planted defect per gate; and real judge-token counts replace the estimates.
6. **R6: Keep the cadence (target 3, gate 5), and make the deviation trigger blocking.**
   - **Owner:** the coordinator.
   - **Why keep it:** the cadence gate fired correctly this time. The breach came from the deviation trigger, which is
     prose that was deferred twice.
   - **Done when:** the R2 check runs before selection, and the next meta-review covers Iteration 108 to no later than
     113.

**Bottom line.** Verification held up: a false count, a publication leak and an over-broad gate were all caught. But
the loop finished work faster than it shipped it, and took directives faster than the packets were answered. The fix is
a WIP limit on the founder lane and one deploy of what is already done.
